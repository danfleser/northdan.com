What clients ask before they start

How much does a web app or custom software cost?

It depends on the number of screens, the integrations and the data volume. After the first call you get a written quote with a clear price and timeline, with no obligation. Our pricing guides give general market ranges.

How long does a project take?

A brochure website usually takes a few weeks; a complex application, a few months. We agree the timeline in the quote, and you follow it on the intermediate demos.

Who owns the source code?

You do, fully. On delivery we hand over the source code, repository access and documentation. You never depend on us to work with another vendor later.

Do you work with clients outside Romania?

Yes. Our clients come from more than 10 countries. We work remotely, in English, with written quotes and regular demos.

What happens after delivery?

You get a 3-month warranty on what we delivered. After that we offer maintenance contracts for updates, monitoring and further development.

Do you build software for EU-funded projects?

Yes — we build the project’s software component and the technical documentation the funding application needs. We are not funding consultants: we are the software vendor.
