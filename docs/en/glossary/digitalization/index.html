Digitalization: what it is and what it is not

Moving a company's processes — orders, documents, approvals, records — off paper, out of phone calls and out of individual memory, into systems that hold them.

6
sections
6
questions answered

Digitalization is the use of digital technology to change how a company works: the order, the approval, the invoice and the archive move off paper, phone calls and memory into systems that store and process them; scanning a document without changing the process is digitization. Eurostat measured in 2025 that 44.3% of Romanian companies with 10–249 employees reach at least a basic level of digital intensity, against 71.4% across the EU-27.

Scanning an order form and emailing the file is not digitalization. Nothing about the work changed: a human still reads it, retypes it, and remembers to chase it. Digitalization is the step after that — the process itself moves into a system, so the order arrives as structured data, reserves stock, triggers an approval that is recorded rather than remembered, and produces a document nobody had to type. The distinction matters because most disappointing projects bought the scanner and skipped the process. What a mid-sized company gains is rarely headcount reduction; it is capacity and certainty. In Romania the regulatory floor has risen as well: mandatory electronic invoicing, transport declarations and standard audit files mean a company still running on spreadsheets now pays for compliance in overtime. Start with the process that hurts most, not with the software that demonstrates best.

Why it matters for your business

✓Answers in seconds, not phone calls

Order status, stock, document history and approvals are visible to whoever needs them without interrupting three colleagues.

✓More capacity from the same team

Volume grows without proportional hiring because the re-keying, chasing and reconciling that consumed the hours is gone.

✓Compliance without a month-end sprint

Structured data feeds statutory reporting directly, so regulatory deadlines stop producing weekends of manual assembly.

Digitization, digitalization, digital transformation: three steps

Digitization turns analogue information into digital form: a paper invoice scanned into a PDF. The process stays the same — a person reads it, retypes it and remembers to chase it.

Digitalization changes the process itself. The same invoice is issued directly as an XML file through e-Factura, lands in the accounts automatically and passes through nobody's hands. Digital transformation goes further: the company sells or bills differently, for example through online orders or subscriptions, because technology made a model possible that did not exist before.

Romanian dictionaries do not separate the words: DEX defines the verb digitaliza as converting analogue signals into digital ones, which is what business English calls digitization. The three-step distinction comes from English usage, not from the dictionary.

What digitalization looks like inside a company: examples by process

These examples are processes, not products; each can be done on its own and shows its effect on its own.

Order, delivery, invoicing: the order is entered once, stock is reserved, the invoice leaves from the same data. Stock: real quantities are visible live, not in a colleague's spreadsheet. Approvals: a purchase or leave request travels through a flow with a history, not through emails and paper. Archive: the 2023 contract is found in seconds by anyone entitled to it. Reporting: yesterday's figures are current, not rebuilt at month-end. Customer relations: each customer's history sits in one place, not in employees' phones.

The order of work is simple: begin with the process that hurts measurably and finish it, instead of starting ten at once.

What is already mandatory in Romania

There is no general legal duty to digitalize in Romania. There are specific electronic reports, and a company working on paper pays for them in overtime.

e-Factura is mandatory for operators established in Romania in dealings with individuals (B2C) from 1 January 2025, under Emergency Ordinance 120/2021 art. 10^1 para. (2), introduced by Emergency Ordinance 69/2024; simplified invoices under art. 319 para. (12) of the Fiscal Code are excepted. Invoices between companies and to public bodies entered the system before B2C. System messages — invoices received, ANAF validations — arrive in the company's private virtual space (SPV).

Two other electronic reporting duties are the SAF-T declaration (D406) and e-Transport for road shipments of high fiscal-risk goods inside Romania and all international road shipments of goods. The detail of each is on the e-Factura, SPV, SAF-T and e-Transport pages.

Where Romania stands: digital intensity and the EU 2030 target

Eurostat measures company digitalization through the Digital Intensity Index (DII, version 3), built from 12 criteria. In 2025, 44.3% of Romanian companies with 10–249 employees (financial sector excluded) reached at least the basic level, against 71.4% in the EU-27; in 2023 the figures were 26.8% against 57.9%. For large companies, with 250 or more employees, Romania stands at 80.7% and the EU-27 at 96.4%. Companies under 10 employees are not in this series, so the figures say nothing about micro-enterprises.

Decision (EU) 2022/2481 on the Digital Decade Policy Programme sets the target for 2030 that more than 90% of SMEs reach at least a basic level of digital intensity. The 12 criteria are explained on the DESI criteria page.

Programmes that co-finance digitalization (regional programmes, POCIDIF, SME Eco-Tech) have different calendars and statuses; they are kept up to date on the funding page for companies.

Frequently asked questions

Which process should a small company digitalize first?

Pick the one that is simultaneously high-volume, error-prone and visible to customers — usually order intake, invoicing or field reporting. It delivers a measurable result within a quarter, which buys the credibility needed for harder projects later. Starting with HR or archiving is common, comfortable, and rarely produces a number anyone remembers.

What is the difference between digitization and digitalization?

Digitization converts a document or an analogue signal into digital form, such as an invoice scanned into a PDF. Digitalization changes the process: the invoice is issued, sent and booked in systems without retyping. The first changes the format, the second changes how the work is done.

What does digital transformation mean?

Digital transformation is changing the business model with technology, not only the processes: online sales, subscriptions, new services. Digitalizing a process comes before it. A company can digitalize its invoicing without transforming its business, but it cannot transform without digitalizing.

Is digitalization mandatory for companies in Romania?

Not as a general duty. Specific electronic reports are mandatory: e-Factura, including B2C from 1 January 2025 (Emergency Ordinance 120/2021 art. 10^1), SAF-T and e-Transport (for companies shipping the goods it covers). Any other digitalization remains a business decision, justified by the time and errors it removes.

What does digitalization cost, and is funding available?

Indicatively, by project size: online presence 1,000–5,000 €, internal processes 5,000–25,000 €, integrated flows above 25,000 €. Details are on the pricing page. European funding for digitalization follows different calendars, kept up to date on the funding page for companies.

Our staff resist the change — how do we get past that?

Resistance is nearly always rational: the new system makes work visible, and the first version is slower than the habit it replaces. What works is involving the loudest sceptic in the design, removing the old path completely once the new one works, and making the first release genuinely easier for the people using it rather than for management reporting.
