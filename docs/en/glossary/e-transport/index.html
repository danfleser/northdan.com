What is RO e-Transport, Romania's road freight declaration system?

Romania's RO e-Transport system explained under Emergency Ordinance 41/2022: when to declare, who declares, how long a UIT code is valid and which fines apply in 2026.

RO e-Transport is Romania's national system through which the tax authority ANAF monitors road shipments of high fiscal-risk goods inside Romania and all international road shipments of goods: the shipment is declared in SPV before departure and receives a UIT code.

It does not cover every van. The condition in ANAF/AVR Order no. 1,337/1,268/2024 (art. 1 para. (4)) is cumulative: a vehicle with a maximum technically permissible mass of at least 2.5 tonnes, carrying goods above 500 kg gross mass or above RON 10,000 excluding VAT, for at least one consignment. A UIT code is valid for 5 calendar days; 15 days only for intra-community acquisitions and the operations in art. 2 point 9 letters g) and j) of Ordinance 41/2022.

For an undeclared shipment the fine is RON 20,000 to 100,000 for companies, and confiscation only comes with repeat offences. For a company that ships often the real issue is volume: a declaration made from the ERP or WMS through the API, when the delivery note is issued, brings the UIT straight into the document.

Checked on 18 November 2026 on legislatie.just.ro.

Why it matters for your business

Declarations from data you already hold

Integration requests the UIT from the order and delivery-note data, with no retyping into the ANAF application, shipment by shipment.

Vehicle changes before departure

If the van is replaced, the new vehicle goes into the declaration before the shipment moves, not after a roadside stop.

Confirmations and corrections tracked

Transport confirmation and goods-data corrections, allowed until the 25th of the month after the transport ends, end up in the declaration history.

When RO e-Transport applies: thresholds and exceptions

Inside Romania, shipments of high fiscal-risk goods are declared, meaning the products in the annex to ANAF President's Order no. 802/2022, as amended. Examples in the original annex include vegetables, fruit, beverages, salt and cement, clothing, footwear, iron and steel. International shipments (import, export, intra-community acquisitions and deliveries, transit) are declared for any goods, under art. 1 para. (2) of the procedure approved by order.

The thresholds apply to the vehicle and the load together: a maximum technically permissible mass of at least 2.5 tonnes, plus over 500 kg gross mass (packaging included) or over RON 10,000 excluding VAT. Foreign currency is converted at the National Bank of Romania rate of the declaration day. If one consignment holds both high-risk and ordinary goods, all of its goods are declared. Transfers between your own warehouses or shops are included. For multimodal transport only the road leg is declared.

Exceptions are set in art. 16 of Ordinance 41/2022: diplomatic missions and NATO forces, excise goods covered by e-DA or e-DAS in the EMCS system, and postal parcels governed by Ordinance 13/2013. The procedure (arts. 13-14) also exempts farmers holding a marketing booklet and cash & carry customers transporting in their own name.

The UIT code: generation, validity and changes

UIT (Unique Identification Transport) is the unique code the system assigns to a declared shipment. The declaration is made at most 3 calendar days before the declared start date and no later than departure, border entry or the place of import. The 5 or 15-day validity runs from the declared start date; an expired UIT can no longer be used.

Before departure the data can be edited and the UIT stays the same. After departure only the vehicle can be updated (art. 8 para. (1^1)). Goods name, quantities and value can be corrected after the UIT expires, at the latest by the 25th of the month following the month the transport ended (art. 11 para. (5)). In groupage freight each consignment has its own UIT, and the UIT may be shown in any intelligible form. After the transport ends, the declarant can mark it confirmed, partly confirmed or rejected; with no choice by the UIT's expiry it counts as confirmed (procedure art. 5). If the system is down, the obligation is suspended and is fulfilled by the end of the next working day.

Who declares and what the carrier does

The obligation follows the transaction, not the vehicle (arts. 8 and 8^1 of Ordinance 41/2022). The Romanian supplier declares domestic and intra-community deliveries, the Romanian beneficiary declares intra-community acquisitions, the consignee or consignor named in the customs declaration declares imports and exports, the depositary declares transit, and for operations without transfer of ownership the service provider, beneficiary or client declares. The carrier declares only if it also holds one of those roles, according to ANAF's clarifications.

The carrier has other duties: it transmits the GPS position for the whole route (art. 8^2) and hands the UIT to the driver. The driver starts the positioning device before the national leg, stops it after delivery or exit from the country (art. 8^3), and presents the documents and the UIT on request to ANAF, AVR or the police (art. 10). A declaration can also be made through a legal representative or an authorised agent.

Fines and confiscation in 2026

Art. 13^1 of Ordinance 41/2022 sets fines of RON 10,000 to 50,000 for individuals and RON 20,000 to 100,000 for legal entities for: an undeclared shipment, use of an expired UIT, unloading in transit, quantities different from those declared, a vehicle not updated, a UIT not made available to the carrier, data changed after departure, a mixed consignment declared partially, and a carrier without GPS data. A driver risks RON 5,000 to 10,000.

Confiscation only follows repeat offences of non-declaration or different quantities: 15% of the value of the undeclared goods for a second offence within 12 months, 50% for a third, 100% from the fourth. On checks after the transport, goods found in the documents and the books are not confiscated. GPS-related fines were suspended by Ordinance 29/2025 until 31 December 2025; the latest consolidation of Ordinance 41/2022 on legislatie.just.ro shows no further suspension, so they apply from 1 January 2026.

e-Transport vs e-Factura, and declaring by app or API

e-Factura (Ordinance 120/2021) concerns the invoice; e-Transport concerns the physical movement of goods by road. Both go through SPV, but a shipment can need a UIT before any invoice exists, for example with a delivery note or a transfer between your own warehouses.

The procedure provides two channels: the Ministry of Finance's free application in SPV, or API microservices from your own software, using electronic identification in SPV. Through the API the declaration is an XML file; the system runs automatic structure and content checks and returns a zip signed by the Ministry of Finance with the UIT, or an XML listing the errors. An EDI message received from a retailer does not replace the declaration.

Frequently asked questions

What does e-Transport mean?⌄

RO e-Transport is the national system created by Emergency Ordinance 41/2022, in which road shipments of high fiscal-risk goods inside Romania and all international road shipments of goods are declared in SPV before departure and receive a UIT code that accompanies the goods for the whole journey.

What is the UIT code and how long is it valid?⌄

The UIT code is the unique identifier of a shipment declared in e-Transport. It is obtained at most 3 calendar days before the declared departure date and is valid for 5 calendar days from that date, or 15 days for intra-community acquisitions and the operations in art. 2 point 9 letters g) and j).

Who declares in e-Transport: the supplier, the beneficiary or the carrier?⌄

The party named by law declares: the supplier for deliveries, the beneficiary for intra-community acquisitions, the importer or exporter at customs, the depositary for transit. The carrier declares only if it also holds one of those roles; otherwise it transmits the GPS position and receives the UIT for the driver.

Must a shipment in a van under 2.5 tonnes be declared in e-Transport?⌄

No, if the vehicle's maximum technically permissible mass is below 2.5 tonnes, the shipment falls outside the condition in ANAF/AVR Order no. 1,337/1,268/2024, art. 1 para. (4). What counts is the permissible mass on the registration document, not the load. A heavier vehicle must also carry over 500 kg or over RON 10,000 excluding VAT.

What is the fine for an undeclared shipment in e-Transport?⌄

Legal entities face RON 20,000 to 100,000 and individuals RON 10,000 to 50,000 (art. 13^1 of Ordinance 41/2022). Confiscation of the undeclared goods' value only follows repeat offences: 15% for a second offence within 12 months, 50% for a third and 100% from the fourth.

How is a UIT code generated: from SPV or through the API?⌄

The UIT comes from the Ministry of Finance's free application in SPV, or through the API from your own software (ERP, WMS), using electronic identification in SPV. Through the API you send an XML file and receive a signed zip with the UIT, or an XML listing the errors to correct.
