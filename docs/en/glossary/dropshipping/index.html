What Is Dropshipping (Direct Supplier Delivery)?

An online retail model with no stock of your own: you sell and collect payment, the supplier ships straight to the customer — margins and risks included.

6
sections
6
questions answered

Dropshipping (from "drop ship", to ship directly) is the online retail model in which a store sells a product it does not hold in stock and the supplier ships it straight to the customer once the store has paid the wholesale price. The store keeps the difference between its selling price and the supplier's price and handles the website, marketing, payment and the customer; the supplier holds the stock and ships. The entry barrier really is low and the model is legitimate, including as a way to test demand before committing money to inventory. The ads promising "passive income" skip the rest: margins are thin and eroded by paid advertising (traffic never arrives on its own), you control neither quality nor delivery times — yet the complaints, returns and reviews are entirely yours — and with goods shipped from Asia, weeks-long deliveries and customs charges land squarely in your relationship with the customer. Toward a Romanian consumer you are the seller: the 14-day withdrawal right comes from OUG 34/2014 (art. 9), and the two-year legal guarantee of conformity from OUG 140/2021 (art. 9). The version with a real shot at a durable business: local or European suppliers, automated stock-and-order integration with the store, a niche with decent margin — and the expectations of a trade, not of a lottery ticket.

Why it matters for your business

✓Entering e-commerce without locked-up capital

With no money tied up in stock or a warehouse, you can validate a niche cheaply — the least expensive serious commercial experiment there is.

✓A wider catalog without dead-inventory risk

Existing stores can add whole categories on a dropshipping basis — testing real demand before purchasing any stock.

✓Run from anywhere, scale elastically

With no logistics of your own, growing volume needs no bigger warehouse — only more orders flowing automatically to suppliers.

How dropshipping works, step by step

1. The store agrees terms with the supplier and imports the supplier's catalog through a product feed, with prices and stock.

2. The customer orders on the site and pays the store's price.

3. The store passes the order to the supplier, automatically through an API or by hand, and pays the supplier's purchase price.

4. The supplier packs the product, usually in a plain parcel or one carrying the store's name, and issues the tracking number (AWB).

5. The tracking number and parcel status reach the customer through the store.

6. Returns and complaints come to the store, not the supplier. The store invoices the customer in its own name, and the supplier invoices the store.

Dropshipping, marketplace and fulfillment: the difference

Dropshipping answers who holds the stock and who ships: the supplier. A marketplace is the platform where many merchants sell, and a merchant can sell there through dropshipping only within that platform's own seller rules.

Fulfillment means the goods are yours, stored and shipped by a logistics operator. In short: dropshipping needs no capital in stock but gives you no control over delivery times; fulfillment needs purchased stock but you control the product.

Selling to consumers in Romania: the rules, article by article

OUG 34/2014 transposes Directive 2011/83/EU and OUG 140/2021 transposes Directive (EU) 2019/771. The articles below are the Romanian ones and apply to sales to consumers in Romania.

Withdrawal: a consumer may withdraw from a distance contract within 14 days without giving a reason (OUG 34/2014, art. 9 para. 1). For goods, the 14 days run from receipt by the consumer or by a person they name other than the carrier (art. 9 para. 2 let. b). If you failed to inform them of this right, the period extends by 12 months (art. 10 para. 1).

Refund: all sums received, including standard delivery, are returned within 14 days of learning of the withdrawal (art. 13 para. 1 and 3). The direct cost of the return falls on the customer only if you told them beforehand (art. 14 para. 2). Since 19 June 2026 online stores must also offer a withdrawal function labelled "retrageți-vă din contract aici", visible throughout the withdrawal period (art. 11^1).

Delivery: unless you agreed otherwise, the order must be delivered without undue delay and at most 30 days after the contract is made (art. 18 para. 1), wherever the supplier is located. If you miss the additional term the customer sets, they may rescind the contract (art. 18 para. 2). The risk of loss or damage passes to the customer only on receipt (art. 20), so a lost parcel is your loss.

Guarantee: the seller is liable for any non-conformity existing at delivery and appearing within 2 years (OUG 140/2021, art. 9 para. 1). A defect found within the first year is presumed to have existed at delivery (art. 10 para. 1). Repair or replacement is free of charge, within a reasonable term of at most 15 calendar days (art. 12 para. 1 let. a and b).

Checked on legislatie.just.ro on 18 November 2026.

Integrating the store with the supplier

A serious dropshipping store synchronizes stock from the supplier's feed: how often the feed updates decides how many orders you sell without goods. Orders flow to the supplier automatically, through an API or a file, with no manual copying.

The tracking number and status flow back to the customer, and returns are routed to the right address. Without those links, orders get copied by hand at night and customers wait for sold-out products.

Frequently asked questions

Is dropshipping legal in Romania?

Yes, it is ordinary commerce, with a registered company, accounting and normal obligations. Toward the customer, the seller is you, not the supplier: the 14-day withdrawal right (OUG 34/2014, art. 9) and the two-year conformity guarantee (OUG 140/2021, art. 9) are your obligations, wherever the parcel started.

Can you still make money with dropshipping, or is the market saturated?

The generic playbook — AliExpress products pushed with Facebook ads — is indeed crowded, its margins consumed by advertising costs. What works: niches backed by real expertise, nearby suppliers (delivery in days, not weeks) and margins that survive the cost of acquiring customers. It is commerce with work attached, not passive income.

What technical integration does a serious dropshipping store need?

Automatic synchronization with the supplier: current stock levels so you never sell what no longer exists, orders transmitted without manual copying, and tracking numbers and statuses returned to the customer. Without them, "hassle-free" turns into copying orders at midnight.

Where does the word dropshipping come from?

It comes from the English "drop ship", to ship directly. Romanian has no established equivalent; it is rendered as direct delivery from the supplier or selling without own stock: the store sells, and the supplier ships the parcel straight to the customer.

Who is responsible if the product does not arrive or is defective?

The store. The risk of loss passes to the customer only when the parcel is received (OUG 34/2014, art. 20), and non-conformity is covered for 2 years (OUG 140/2021, art. 9). For a non-conforming product you can claim redress from the supplier (OUG 140/2021, art. 16); for a lost parcel, recovery depends on your contracts with the supplier and carrier.

How fast must a dropshipping order be delivered?

Within 30 days of the contract being made, unless you agreed another term (OUG 34/2014, art. 18 para. 1). If you miss the additional term the customer sets, they may rescind the contract. The delivery time shown on the site must be the supplier's real one.
