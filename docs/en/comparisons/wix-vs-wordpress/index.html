Wix or WordPress: renting or owning

On Wix you rent your website; on WordPress you own it — with everything each option implies.

5
sections
5
questions answered

Choose Wix when you want a brochure site online over a weekend and do not want to deal with hosting or updates; choose WordPress when the company site must grow for years, be movable to another provider and give you control over SEO. The difference that is not visible at the start: Wix states in its Help Center that a Wix site runs only on Wix's servers — the content stays yours, products and contacts export as CSV, but the pages and design are rebuilt from scratch if you leave. In every other respect Wix does its job honestly: a visual editor where you drag elements with the mouse, hosting and security included, from $17 a month (Light plan, billed yearly, excluding VAT, the price shown on wix.com/plans on 18.11.2026). Its limits are an SEO structure you do not control and features available only inside the Wix App Market. WordPress is the philosophical opposite: the software is free, you pay only for your own hosting at 100–400 lei a year, and the site is portable — you archive it and move it to any provider at any time. The price of that freedom is that updates and security become your responsibility, or that of somebody paid for it. The simple rule: testing an idea or wanting a minimal presence, Wix; building a long-term digital asset for the company, WordPress or a custom site. We do not build on Wix; we build WordPress and custom sites — which is why we say openly where Wix is the right choice.

Key takeaways

✓Where Wix wins

From nothing to a published site over a weekend, without touching code or hosting. For a small practice, a sole trader or an idea worth testing, a Light subscription at $17 a month (billed yearly, excluding VAT, Wix's price on 18.11.2026) buys exactly the right thing: an online presence with no headaches.

✓Where WordPress wins

Real ownership: the files and the database are yours, movable to any provider. Over 79,000 free extensions in the wordpress.org directory (18.11.2026), an SEO structure controllable down to the last detail, and no ceiling imposed by the platform.

✓The long-run difference

Three years of Wix Light to Business is $612–1,404 excluding VAT at today's price. The content stays yours, but the site itself does not move. WordPress costs less only if someone in the company does the upkeep, and more if you pay for maintenance (an indicative €50–150 a month) — what you buy is a site you can move.

Where the balance tips

Technical effort: Wix wins outright — there are no updates, no broken plugins, no site compromised through neglect. SEO: WordPress wins for sites competing on hard keywords, because it gives you control over speed, URL structure, structured data and every technical detail Wix decides on your behalf. Costs: WordPress is no saving if you pay someone to maintain it; you pay for ownership and control, not for a smaller bill.

The decisive criterion remains portability. Wix says in its Help Center that the site must be hosted and operated on Wix's servers; the content stays yours, physical products and contacts export as CSV, and you can keep the domain with another registrar. The pages, the design and the structure do not leave, so they are rebuilt on the new platform. Companies that grew on Wix and then wanted a serious site paid for a complete rebuild, plus the delicate transition period in search. If there is any chance the site becomes an important customer channel within 3–5 years, choose a foundation you can leave from the start.

What each costs over three years (prices on 18.11.2026)

Wix, according to wix.com/plans (seen from Romania, 18.11.2026): prices in dollars, per month, billed yearly upfront and excluding taxes — Light $17, Core $29, Business $39. Light does not accept online payments; Core and Business do. The domain is free only for the first year, and the displayed offer applies to the initial term, so renewal may be priced differently. If the price does not rise, 36 months comes to $612 (Light), $1,044 (Core) or $1,404 (Business), excluding VAT.

WordPress: the software is free; shared hosting costs 100–400 lei a year and a .ro domain 40–70 lei a year. You do the upkeep in 2–3 hours a month or pay for it, an indicative €50–150 a month. With a maintenance contract, WordPress costs more than Wix. One more detail matters for a Romanian company: Wix Payments is not available for businesses in Romania (the country list on support.wix.com, 18.11.2026), so selling online on Wix goes through a third-party processor. For scale, W3Techs shows WordPress on 38.5% of all websites and Wix on 4.2% (18.11.2026).

Where you need no agency at all — ours included

For a brochure site of four or five pages with no special requirements, you need not pay anyone: a Wix subscription, or WordPress with a free theme installed in one evening following a tutorial, covers the need completely. Any agency asking €1,500 for that is selling you work the platforms already do by themselves.

An agency starts to be worth the money only when requirements appear that platforms do not solve out of the box: a design distinct from competitors, real SEO work in a competitive market, integrations with booking or invoicing systems, or a site that must load in under a second for paid campaigns. Until then, save the money and put it into good content — content moves the needle more than the platform does.

Frequently asked questions

Is it really impossible to move a Wix site to another provider?

Not as a site: Wix says a Wix site runs only on Wix's servers. The content stays yours — products and contacts export as CSV, texts and images you copy — and you can keep the domain with another registrar. Pages, design and structure are rebuilt on the new platform, with 301 redirects.

Is it true that Wix is weak at SEO?

For moderate local competition, Wix is sufficient — its pages index normally. The ceiling shows in competitive markets: you cannot finely control loading speed, the generated code is bulky, and some advanced technical optimisations are simply not accessible.

Can I manage WordPress without technical knowledge?

For publishing content, yes: the current editor is comparable to Wix in ease of use. The difference appears in upkeep — theme and extension updates, backups, security. Either you set aside 2–3 hours a month to learn, or you pay an indicative €50–150 a month to somebody who does it for you.

Is there a third option — neither Wix nor WordPress?

Yes: a static or custom site, written on modern code with no platform underneath. It makes sense when speed and security are critical or when the site is part of a bigger product. A custom site costs an indicative €1,500–5,000 and needs developers for any change — not for people who edit frequently themselves.

Can I sell online on Wix as a company in Romania?

Yes, but not through Wix Payments: on 18.11.2026 Wix's payment service is not available to businesses in Romania, so you connect a third-party processor. You need the Core plan ($29 a month, billed yearly, excluding VAT) or higher; Light does not accept payments. For a shop with e-Factura and couriers, compare Shopify and WooCommerce first.
