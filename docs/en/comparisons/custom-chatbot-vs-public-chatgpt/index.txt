A public subscription to a general-purpose assistant is a personal tool: every colleague drafts faster, summarises a document, translates, writes an email or cleans up a spreadsheet. It knows only what you upload into a conversation or a project, and it does not guarantee that it answers only from that; it cannot be put on your website in front of customers; and the company receives conversation logs through a compliance interface only on the Enterprise plan.
