Start-Up Nation session 2: from the free course to company enrolment and reimbursement

Enrolment in the free course of session 2 is open until 27 November 2026, 20:00 — the original 30-day deadline has already been extended. After the course, graduates' companies enrol for funding in a 45-working-day window whose date MEDAT has not yet announced.

Session 2 of the fourth edition of Start-Up Nation opened 17,600 places on 6 October 2026 in the free 40-hour entrepreneurship course for young people aged 18 to under 30, with enrolment on minimis.imm.gov.ro until 27 November 2026, 20:00; companies founded by graduates can then apply for up to RON 250,000, at most 90% of eligible costs. Three documents decide what is true about each stage, and none of them is a list of programmes: the implementation procedure, the ministerial order approving it and the electronic enrolment application. What is open now: course enrolment, extended by the MEDAT announcement of 4 November 2026, when more than 11,400 places were still free. What comes next: company enrolment, for which the announcements give no date yet. What is firm: expenditure is made and the reimbursement request filed within 12 months of the contract taking effect, but no later than 8 December 2027 — for session 2 the real window may be shorter. What has already closed: company enrolment for the first session, in May 2026. Verified on 18 November 2026 against the MEDAT announcements of 30 September, 4 November and 13 November 2026 and the consolidated procedure: the course deadline, places, categories, ceiling and the 90% rate. Northdan Soft prepares the digital chapter of the business plan — specification, dated technical offer, cost breakdown — with no promises of score or approval.

Who can enrol in session 2 and where places remain

The course has four categories of places, all for people aged 18 to under 30: 950 for Bucharest-Ilfov, 300 for Roma from Bucharest-Ilfov, 13,500 for the less developed regions and 2,850 for Roma from those regions, 17,600 in total. The education requirement is ISCED level 2, and labour-market status does not matter; young people returning to Romania are accepted. The course is 40 hours, online or in person, and ends with a certificate accredited by MMFTSS.

If you were declared eligible in the first session and did not graduate, you re-enrol by updating your initial application, not by filing a new one. The over-30 category of first-session funding does not appear among the session 2 course categories.

Free places are not evenly spread. On 4 November 2026 at 12:00, more than 8,300 places were free in the less developed regions, more than 2,700 for Roma from those regions, more than 60 in Bucharest-Ilfov and about 260 for Roma from Bucharest-Ilfov. Anyone domiciled in Bucharest-Ilfov therefore has the fewest places left.

The session 2 calendar, what is fixed and what is not

The dates below come from MEDAT announcements and the consolidated procedure.

6 October 2026, 10:00 — course enrolment opened, for 30 days. 4 November 2026 — the deadline was extended to 27 November 2026, 20:00. MEDAT also announced four free webinars, 1,000 places each, 10:00 to 11:00: on 17 and 24 November about enrolment, on 19 and 26 November about accessing funding (announcement of 13 November 2026).

The company enrolment date is not announced. The procedure (point 6.3.1, para. 7, as amended by Order 2,338/2025) provides a 45-working-day window, from 10:00 on the first day to 20:00 on the last; it can be extended, but no later than 7 working days after the last course ends, and the start date is published beforehand on the ministry website (para. 6). RUE numbers and lists follow within 3 working days of the window closing (para. 13), and the selection threshold is a minimum of 50 points out of 100 (para. 14).

After the contract, expenditure is made and the reimbursement request filed within 12 months of it taking effect, but no later than 8 December 2027 (point 6.3.2, para. 22, as amended by Order 922/2026). Whoever enters now has less time: a contract signed in mid-2027 leaves, by our reading of the text in force, about 6 months until 8 December 2027. That is why the digital documents are prepared beforehand, not after approval.

How to recognise a real announcement about session 2

The hierarchy of sources is short and has no exceptions. The implementation procedure sets the conditions, expense categories, scoring grid and calendar; the ministerial order approves it; the electronic enrolment application opens on the date in the procedure. The procedure is approved by MEDAT Order no. 328/17.03.2025 (OG no. 243/20.03.2025), amended by Orders 1,565/2025 (OG 647/09.07.2025), 2,338/2025 (OG 1091/26.11.2025), 280/2026 (OG 167/04.03.2026), 480/2026 (OG 256/01.04.2026) and 922/2026 (OG 526/26.06.2026); the consolidated text is at legislatie.just.ro/Public/DetaliiDocumentAfis/311591. Anything else — article, programme list, post — is commentary on these documents, useful only if it points to them.

The first sign a page runs on old information is the RON 200,000 figure. It belongs to the 2017, 2018-2019 and 2022 editions, all closed; for session 2 the announced support is up to RON 250,000. Whoever builds a plan on 200,000 starts RON 50,000 below the announced ceiling.

What you can fix now, while the course is open

The step with a deadline is course enrolment, by 27 November 2026. Meanwhile, the time until company enrolment is useful if it produces documents, not intentions: the functional specification of the digital solution, the supplier's signed technical offer and the cost breakdown by category. These go into the business plan as they are, and answer an evaluator's questions — what is built, with what functionality, in what stages and at what market price.

The cost structure is already in the procedure (point 5.2, para. 2): the digital component — presentation website, online store, domain, promotion of the site or store, software, electronic signature, IT equipment — sits at letter g). The category-by-category detail is on the Start-Up Nation eligible costs page, which we do not duplicate here.

The electronic signature is mandatory for enrolment (point 6.3.1, para. 16) and is the one eligible cost on that list you can incur before the contract. Buy it early, not on the first day of the window.

The date on the offer, and the limits we keep

An offer signed today carries today's date. If the funding application comes in a few months, we reissue it at the correct date with the prices of that time. We do not backdate anything and do not issue undated offers.

We do not write the business plan, do not take the entrepreneurship course for you, do not file the application, do not assess your company's eligibility and do not promise approval. We supply the software component and work alongside your consultant, not in their place. The procedure itself explains why (point 5.2, para. 2, letter h): consultancy is eligible up to RON 10,000 and may be bought before the contract, but a firm that supplies consultancy in the programme cannot supply any other eligible cost in the same project, or none of that supplier's costs are reimbursed. The consultant who writes the plan therefore cannot also deliver the website, and we do not write the plan while delivering the website.

Frequently asked questions

Until when can I enrol, and when are funding applications filed?

For the free course, until 27 November 2026, 20:00, on minimis.imm.gov.ro; the original 30-day deadline was already extended on 4 November. Funding applications come after the course, in stage two, whose date the MEDAT announcements verified on 18 November 2026 do not give.

When do companies enrol for funding in session 2?

On 18 November 2026 the date is not announced. The procedure provides a 45-working-day window, from 10:00 on the first day to 20:00 on the last, with the date published beforehand on the ministry website. It can be extended, but to no later than 7 working days after the last course ends.

Who can enrol in the session 2 courses?

Young people aged 18 to under 30 with at least ISCED level 2 education, whatever their labour-market status. Places are split into four categories: Bucharest-Ilfov, Roma from Bucharest-Ilfov, the less developed regions and Roma from those regions. The course is 40 hours, online or in person.

Can I apply if I am over 30?

Not to the session 2 courses, which opened only the four categories for people aged 18 to under 30. An over-30 category existed in first-session funding, with 2,575 places in the less developed regions, but it is not among the course categories MEDAT announced for session 2. The categories of the session 2 company window are not yet announced; check the MEDAT announcement before giving up.

If I did not finish the course in the first session, can I re-enrol?

Yes, if you were declared eligible in the first session and did not graduate. You re-enrol by updating your initial application from the same e-mail account, not by filing a new one, per the MEDAT announcement of 30 September 2026. The deadline is the course's: 27 November 2026, 20:00.

What does the 8 December 2027 deadline mean?

Expenditure is made and the reimbursement request filed within 12 months of the contract taking effect, but no later than 8 December 2027. For session 2, a contract signed in mid-2027 leaves, by our reading of the text in force, about 6 months.

Why do other pages show the RON 200,000 ceiling?

That was the ceiling of the 2017, 2018-2019 and 2022 editions, three closed sessions, and those pages were never updated. The support announced for session 2 is up to RON 250,000 per beneficiary. It is the simplest test of whether a source runs on current information.

Can Northdan write my Start-Up Nation business plan?

No. You build the plan with a consultant or alone; we deliver only the technology chapter, with the offer and cost breakdown that support it. The procedure bars a firm that supplies consultancy from supplying any other eligible cost in the same project, so the plan's consultant cannot also deliver the website.
