An ERP financed with EU funds: where it fits today and what scores points

An ERP has two open routes today, with very different rules: in one it is named in the guide and earns points, in the other only a licence fits.

5

An ERP can be financed from EU funds today on two routes: call 2 of Action 2.2 in the Centru Regional Programme, whose guide lists ERP explicitly among eligible intangible assets (5.3.2.1), for non-ICT SMEs implementing in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș or Sibiu, filing in MySMIS2021 between 2 December 2026, 12:00, and 2 February 2027, 12:00; and SME Eco-Tech, open until 30 December 2026, 20:00, where software qualifies only up to 15,000 lei excluding VAT. In call 2 an ERP also earns points: criterion 1.2 of the evaluation grid (Annex 4) gives 15 points instead of 10 to a firm that meets at least 7 of the 12 DESI criteria and uses at least one of ERP, CRM, IoT, cloud, AI, VR/AR or RPA. The grant in call 2 is 15,000–200,000 EUR with at least 10% own contribution, and implementation after contract signing lasts at most 24 months. The 20,000–100,000 EUR band from PNRR C9 is no longer a route: applications closed on 30 June 2023 and the payment deadline, 30 October 2026, has passed. Checked on 18 November 2026 against the final call 2 guide, Annex 4 and the SME Eco-Tech procedure.

The open routes for an ERP today

Call 2 of PR Centru Action 2.2 is for non-ICT SMEs implementing in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș or Sibiu; firms with CAEN codes on the Annex 3 list are excluded, and a software company cannot apply. The grant is 15,000–200,000 EUR. The guide names ERP among eligible activities (5.2.2 A a: “data management and analysis systems (e.g. RPA, CRM, PLM, ERP etc.)”) and among intangible assets (5.3.2.1 a). Installation, configuration, commissioning and testing are eligible (5.3.2.1 f), and staff training is reimbursed as a unit cost of 658.63 EUR per employee who completes a programme of at least 12 hours with a certificate (5.3.2.6).

SME Eco-Tech, open until 30 December 2026, 20:00, caps software needed for the activity at 15,000 lei excluding VAT and rules out installation, labour, commissioning and training; an ERP implementation does not fit here, a licence with a few modules may. The programme requires a project of at least 668,100 lei in eligible costs, of which the bank credit is at least 400,860 lei and the grant at most 267,240 lei (40%), and green equipment of at least 40% of total eligible costs.

For a firm that does not exist yet, Start-Up Nation session 2 is a third path, only for course graduates aged 18 to 30: course enrolment is open until 27.11.2026, 20:00, followed by up to 250,000 lei per beneficiary, at most 90%, with spending until 8 December 2027.

What the system must be to count as an ERP in call 2

The guide's definition (item 13) asks for a set of applications that integrate and process information for several departments — planning, purchasing, sales, marketing, finance, human resources — in a client-server or web environment, cover most of the company's processes and transactions, store each item once and give real-time access to data. A tool for invoicing and inventory alone risks falling short, and the criterion 1.2 score depends on the definition.

Intangible assets are accepted if used exclusively by the beneficiary, bought at market conditions from a third party with no links to the buyer, and kept in the firm's assets for at least 3 years after project completion (guide 5.3.2.1). In the evaluation grid, criterion 2.2 gives 5 points when intangible investment exceeds 50% of tangible investment, against 3 and 2 points on the lower bands.

Licence, cloud subscription or bespoke ERP

The guide accepts temporary subscription licences (5.2.2 A a) and bespoke or adapted software (5.3.2.1 a). SaaS, PaaS or IaaS subscriptions are reimbursed only for the implementation period, on condition that the firm covers the costs from its own resources during the durability period (5.3.2.5); an upfront payment for 3 or 5 years counts proportionally to the implementation period.

In practice a cloud ERP means a subscription the firm pays after the project ends, and a bespoke ERP means maintenance over the same span. Durability is three years from the final payment according to the guide (5.1.1.4); confirm the term with ADR Centru before putting it in the budget.

Closed routes that articles still quote

PNRR C9 I3.1 (20,000–100,000 EUR): applications closed on 30.06.2023, implementation ran to 30.06 and 31.07.2026, payment to 30.10.2026. POCIDIF Action 2.1 closed on 30.10.2026, 17:00 (MIPE Order no. 1500/29.09.2026) and financed ICT firms building a product for the market, never an ERP for own use. Do not prepare for either. The reimbursed scope stays the one in the funding application: extending the ERP beyond it is paid separately, from the firm's own funds.

The figures any cap discussion starts from

We publish indicative bands; a firm figure comes from a written offer after the process analysis. Implementing a market product, with migration and training, costs 5,000–20,000 EUR. The core of a bespoke system — inventory, purchasing, sales, invoicing — costs 10,000–15,000 EUR, and a modular bespoke ERP without per-user licences falls in 15,000–60,000 EUR.

In call 2 the minimum grant is 15,000 EUR, so at 90% the eligible project must be at least 16,667 EUR (computed from guide 3.4 and 5.4); a project made only of a 10,000–15,000 EUR core is under that floor and needs other eligible lines, such as training, the hardware that runs the software, or the audit. The most forgotten cost: maintenance of a bespoke system is planned at 15–20% per year of the development cost and stays with the beneficiary throughout the durability period.

Frequently asked questions

Can an ERP be bought with EU funds in 2026?

Yes, on two routes: PR Centru call 2, where the guide names ERP (5.3.2.1), for non-ICT SMEs in the Centru region, filing from 02.12.2026 to 02.02.2027 with a 15,000–200,000 EUR grant; and SME Eco-Tech, with software up to 15,000 lei excluding VAT, open until 30.12.2026.

Is a cloud ERP subscription reimbursed?

Yes, only for the implementation period (guide 5.3.2.5). During the three durability years the firm pays it from its own funds, and an upfront payment for 3–5 years counts proportionally to the implementation period.

Are ERP implementation and training eligible?

In call 2 yes: installation, configuration, commissioning and testing (5.3.2.1 f), and training as a unit cost of 658.63 EUR per certified employee (5.3.2.6). In SME Eco-Tech no: the procedure excludes installation, labour, commissioning and training (4.3 para. 2).

Can I still apply to the 100,000 EUR PNRR digitalization call?

No. PNRR C9 I3.1 closed to applications on 30.06.2023 and was not reopened; existing contracts had a payment deadline of 30.10.2026, now past.

How long does an ERP implementation take inside a fixed-deadline project?

For a typical SME, between three and eight months depending on modules and the quality of existing data. In call 2 implementation after the contract takes at most 24 months (guide 5.7.3), extendable only up to 31.12.2029; when the deadline is tight, we narrow phase one to the mandatory flows and schedule the rest after reimbursement.
