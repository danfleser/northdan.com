PNRR advanced digital technology grants (C9 I3): who received them, the 31 December 2026 end date and what comes next

The EUR 150 million call closed in May 2024, and for contracted projects the scheme sets one final deadline: 31 December 2026, for both implementation and payments.

The programme's official applicant guide
ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

The PNRR C9 I3 call «Granturi pentru sprijinirea antreprenorilor în dezvoltarea tehnologiilor digitale avansate» (grants for entrepreneurs developing advanced digital technologies) has not accepted applications since 22 May 2024, and on 18 November 2026 contracted projects are in their last six weeks: the scheme approved by MIPE Order no. 464/2024 caps implementation at 31 December 2026, and aid payments can also be made only until 31 December 2026 (art. 14(8) and art. 24(1)). The call had EUR 150 million (EUR 130 million from the PNRR and EUR 20 million from the state budget, for non-deductible VAT) and targeted 108 contracts, and MIPE reported 52 signed contracts (MIPE page on the digitalization components for SMEs and NGOs, published on oportunitati-ue.gov.ro on 29 October 2025). Only partnerships could apply: an SME not active in IT as lead, and an IT SME as partner, both established by 31 December 2021 and with operating profit in 2022. Grants were EUR 500,000–700,000 for a micro-enterprise lead, EUR 500,000–1,500,000 for a small one and EUR 500,000–3,000,000 for a medium one (art. 6(2)). Verified on 18 November 2026 in the scheme published in Monitorul Oficial no. 155/26.02.2024, as amended by MIPE Orders 758/2025 and 1988/2025 (consolidated text on legislatie.just.ro), and on the call page at mfe.gov.ro. Northdan Soft does not write funding applications, is not a party to the partnership and promises no approval: we build or finish the software components of an already contracted project and hand over the technical documents the transfer request needs.

Two EUR 3,000,000 ceilings are easy to confuse. The second belongs to POCIDIF Action 2.1, which closed on 30 October 2026, where that amount went only to an innovative hardware product made by the beneficiary; for a software result the ceiling was EUR 1,500,000, with a EUR 200,000 minimum (MIPE Order no. 965/23.06.2026).

Call timeline, from launch to 31 December 2026

The applicant guide was approved by MIPE Order no. 815/21.03.2024 and the call launched on 25 March 2024. Submission ran from 25 March to 22 May 2024, after two extensions (MIPE Order no. 1068/22.04.2024 and Order no. 2026/13.05.2024). The final list after technical and financial evaluation was published on 22 January 2025, followed by interim lists of projects admitted for funding and by contracting.

MIPE Order no. 758 of 15 April 2025 amended the scheme, and Order no. 1988 of 2 July 2025 amended the intensity increases for industrial research and experimental development (art. 9(3)) and added the increase for feasibility studies (art. 9(4)). On 22 July 2025 Instruction no. 1 C9.I3.1.TA was issued, on the supporting documents of the transfer request. Aid could be granted until 30 June 2026 (art. 24(1)(a)), and implementation and payments end on 31 December 2026.

On 18 November 2026, there is no amending or extension order on mfe.gov.ro or oportunitati-ue.gov.ro after Order 1988/2025 and Instruction no. 1 of 22.07.2025, so no extension beyond 31 December 2026.

Who could apply: a non-IT SME as lead and an IT SME as partner

The scheme requires a partnership (art. 3 and art. 14(1)). The lead is the SME not active in IT: it starts the project, files the application and signs the financing contract. The partner is an SME active in IT. A lead with an IT activity code could enter only for an eligible non-IT activity, shown by a chartered accountant's report (art. 14(9)).

The lead adopts the technology, with at least 70% regional aid, while the research and development part, at most 30%, is carried out jointly by lead and partner; the lead's de minimis aid does not exceed EUR 300,000 (art. 6(3)). The domains covered were artificial intelligence, data and cloud, blockchain, high-performance computing and quantum technologies, IoT/IIoT, cybersecurity, augmented and virtual reality and robotic process automation (art. 1 and art. 13). The scheme allowed at most 300 beneficiaries (art. 26).

The two EUR 3 million ceilings, side by side

PNRR C9 I3: EUR 500,000–3,000,000 per project, depending on the size of the lead, submission 25 March – 22 May 2024, in partnership. The figure now matters only to beneficiaries in implementation.

POCIDIF Action 2.1: minimum EUR 200,000, maximum EUR 1,500,000 for a software solution result and EUR 3,000,000 only for an innovative hardware product made by the beneficiary. Submission ran from 30 June to 30 October 2026 (extended by MIPE Order no. 1500/29.09.2026) and the call is closed. If a project's result was an application, the correct ceiling was EUR 1,500,000, not 3,000,000.

The last six weeks of a C9 I3 project

By 31 December 2026, what the project promised must be delivered, accepted and paid. The critical part is not buying equipment but development: trained and validated models, the data platform, sensor integration, hardened infrastructure. The transfer request needs acceptance protocols, delivery reports, technical documentation and proof of operation, and the research and development part is reported separately.

The date in the financing contract is the one that counts for each project. Any possible extension is confirmed only by your MIPE project officer, not by a press announcement. After 31 December 2026 an SME beneficiary must still maintain the investment: the durability period is 3 years for SMEs, counted from project completion (art. 3(uu)).

What a company that missed the call can do now

No new C9 I3 round has been announced, and the PNRR ends in 2026. The nearest call for a non-IT SME in the Centru region is PR Centru 2.2, call 2: EUR 15,000–200,000, submission from 2 December 2026, 12:00, to 2 February 2027, 12:00. Nationally, MIPE estimates POCIDIF 1.1.1 «Sprijin pentru întreprinderile inovatoare» between 15 December 2026 and 15 February 2027, but no guide has been published.

Frequently asked questions

Can projects still be submitted to the PNRR advanced digital technologies call?

No. Submission ran from 25 March to 22 May 2024 and has ended. No new round has been announced, and the PNRR ends in 2026. Contracted projects are in implementation.

By when must a C9 I3 project be finished?

Implementation lasts at most 30 months from the contract, but no later than 31 December 2026 (art. 14(8)). Aid payments can also be made only until 31 December 2026 (art. 24(1)(b)). The date in your financing contract governs.

Who could apply under PNRR C9 I3?

A partnership between an SME not active in IT, as lead, and an IT SME, as partner. Both had to be established by 31 December 2021 and show operating profit in 2022 (art. 14(1)).

How large were the grants under PNRR C9 I3?

EUR 500,000–700,000 for a micro-enterprise lead, up to EUR 1,500,000 for a small one and up to EUR 3,000,000 for a medium one. At least 70% was regional aid, at most 30% research and development, and the lead's de minimis aid was capped at EUR 300,000 (art. 6).

Is the EUR 3 million ceiling in POCIDIF 2.1 the same thing?

No. Under POCIDIF Action 2.1, closed on 30 October 2026, EUR 3,000,000 went only to an innovative hardware product made by the beneficiary. For a software result the ceiling was EUR 1,500,000, with a EUR 200,000 minimum (MIPE Order no. 965/23.06.2026).

How does this call differ from the PNRR SME digitalization call of up to EUR 100,000?

Both sit in PNRR C9, investment 3, measure 1. The small call gave EUR 20,000–100,000 to a single firm, as de minimis aid, and closed on 30 June 2023. This one gave EUR 0.5–3 million to a non-IT plus IT partnership, as state aid and de minimis, with implementation until 31 December 2026.
