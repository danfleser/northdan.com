What a company can still get from PNRR now that every call has closed

No PNRR call still takes grant applications from companies. PNRR money still reaches companies by two routes without a call: a bank loan with an InvestEU guarantee, or capital from an investment fund.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

As of 18 November 2026, no PNRR line still accepts grant applications from companies, and the PNRR money that still reaches a company comes by two routes with no call and no file at the ministry: a loan from a partner bank of the European Investment Fund, backed by the InvestEU guarantee financed through PNRR (about 2,000 SMEs and EUR 610 million by July 2025; the instrument was topped up with EUR 200 million, paid on 28 August 2026), or an equity investment from one of the 20 funds that received the full EUR 380 million of the Recovery Equity Fund (MIPE, 12 June 2026). Verified on 18 November 2026 on mfe.gov.ro, in the PNRR announcements and in the MIPE releases of 22.07.2025, 12.06.2026 and 02.09.2026. For firms holding a C9 digitalization contract the calendar is over: implementation and the final transfer request were due on 31 July 2026 (Ordinul MIPE nr. 964/2026), and 30 October 2026 (Ordinul MIPE nr. 963/2026) was the date by which MIPE paid the aid; MIPE's list of 2 November 2026 shows the last payments on 30 October (189 that day); no new order for this call appears on mfe.gov.ro. The grants taking applications now sit outside PNRR: the Centru Regional Programme, call 2, from 2 December 2026, and SME Eco-Tech, until 30 December 2026; below you also find the explanation for the EUR 20,000–100,000 figure that many pages still present as current. Northdan Soft builds the software and its acceptance documents; it does not write funding applications, loan files or fund pitch decks, and it promises no approval.

What PNRR money still reaches companies in November 2026

The first route is the InvestEU guarantee, the Romania Compartment financed through PNRR. The company files nothing with the ministry: it asks a partner bank for a loan "with InvestEU guarantee", and the European Investment Fund guarantees the bank, which lowers the interest, the collateral required and the down payment. By July 2025 about 2,000 SMEs had received EUR 610 million, at an average loan of roughly EUR 300,000, through 12 partner banks: BCR, Banca Transilvania, CEC Bank, Raiffeisen, ProCredit, UniCredit, Alpha Bank, Libra Internet Bank, BRD, Patria Bank, Intesa Sanpaolo and Garanti BBVA (MIPE, 22.07.2025). On 2 September 2026 MIPE announced that the EUR 200 million top-up of the "portfolio guarantee for resilience" was paid on 28 August, after Government Decision no. 625/2026, and that operationalisation follows, meaning making it available to the partner banks; digitalizing a business is among the uses. The release does not say the top-up is PNRR money, so we do not write it that way. We do not know which banks offer it today: ask your bank directly.

The second route is equity. The Recovery Equity Fund of Funds (REF), from PNRR Component 9 and managed by the European Investment Fund, distributed the full EUR 380 million of investable budget to 20 venture capital, private equity and renewable-energy infrastructure funds; the last allocation was approved in June 2026. By the end of 2025 the funds had financed 24 companies with over EUR 140 million; among the Romania-led teams MIPE cites GapMinder, Morphosis, EarlyGame Ventures and Booster Capital. The company receives money in exchange for a share of its equity, directly from the fund, not from the ministry, and the route suits growing companies, not a EUR 30,000 software purchase.

What does not exist: no open PNRR grant for companies, no submission call, and no "PNRR 2026 consulting session" that leads to a grant. Whoever sells you that is selling a closed line.

The closed PNRR lines and their last dates

SME digitalization, Component 9 Investment 3.1: applications between 15 February and 30 June 2023; implementation was due on 30 June 2026 (Ordinul MIPE nr. 607/2026), moved to 31 July 2026 by Ordinul MIPE nr. 964/23.06.2026 for projects that notified an extension; the final transfer request, also due by 31 July 2026, with costs incurred and paid during implementation; Ordinul MIPE nr. 963/2026 sets the date by which MIPE pays the aid, 30 October 2026, not a beneficiary deadline. Details are on the line's page.

Advanced digital technologies, Component 9, with projects between EUR 500,000 and EUR 3,000,000: closed for applications; implementation status is on its page. The call for non-governmental organisations, Component 7 Investment 9: implementation reached 31 July 2026 by Ordinul MIPE nr. 1051/2026, after the steps of 30 April (Order 5.774/2025) and 30 June (Order 609/2026).

Investment 19 of Component 7 is the most frequently misdescribed. It is not a grant: it is de minimis aid of at most EUR 17,000 per undertaking, granted exclusively as employee training programmes across nine technology domains, run by the Romanian Digitalization Authority (ADR). The company receives no money in its account and cannot redirect the amount to licences, equipment or software development. Enrolment closed on 5 May 2025, 17:00, the declared implementation period is December 2022 – December 2025, and the project closing event took place on 18 May 2026.

The EUR 20,000–100,000 figure: what it is and what it is not

That band still appears on pages presented as current, sometimes split by company size. What we can state as verified is what it is not: it describes no call receiving applications at this moment. Not even POCIDIF Action 2.1, closed on 30 October 2026, had a tier below EUR 200,000, and its ceiling for a software result was EUR 1,500,000.

The band belongs to the PNRR C9 I3.1 call for digitalizing SMEs outside the IT sector: applications were received between 15 February and 30 June 2023 and the call has been closed ever since, with its own page here. Two further closed documents circulate around it, disambiguated on our POC 2014-2020 archive page. The practical conclusion is unchanged: a plan sized at EUR 100,000 on the assumption of an open call rests on a figure with no call behind it.

The same caution applies to the EUR 3,000,000 figure, which appears on two different programmes. On the PNRR advanced-technologies line it belongs to a closed call; on POCIDIF Action 2.1 it was granted only to projects whose result was an innovative hardware product manufactured by the beneficiary, and the portion above EUR 1,500,000 had to be justified by financing the production line. A software company budgeting on EUR 3,000,000 is reading somebody else's ceiling.

What takes applications now, outside PNRR

PR Centru 2.2, call 2: EUR 15,000–200,000, submission from 2 December 2026, 12:00, to 2 February 2027, 12:00, with at least 10% own contribution, for non-ICT SMEs in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș and Sibiu. SME Eco-Tech: until 30 December 2026, 20:00, a grant of at most 40%, capped at RON 267,240, together with a bank loan of at least 60%. Start-Up Nation, session 2: enrolment in the free course, for people aged 18 to under 30, closes on 27 November 2026, 20:00; firms founded by graduates can then ask for up to RON 250,000, at most 90% of eligible costs.

POCIDIF Action 2.1 closed on 30 October 2026, 17:00 (deadline extended by Ordinul MIPE nr. 1500/29.09.2026) and addressed only firms holding ICT CAEN codes. Each call has its own page, with conditions and eligible costs.

The file of a finished PNRR project

After 31 July 2026 nothing eligible can be delivered or paid on a C9 or NGO contract, so there are no documents to backdate and we sign none. What remains is the file for audits: the software's technical documentation, the user manual, the acceptance protocols, licences, invoices and payment proofs, kept as long as the financing contract requires, including through the sustainability period.

One limit stated upfront: we do not sign acceptance protocols, conformity declarations or test reports for software delivered by another supplier. We can audit what we find and document what exists, but our signature covers only our own work.

What to prepare now if you are waiting for a new call

A new wave of digitalization funding becomes real only when an official guide is published; until then, any circulating calendar is an estimate. What depends on no guide: an inventory of the processes to digitalize, a technical specification of the solution, and a budget by cost category built on real offers, at least two or a dated market analysis for each price. The same set of documents also helps with a guaranteed loan or a conversation with a fund. The application and the business plan remain yours and your consultant's.

Frequently asked questions

Is there still PNRR money for companies in 2026?⌄

Not as grants: on 18.11.2026 no PNRR call accepts applications from companies. Two routes without a call remain: loans with an InvestEU guarantee from the European Investment Fund's partner banks, and equity from the 20 Recovery Equity Fund funds.

How do I get a loan with the InvestEU guarantee from PNRR?⌄

Through a bank, not MIPE: you ask a partner bank for a loan with an InvestEU guarantee. In July 2025 there were 12 partner banks, from BCR to Garanti BBVA. The EUR 200 million top-up was paid on 28.08.2026 and is being operationalised, so ask your bank whether it already uses it.

What is the Recovery Equity Fund and who receives money from it?⌄

A PNRR fund of funds managed by the European Investment Fund, which allocated the full EUR 380 million to 20 equity funds. The companies they invest in receive the money, in exchange for shares: 24 companies, with over EUR 140 million, by the end of 2025.

Until when did MIPE pay the C9 digitalization grants?⌄

Until 30.10.2026, under Ordinul MIPE nr. 963/2026. MIPE's list of 02.11.2026 shows the last payments on 30 October 2026. Beneficiaries' final transfer request was due on 31.07.2026, so there is no remaining filing date for this call.

What does Investment 19 provide, and why can it not buy software?⌄

The de minimis aid of at most EUR 17,000 per undertaking is granted exclusively as training programmes in the nine technology domains listed by ADR. The company reimburses no invoices and receives no sum in its account. Enrolment closed on 05.05.2025, and a supplier tying a software offer to this investment is selling outside the scheme.

Where do I find the MIPE orders for my project?⌄

They are issued by the Ministry of Investments and European Projects and published in the PNRR announcements on mfe.gov.ro. For SME digitalization (C9) the relevant ones are nr. 607/2026, 964/2026 and 963/2026; for the NGO call, nr. 1051/2026. Read them with your own contract, because addenda can change what applies.

My C9 project has ended. What do I keep for audits?⌄

The software's technical documentation, the manual, the acceptance protocols, licences, invoices and payment proofs, for as long as the financing contract requires, including the sustainability period. Documents cannot be backdated and no costs can be added after 31.07.2026.
