EU Funding

The 2021-2027 EU framework: where companies find their funding

A business owner's map of Romania's 2021-2027 EU programmes: which programme does what, and where exactly digitalization gets funded.

ⓘNorthdan Soft este furnizor de servicii software, nu consultant de finanțare, și nu este afiliat autorităților de management sau agențiilor de dezvoltare. Informațiile despre programe au caracter orientativ — verifică întotdeauna condițiile în ghidul oficial al programului.

Romania divided its 2021-2027 EU money across programmes with distinct roles, and companies that understand this map find their funding line far faster. Company digitalization lives mainly in POCIDIF — Programul Operațional Creștere Inteligentă, Digitalizare și Instrumente Financiare (Smart Growth, Digitalisation and Financial Instruments Programme) — where action 2.1 has a call open right now (30 June – 30 September 2026) for SMEs with an IT&C CAEN code. Just transition has its own programme, PTJ (Programul Tranziție Justă), limited to six counties — Gorj, Hunedoara, Dolj, Galați, Prahova and Mureș — currently in implementation, with new calls announced by MIPE. PEO, the Education and Employment Programme, covers skills and jobs: the guide of a relevant call has been published, but the launch is delayed. Separate from this framework runs the PNRR, Romania's recovery and resilience plan with its own deadlines, whose digitalization chapter is in project execution. Northdan Soft helps companies turn any of these opportunities into a concrete software project, documented correctly for the application file.

The programmes that matter to the private sector

POCIDIF concentrates smart growth and digitalization: direct grants (action 2.1, open until 30 September 2026 for the IT&C sector), subsidised services through the EDIH network, and financial instruments such as guaranteed credit via FNGCIMM.

PTJ funds the economic reconversion of the six coal and heavy-industry counties; PEO targets skills and employment, with a published guide and a call not yet launched; the regional programmes and the agricultural schemes (through AFIR) complete the picture, each with its own guides and calendars.

PNRR: a separate instrument with separate deadlines

The PNRR is not part of the 2021-2027 financial framework but of the post-pandemic recovery mechanism, which is exactly why its deadlines are far harsher. Its SME digitalization calls have closed, and winning projects must be completed and paid in 2026.

In practice, in 2026 the PNRR is no longer a source of new digitalization money but an execution ground — and Northdan is the vendor delivering the software of these projects on schedule, acceptance documents included.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between the 2021-2027 funds and the PNRR?⌄

The 2021-2027 framework is the classic multiannual EU budget, with programmes like POCIDIF, PTJ or PEO and a long execution horizon. The PNRR is the recovery mechanism, with compressed deadlines — which is why its digitalization projects are already closing in 2026.

Which programme in the 2021-2027 framework funds company digitalization?⌄

The main vehicle is POCIDIF: action 2.1 for grants (open to the IT&C sector in the summer of 2026), the EDIH network for subsidised services and the FNGCIMM scheme for guaranteed credit with a grant component. Other programmes carry digitalization only as a secondary activity.

My company is based in Prahova. What does PTJ mean for me?⌄

Prahova is one of the six PTJ-eligible counties, together with Gorj, Hunedoara, Dolj, Galați and Mureș. Contracted calls are in implementation and MIPE has announced future sessions — worth having your project defined before they launch.
