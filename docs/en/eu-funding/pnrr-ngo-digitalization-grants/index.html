PNRR C9 — NGO digitalization: closing projects out in 2026

Romania's non-profit sector had its own digitalization call within PNRR C9, parallel to the SME line; applications are closed, and organisations with signed contracts are now racing to finish their projects.

Non-profits were not left out of component C9 of PNRR, Romania's National Recovery and Resilience Plan: a dedicated call for digitalizing non-governmental organisations ran in parallel with the SME line, with amounts and conditions set per the official applicant guide. Project submission closed long ago, but the story did not end there — through Ordinul MIPE 609/2026 (an order of Romania's EU funds ministry), implementation deadlines for NGO projects were extended, mirroring Ordinul 607/2026 issued for SMEs. One distinction, however, binds every C9 beneficiary: the extension concerns implementation — delivering and accepting the digital solutions — whereas the payment claim keeps its own calendar, set through the applicable MIPE acts. For an organisation with a contract underway, the priority is a well-run finishing stretch: the website, donation platform or donor CRM accepted before the deadline, with the technical paperwork ready for disbursement. Northdan Soft covers exactly that ground, as a software vendor serving the non-profit sector.

The NGO call within C9, in brief

Component C9 of the PNRR included, alongside the well-known SME digitalization line, a parallel call reserved for non-governmental organisations. Grant values and the precise list of eligible costs are those in the call's official guide — no unverified figures circulate on this page.

The typology of funded projects, per the guide, revolved around the sector's real needs: a credible web presence, online fundraising tools, donor-relationship records and the digitalization of the organisation's internal administrative flows.

Ordinul 609/2026 and the two deadlines that must not be confused

MIPE regulated the deadline extensions through a pair of orders: 607/2026 for SME projects and 609/2026 for NGO ones. If your organisation holds a contract on the digitalization call, order 609 is the one governing your completion calendar.

Extending implementation does not rewrite the disbursement calendar: on C9, filing the payment claim carries its own deadlines, established through the MIPE acts applicable to the call. The right plan for an NGO is delivering and accepting the solutions by the implementation deadline, then preparing the payment file without panic.

Frequently asked questions

Can I still submit a project to the PNRR NGO digitalization call?⌄

No — the NGO-dedicated call within component C9 is closed for submissions, and contracted projects are in implementation. If MIPE launches a new line for the non-profit sector, we will flag it on this page together with the official announcement.

What did Ordinul MIPE 609/2026 change for NGOs with digitalization projects?⌄

Order 609/2026 extended the implementation deadlines for NGO digitalization projects under C9, in parallel with Order 607/2026 covering SMEs. Check the addendum to your contract for the exact date applying to your organisation.

Does the implementation extension also postpone the payment claim for NGO projects?⌄

Not automatically — on component C9, implementation and the payment claim run on separate calendars, each set through the applicable MIPE acts. Get the digital solutions accepted by the implementation deadline and treat disbursement as a distinct stage, with documents prepared in advance.

Which digital solutions were typically eligible for NGOs on this call?⌄

Per the official guide, projects typically covered websites, online donation platforms, CRM for donor records and the digitalization of internal processes — the exact list and ceilings remain those in the documentation published by MIPE.

How does Northdan Soft help an NGO whose PNRR project is running late?⌄

We take over the remaining software — website, donation platform, donor CRM — deliver it in short stages before the deadline extended by Ordinul 609/2026 and hand over the acceptance documents for the payment file. First step: a WhatsApp message with the project's current status.
