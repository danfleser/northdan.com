Grant-funded software in Romania: for your own use, or as a product to sell

Software used inside your own company has grant money in winter 2026–2027; software built as a product for sale has no open national call at 18 November 2026, and the European routes remain (EIC Accelerator, Digital Europe).

As of 18 November 2026, non-repayable funds for software exist in the national and regional programmes only for software the company uses, not for software it sells: call 2 of Action 2.2 in PR Centru grants EUR 15,000–200,000 to non-ICT SMEs in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș and Sibiu, with submission between 02.12.2026 at 12:00 and 02.02.2027 at 12:00 (guide 4.3.1, 4.3.2, 5.4), while SME Eco-Tech accepts software of up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT, as part of a bank-credit project, until 30.12.2026 at 20:00. The EUR 20,000–100,000 range often shown as a current offer belongs to the PNRR C9 SME digitalisation call, which closed to submissions on 30 June 2023. For software developed as an innovative product, the last national call reserved for ICT SMEs was POCIDIF Action 2.1 (EUR 200,000–1,500,000 per project), closed on 30.10.2026 at 17:00 by MIPE Order no. 1500/29.09.2026; the next call for innovative enterprises, POCIDIF 1.1.1, appears only as an estimate in the MIPE calendar for 15.12.2026–15.02.2027; for an innovative software product, EIC Accelerator (grant of up to EUR 2.5 million, short proposal open at any time) and Digital Europe CYBER-11-AI4SME, until 14.01.2027, remain. A company that does not exist yet can put software into its Start-Up Nation business plan, session 2 (up to RON 250,000, at most 90%), if the founder is aged 18 to 29 (under 30) and enrols in the mandatory course by 27.11.2026 at 20:00. The EUR 3,000,000 figure from headlines applied under POCIDIF 2.1 only to projects whose result is an innovative hardware product. Northdan Soft is the software supplier in the file: it does not write the application, does not decide eligibility and does not promise approval.

Software used in the company: what PR Centru call 2 accepts

The guide (5.3.2.1) lists under intangible assets software that is bought or custom-built: e-invoicing, RPA, CRM, PLM, ERP, finance, HR and logistics applications, artificial intelligence and chatbots, IoT, VR/AR, blockchain, plus a presentation website, an online store and cybersecurity. There is no separate ceiling on software and no fixed ratio between hardware and software (5.3.2.2). The grant is at most 90%, the same for micro, small and medium enterprises, so at least 10% is the company's own contribution (3.4, 5.5); a project without intangible assets is ineligible (3.6, 5.2.4).

The conditions that concern the supplier: the software is bought from third parties unrelated to the buyer (5.3.2.1), no firm order is placed before submission (5.3.3), and cloud subscriptions are settled only for the implementation period, after which the company pays them during the 3-year durability period (5.3.2.5, 5.1.1.4). Companies with ICT codes (5821, 5829, 6201, 6202, 6209 and others in Annex 3) cannot apply.

The categories are written separately: development or licence, installation, configuration and testing (5.3.2.1 f), migration of legacy data and integrations. Maintenance is not eligible (5.3.3), and training is settled separately, as a unit cost of EUR 658.63 per certified employee (5.3.2.6). Recurring costs after implementation usually fall outside the eligible budget. VAT treatment also differs from programme to programme and must be read before comparing offers.

SME Eco-Tech: software accompanies the project, it does not lead it

The SME Eco-Tech procedure (4.1) includes in the digital package software needed for the activity of up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT. The grant is at most 40%, no more than RON 267,240, and comes only together with a loan of at least 60% of eligible costs; the project is at least RON 668,100, and at least 40% of the costs must go to green equipment or resource-saving solutions.

Staff training, installation and commissioning are ineligible. Not even the core of a custom ERP (EUR 10,000–15,000, excluding VAT) fits under the programme's software cap.

Software as a product: what has closed and what comes next

POCIDIF Action 2.1 funded the development of an innovative software solution with EUR 200,000–1,500,000 per project, per the guide approved by MIPE Order no. 965/23.06.2026, for SMEs holding CAEN codes from the call's list (software publishing and programming, but also manufacture of electronic components); submission ended on 30.10.2026. The call allocation was EUR 48,813,278 and implementation up to 24 months, but no later than 31 December 2029. The EUR 3,000,000 ceiling covered only projects whose result is an innovative hardware product.

The last national call that set these rules: the result was shown by one innovative product and three proofs of commercialisation (contracts or invoices, not proformas), investment performance had to exceed 0.5, the technical audit was done by a CISA, CISM or CISSP auditor, and outsourcing all technical activities was not allowed (at least 20% of the aid for the core activity went to the beneficiary's own staff). The next call for innovative enterprises, POCIDIF 1.1.1, is estimated in the MIPE calendar for 15.12.2026–15.02.2027; check the guide when it appears. An ICT company cannot use call 2 in Centru for its own product.

Announced routes, not yet launched

PR București-Ilfov Action 1.7 foresees EUR 25,000–200,000 per project, but the guide is unpublished at 18.11.2026. PR Sud-Est Action 1.3 is not launched and not in consultation. Vouchers for artificial intelligence, cloud and cybersecurity are announced, with no call open. Until the guides appear, nothing is contracted: an order placed before submission makes the cost ineligible.

Amounts still circulating that are no longer valid

The EUR 20,000–100,000 range is PNRR C9 I3.1, with submission between 15.02 and 30.06.2023; implementation and payments have ended, the last deadline being 30.10.2026. The EUR 500,000 – 3 million figure for IT firms, from 2025 articles, is the POCIDIF 2.1 call, now closed. No call open in 2026 provides the EUR 20,000–100,000 range.

What the supplier must itemise, and what the software part costs

The technical offer: functional scope by module, architecture and integrations, migrated data, user roles, acceptance criteria, warranty and a staged delivery schedule. The budget: the same elements with a price on each, using the category names from the call's guide rather than a structure of your own. At settlement the standard set is an acceptance report per stage, the invoice, the activity report and proof of operation.

Indicatively, excluding VAT, a small automation sits between EUR 3,000 and 10,000, a business application between EUR 15,000 and 60,000, and a complex platform starts at EUR 60,000. For web applications the usual band is EUR 5,000–15,000 for a simple application and EUR 15,000–50,000 with integrations. The figures move with the number of connected systems, the roles and approval flows, the reports and the migration of legacy data; our public price guides can be used as a dated source of comparison.

What we do not do

We do not write funding applications, we do not submit files and we do not promise approval. We do not assess your company's eligibility and we do not confirm that a cost qualifies. We issue no accommodation offers: the call 2 guide requires software from third parties unrelated to the buyer (5.3.2.1) and does not allow the same firm to write the digitalisation plan and supply the software in the same project (5.3.2.8). If you choose us as the supplier, we do not write the digitalisation plan.

Frequently asked questions

Which grants for software are open now?

PR Centru call 2, from 02.12.2026, with EUR 15,000–200,000 for non-ICT SMEs in Centru, and SME Eco-Tech, with software of up to RON 15,000, until 30.12.2026 at 20:00. New companies can use Start-Up Nation, with the course open until 27.11.2026.

Can I still get EUR 20,000–100,000 for software from PNRR?

No, the PNRR C9 SME digitalisation call closed on 30.06.2023. Contracted companies had payments until 30.10.2026, which has passed. The amount keeps circulating as a current offer on pages never updated; no call open in 2026 provides that range.

Can I get funding for software that I will sell?

Not from national funds now: POCIDIF 2.1 closed on 30.10.2026 at 17:00, and POCIDIF 1.1.1 for innovative enterprises is only estimated in the MIPE calendar for 15.12.2026–15.02.2027. At European level, EIC Accelerator and Digital Europe CYBER-11-AI4SME (until 14.01.2027) remain. Watch for the guide before committing any spending.

Can an IT company get money for its own software under PR Centru call 2?

No. Annex 3 excludes ICT codes, including 5829, 6201, 6202 and 6209, and the call funds the digitalisation of companies in other fields. The software must be bought from a third-party supplier unrelated to the buyer.

Are software subscriptions and annual licences reimbursed?

In call 2, only the part falling in the implementation period (guide 5.3.2.5). During the 3-year durability period the company pays subscriptions from its own funds (5.1.1.4). We put them on a separate line, with the covered period written explicitly.

Are there funds for software in Bucharest or outside Centru?

Yes, but only the national programmes: SME Eco-Tech (software of up to RON 15,000, with a bank loan, until 30.12.2026) and, for new companies, Start-Up Nation (course until 27.11.2026). The regional calls are not open: PR București-Ilfov 1.7 has no published guide, PR Sud-Est 1.3 is not launched.

Can Northdan help me obtain the funding itself?

No. We do not write funding applications and we do not promise approval, which is the consultant's role. We cover the supplier component: technical offer, budget by eligible cost category, draft contract, development and the documents required at settlement.
