Which guaranteed loan a company can sign for digitalization, and which routes still wait for banks

A guarantee does not give you money and does not excuse repayment; it covers the bank's risk. On 18 November 2026 the guarantees you can sign carry no grant, and those with a European grant have no bank yet.

On 18 November 2026 a guaranteed loan for digitalization can be signed at a bank with the portfolio guarantee of the Investment and Development Bank, BID (up to 70% of the loan, up to 80% for women-led firms or innovative and green projects), or with an FNGCIMM guarantee from its own funds (up to 80% on investment loans, with a fee), while the three European instruments with an 80% guarantee, a 0% fee and a grant — national POCIDIF, PR Centru and PR Sud-Vest Oltenia — still have no partner bank listed on fngcimm.ro.

The word guaranteed describes what happens between the state and the bank, not between the state and your company: if the borrower stops paying, the fund covers part of the lender's loss, and the borrower still owes the whole amount. The decision belongs to the bank, which assesses creditworthiness by its own criteria; there is no submission session and no score, only a classic credit file. Verified on 18 November 2026 on fngcimm.ro and in the de-minimis scheme approved by MIPE Order no. 1,518/2026 (Official Gazette no. 844 bis of 7 October 2026).

The five guaranteed-loan routes, with their status on 18 November 2026

BID, the Investment and Development Bank portfolio guarantee: at partner banks (for example ING, BCR) it covers 70% of leu investment loans and 65% of euro ones, 60% and 55% of working capital, and up to 80% for women-led, innovative or green firms. The guarantee is capped at RON 10 million per transaction, under de minimis, and loan contracts must be signed by 31 December 2027. Software and licences count as intangible assets. At UniCredit the BID guarantee costs 0.7% a year on the monthly balance. BID does not co-finance or pre-finance projects that already have a grant. It can be signed now.

FNGCIMM own funds: the Expresă guarantee covers up to 80% of investment loans, up to EUR 2.5 million per firm or group; the Plafon guarantee carries a fee by risk class, about 2.5% on average. FNGCIMM itself writes that these are not state guarantees. It can be signed now, with no grant.

National POCIDIF: an 80% guarantee, 0% fee, a loan of up to the RON equivalent of EUR 500,000, with a grant on top. No bank listed.

PR Centru: an 80% guarantee, 0% fee, a loan of up to EUR 500,000. The capital rebate is up to 15% of the balance and is paid only if both turnover and headcount grow; the total grant is at most 25% of the loan. Investment loans run up to 60 months, and only companies under Law 31/1990 with an operating profit in the previous year may enter. No intermediary listed.

PR Sud-Vest Oltenia, for Dolj, Gorj, Mehedinți, Olt and Vâlcea: an 80% guarantee, 0% fee, a loan of up to EUR 200,000, a capital rebate of up to 25%, a total grant of at most 49%, investment loans up to 60 months. No bank listed.

SME Eco-Tech is not a guarantee route: it is a loan plus a grant with no guarantee. The grant is at most 40%, capped at RON 267,240, the loan at least 60%, applications run until 30 December 2026, 20:00, and software for the activity is eligible up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT.

POCIDIF: a 20% capital rebate and a 49% grant ceiling, not one instead of the other

The capital rebate is at most 20% on investment loans and at most 10% on working-capital loans (scheme art. 8(5)c). The 49% figure is something else: the ceiling on the whole grant, capital rebate plus interest subsidy, for one beneficiary; at portfolio level the ceiling is 45%. If the two components together exceed it, the beneficiary picks one (art. 8(6)–(8)). The interest subsidy covers the first 24 months on investment loans and the first 12 on working-capital loans, and interest is capped at ROBOR 3M plus 3%, or plus 3.5% on working capital (art. 8(5)b).

The rebate depends on turnover growing by at least 2% in the year after the investment ends; it can be paid in advance after at least 12 months if turnover grew by at least 2% (art. 8(5)d and f). If the condition stops being met, the rebate is cancelled and converted into loan (art. 9(8)). The scheme runs until 31 December 2029, and its indicative budget plans RON 0 for grants in 2026, with grants from 2027 (art. 15 and 16(3)).

On the scheme's maximum, a 20% rebate on a EUR 500,000-equivalent investment loan is EUR 100,000, only if the turnover condition is met, and it counts in full toward the EUR 300,000 de-minimis ceiling over three years under Regulation (EU) 2023/2831.

Who a guaranteed loan makes sense for

The right profile: a company with a decent financial track record and a digitalization project that generates measurable value — an ERP that cuts operating costs, a platform that opens a sales channel. Software, programme licences and patents are explicitly eligible on investment loans (art. 14(a)). Repayment requires the investment to produce, not merely to exist.

The wrong profile: a company with no repayment capacity hoping for free money. Refinancing is not accepted either, and the support cannot accompany loans for activities or costs that have already received other public funding, European or national. The entry condition is age: one year since incorporation and one full fiscal year of activity, with the registered office and the activity in Romania.

Before signing a BID loan for software you may later want to move into POCIDIF, ask the bank or FNGCIMM: a cost already financed with another publicly supported loan can block POCIDIF support for the same costs (scheme art. 8(10)).

Frequently asked questions

Which guaranteed loan for digitalization can I sign right now?

One with a BID guarantee at partner banks (for example ING, BCR), or with an FNGCIMM own-funds guarantee; neither carries a grant. The three European instruments with a grant — POCIDIF, PR Centru and PR Sud-Vest Oltenia — have no partner bank listed on fngcimm.ro on 18 November 2026.

Is the grant in the POCIDIF-FNGCIMM instrument 20% or 49%?

Both figures are correct but describe different things: 20% is the maximum capital rebate on an investment loan (art. 8(5)c), and 49% is the ceiling on the whole grant of one beneficiary, capital rebate plus interest subsidy (art. 8(7)). The capital rebate alone never reaches 49%.

How much can I borrow through the POCIDIF-FNGCIMM guarantee scheme?

The maximum is the RON equivalent of EUR 500,000 per loan, at the reference rate valid on the date the financial intermediary signs the contract with the beneficiary, within de-minimis limits. The guarantee covers up to 80% per transaction, so at most EUR 400,000, and the guarantee fee is 0%.

Do software and licences qualify for a guaranteed loan?

Yes. The POCIDIF scheme lists them explicitly for investment loans: intangible assets, software, programme licences and patents (art. 14(a)). ING's page for the BID-guaranteed loan also counts software and licences as financeable investments, with contracts signed by 31 December 2027.

Is a guaranteed loan easier to obtain than a digitalization grant?

It is more predictable, not necessarily easier: you do not wait for sessions and do not compete on score, but you go through the bank's creditworthiness assessment. Companies in weak financial shape can be turned down by the bank just as a weak file fails a grant evaluation.
