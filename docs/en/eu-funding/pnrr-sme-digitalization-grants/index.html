PNRR C9 I3.1 — SME digitalization: the 2026 finishing sprint

Applications closed back in 2023 — what 2026 is about is delivery: implementation wraps up on 30 June, and the payment claim must be filed by 30 October.

30 June 2026 is the date by which projects financed under PNRR C9 I3.1 «Digitalizarea IMM-urilor — grant de până la 100.000 euro» (the SME digitalization call of Romania's Recovery and Resilience Plan) must be implemented, under Ordinul MIPE 607/2026 — with a single exception: projects that filed an extension request in time may run until 31 July 2026. The payment claim follows an entirely separate calendar: it can be submitted until 30 October 2026, under Ordinul MIPE 963/2026. The call itself — applications open from 15 February to 30 June 2023, de minimis grants of EUR 20,000–100,000, a total budget of EUR 347.5 million and minimum 10% co-financing — has long been closed; no company can apply today. For a non-IT SME holding a signed contract, what counts is finishing well: software delivered and accepted on schedule, then correct paperwork for the payment claim. That is precisely where Northdan Soft steps in, as the software vendor — while also keeping watch on the next wave of calls announced by MIPE, Romania's Ministry of Investments and European Projects.

A closed call with everything at stake in 2026

The PNRR C9 I3.1 application window ran from 15 February to 30 June 2023, with a EUR 347.5 million budget and de minimis grants of EUR 20,000–100,000 per enterprise, at minimum 10% co-financing. Beneficiaries were non-IT SMEs pursuing the digitalization of their operations.

Eligible costs spanned a broad technology range: presentation websites and e-commerce, ERP/CRM systems, software licences, cloud services, RPA automation, cybersecurity solutions, electronic signature and staff training on the purchased tools — the exact details remain those in the consolidated official applicant guide.

The implementation deadline is not the payment deadline

The costliest confusion in this programme is mixing up the two dates. Project implementation must be completed by 30 June 2026, under Ordinul MIPE 607/2026; only projects with an extension request filed in time benefit from the 31 July 2026 date.

The payment claim runs on its own calendar: Ordinul MIPE 963/2026 allows submission until 30 October 2026. In practice, the software must be delivered and accepted by the implementation deadline, and the window afterwards is for the disbursement file — but missing the first deadline puts the entire grant at risk.

Frequently asked questions

Can I still apply to the PNRR C9 I3.1 SME digitalization call?⌄

No — submissions under PNRR C9 I3.1 ended on 30 June 2023. Should MIPE launch a new SME digitalization round, this page's status will be updated as soon as the official announcement appears.

By when must implementation be completed under PNRR C9 I3.1?⌄

The general deadline is 30 June 2026, set by Ordinul MIPE 607/2026. The 31 July 2026 date applies exclusively to projects that filed an extension request within the allowed timeframe.

What is the payment claim deadline for the PNRR SME digitalization grants?⌄

The payment claim under the C9 I3.1 call can be filed until 30 October 2026, under Ordinul MIPE 963/2026 — a deadline separate from implementation, concerning strictly the disbursement stage.

My vendor on the PNRR digitalization project is late. What can Northdan Soft take over?⌄

As a software vendor, we pick up the remaining development on a C9 I3.1 project — website, e-commerce, ERP/CRM — deliver in short stages and hand over the acceptance documents the payment file requires. The fastest way to reach us is WhatsApp.

Which types of software were eligible under the SME digitalization call?⌄

The C9 I3.1 guide accepted websites and e-commerce, ERP/CRM, software licences, cloud, RPA, cybersecurity, electronic signature and training — the exact list and conditions remain those in the consolidated official guide published by MIPE.
