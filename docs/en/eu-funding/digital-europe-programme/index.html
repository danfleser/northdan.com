The programme's official applicant guide
ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

Romanian companies take part in the Digital Europe Programme (DIGITAL) by applying on the EU Funding and Tenders portal, alone where a topic allows it (as for the SME cybersecurity topic CYBER-11-AI4SME, open until 14 January 2027, 17:00 CET, at 75% for SMEs) or in multinational consortia, and a Romanian partner in a winning project can then ask UEFISCDI for national co-financing, provided it holds the required national authorisation letter. Let us be direct about what it is not: a source of simple grants for digitalising an individual company, since the national calls exist for that. DIGITAL is the instrument through which the European Union builds strategic digital capacities, with a total budget of over EUR 8.1 billion for 2021-2027 (Regulation (EU) 2021/694, art. 9(1), originally set EUR 7,588 million in current prices, hence the 7.5 and 7.588 figures on Romanian government pages), managed directly by the European Commission rather than through national authorities. Its capacity areas are supercomputing (HPC), artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, advanced digital skills, the broad deployment of digital technologies, and, added in September 2023 through the EU Chips Act, semiconductors. The 2026 round closed on 1 October 2026, including DIGITAL-2026-SKILLS-10 and DIGITAL-2026-BESTUSE-10; open today, 18 November 2026, are the ECCC cybersecurity calls DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11 (until 14 January 2027) and CYBER-12 (until 16 March 2027). For most firms the most accessible touchpoint remains the EDIH network: Romania's seven hubs, financed half from Digital Europe and half from POCIDIF. Northdan Soft enters the picture as a technical implementation partner: we develop the software component of projects; we do not write funding applications.

If your company joins a multinational consortium on a DIGITAL call, Northdan Soft can deliver the software component: development, AI integration, cybersecurity.

Open now: the ECCC cybersecurity calls

On the EU Funding and Tenders portal, call DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11, "Strengthening European Cybersecurity Technologies, Capacities and Preparedness", has been open since 1 September 2026, with a deadline of 14 January 2027, 17:00 CET, and a single-stage submission. Its seven topics are REGCABH, COORDPREP, DUALUSE, EULEG, AI4SME, NCC and CYBERAI, EUR 96 million in total. Topic budgets: EUR 5 million for REGCABH, 10 for DUALUSE, 15 for COORDPREP and CYBERAI, 11 for NCC, 20 for AI4SME and 20 for EULEG.

CYBER-11-AI4SME ("Strengthening cybersecurity capacities of European SMEs with cybersecure AI-powered solutions") is a "DIGITAL JU SME Support Actions" action: 5 expected grants, with an EU contribution of EUR 3 to 5 million each, funded at 75% for SMEs and 50% for other entities. The stated objective is market uptake of AI-powered cybersecurity solutions, notably in SMEs. Applicants must be legal entities established in the EU or EEA and not controlled from outside them (Article 12(5) security restrictions, section 6 of the call document).

Separately, two CYBER-12 topics (DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-12-NATCH and -CBCH), for national and cross-border cyber hubs, are open from 15 October 2026 to 16 March 2027. They are not a funding line for an individual company; check eligibility in the call document.

National co-financing from UEFISCDI

A Romanian partner in a winning DIGITAL project can ask UEFISCDI for national co-financing under PNCDI IV, Programme 5.8, Subprogramme 5.8.2. The rules are in the UEFISCDI information pack of 8 July 2026: Romanian research organisations and enterprises that are partners in a winning project can apply, and enterprises must have R&D in their object of activity. A partner with a EUR 0 budget in the Grant Agreement cannot apply. The pack also requires a letter of authorisation/financial support for national co-financing from the state research authority for the Romanian participants; request it early.

The national money is at most equal to the EU grant, and total funding cannot exceed 100% of eligible costs. There is no scientific re-evaluation, only an eligibility check, and subcontracting is not eligible. Submission is continuous on uefiscdi-direct.ro and projects are accepted in order of receipt within the budget. The 2026 budget is RON 1,000,000, which is small: check what remains before you count on it.

What DIGITAL funds, who manages it and who to contact in Romania

The Digital Europe Programme is managed directly by the European Commission; unlike POCIDIF or the regional programmes, there is no national managing authority. HaDEA, its executive agency, runs the actions on advanced digital skills, accelerating best use of technologies, cloud, data and artificial intelligence, while the cybersecurity calls are run by the ECCC, which has its seat in Bucharest. The budget exceeds EUR 8.1 billion for 2021-2027, according to the Commission's official page.

Romania's national contact point is the Authority for the Digitalisation of Romania (ADR). For cyber topics, the AI4SME topic page sends applicants first to the National Coordination Centre (NCC) of their country, NCC-RO in Romania. The money goes to European digital capacities: supercomputing, AI, cybersecurity, advanced digital skills and wide technology use, plus semiconductors since September 2023.

How a Romanian company concretely participates

Participation runs through the EU Funding and Tenders portal, where the calls are published. The general rule is a multinational consortium, alongside universities, institutes or companies from other member states. The exception: for all CYBER-11 topics except REGCABH (which needs at least 2 applicants from 2 countries) the call document sets no minimum consortium composition, so a single applicant is allowed (CYBER-11 call document v1.0, 1 September 2026, section 6). Other calls set their own rules.

Funding rates depend on the action type. In CYBER-11, five topics are Simple Grants at 50%, REGCABH is exceptionally 70%, and AI4SME (an SME Support Action) is 75% for SMEs and 50% for others. Annex 1 of the call document also lists Coordination and Support Actions at 100% as a general DIGITAL action type, but no CYBER-11 topic uses it. Check the fiche of the topic you target.

Some DIGITAL projects redistribute money through cascade (FSTP) calls, listed separately on the portal and on oportunitati-ue.gov.ro. Those open on 18 November 2026 target incubators, students and scholarships, not the digitalisation of a firm.

The 2026 round is closed: DIGITAL-2026-SKILLS-10 and DIGITAL-2026-BESTUSE-10 were published on 10 April 2026, opened on 21 April 2026 and closed on 1 October 2026, with topic budgets from EUR 14.4 million for the European Health Data Space down to EUR 2 million for the national coalitions. For the other calls the Commission listed on 30 July 2026, read each fiche on the portal.

EDIH: the programme's accessible face for SMEs

For most Romanian SMEs, the realistic contact with Digital Europe is not a consortium project but the EDIH network. The 7 Romanian hubs are DIH4Society and TDIH (North-West), FIT EDIH (Centre), WeH (South-Muntenia), CiTyInnoHub (South-East), eDIH-DIZ (North-East) and DIGIVEST (West). They are financed 50% from DIGITAL and 50% from POCIDIF, under an action with an indicative budget of EUR 35 million, and deliver services to companies (technology testing, training, orientation towards funding), not cash grants.

On the POCIDIF calls that fund the network, the eligible applicant is the hub, never the company receiving the services. Northdan Soft is not an EDIH and does not offer funds-access consultancy: we are the software vendor that turns the recommendations of an EDIH journey or the specifications of a DIGITAL project into delivered implementation, with a technical offer and an eligible-cost budget.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get a Digital Europe grant to digitalise my company?

Realistically, no. DIGITAL funds European digital capacities through projects usually run by multinational consortia, not the individual digitalisation of one firm. For your own digitalisation, look at the national and regional calls or at the services of the EDIH hubs.

What share of costs does a DIGITAL call cover?

It depends on the action type. In the CYBER-11 call, five topics are Simple Grants at 50%, REGCABH is exceptionally 70%, and AI4SME (an SME Support Action) is 75% for SMEs and 50% for others. Check the fiche of your topic before building a budget.

Can I apply to a Digital Europe call without a consortium?

In CYBER-11 only REGCABH requires a consortium (at least 2 applicants from 2 countries); for the other six topics, including AI4SME, the call document (section 6) sets no minimum composition, so a single applicant can apply. Other DIGITAL calls set their own rules in their fiche.

Is there Romanian national co-financing for Digital Europe projects?

Yes. UEFISCDI, under Subprogramme 5.8.2 of PNCDI IV, can give a Romanian partner national money of up to the EU grant amount, at most 100% of eligible costs. Submission is continuous, the 2026 budget is RON 1,000,000, and the pack requires a national authorisation letter from the state research authority; request it early.

Where do I find the open Digital Europe calls?

On the European Commission's EU Funding and Tenders portal. The 2026 round, including DIGITAL-2026-SKILLS-10 and DIGITAL-2026-BESTUSE-10, closed on 1 October 2026. Open as of 18 November 2026 are the ECCC cybersecurity calls: CYBER-11, including AI4SME for SMEs, until 14 January 2027, and CYBER-12 until 16 March 2027.

How is Digital Europe connected to Romania's EDIH hubs?

The EDIH network is the programme's own creation: the 7 Romanian hubs are co-financed 50% from Digital Europe and 50% from POCIDIF. They are the local entry gate: a company receives subsidised services there without taking part in a European call.
