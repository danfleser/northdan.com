Entrepreneur in Oltenia: the region closed its calls — your company has not closed its options

Oltenia spent its regional digitalization money early. This guide walks through the three doors still open — one of which only two counties can use.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

The digitalization funding map for a company in Craiova, Slatina or Râmnicu Vâlcea reads quickly, but it deserves a correct reading. The regional door is closed: the SME call of the Sud-Vest Oltenia Regional Programme, with its €21.1 million budget, ended in August 2024, its projects are being implemented, and no new round has been announced — if your company is among the winners, go straight to the implementation side of this site; the rest of this guide is not about you. The national door is open, selectively: POCIDIF 2.1 (the Smart Growth, Digitalization and Financial Instruments Programme) for companies with technology CAEN codes, until 30 September 2026 with large grants; the MEDAT sessions for manufacturing, trade and women's entrepreneurship; Start-Up Nation for young companies — each with a fact sheet and verified status on this site. The special door opens only for Dolj and Gorj: the Programul Tranziție Justă (Just Transition Programme), with budgets dedicated to the economic reconversion of the two counties — company investments, including ones with substantial digital components, in a competition restricted to the eligible counties rather than a national one. It is the zone's most underrated competitive advantage. Whichever door fits, our part stays the same: analysis of the company's processes, a software budget breakdown built on the relevant guide's structure and, at implementation, delivery with the reimbursement documentation kept current. The first written exchange — free — establishes which door matches your profile.

Frequently asked questions

Transport company in Craiova: what digitalization funding is realistically within reach?⌄

It depends on the investment: for fleet management and logistics systems, the MEDAT sessions and any PTJ calls (Dolj is eligible) are the routes to follow; POCIDIF does not apply without a technology CAEN code. Send us your profile and the investment you have in mind — we reply with a shortlist of matching programmes and prepare the technical side.

What kind of digital projects does the Just Transition Programme fund for companies in Gorj?⌄

PTJ funds productive investments that create jobs in the transition counties — with the digital component (automation, production systems, digitalized workflows) entering as part of the investment. The concrete calls and their conditions are managed at national level; our PTJ pages keep the calendar verified.

Is there any point hoping for a new regional round in Oltenia?⌄

Hope is not a plan: the priority's budget was committed in the 2024 calls, and ADR Sud-Vest Oltenia had announced nothing new at our last verification. If that changes we update these pages on our monthly verification cycle — but your company's strategy should be built on windows that exist, not on wished-for ones.
