Funding for the digitalization of professional services firms

In a professional practice, the billable hour lost hunting for documents in old e-mails is the most expensive hour in the firm — and no report ever shows it.

Accountants, lawyers and consultants sell time and expertise, yet a surprisingly large slice of the day goes somewhere else: chasing missing documents from clients, drafting the same letter with different figures for the tenth time, hunting for the final version of a contract through e-mail attachments. Digitalizing a practice targets precisely those leaks — automatic generation of repetitive documents from templates and structured data, a portal where the client uploads their own paperwork and follows the status of their case, an electronic archive where any file surfaces in seconds with full version history. On funding, professional services sit in an unusual position, and the obstacle is how the calls are built rather than the budget: one ties the right to apply to the applicant's authorised CAEN activity code, the other to a credit-backed investment. POCIDIF Action 2.1 accepts nine codes, all from electronic components manufacturing and IT services. The Swiss SME Eco-Tech scheme dropped its manufacturing-only condition on 7 July 2026 and now admits trade, services and micro-enterprises, but it still finances an investment: a project starts at RON 668,100 in eligible costs, at least 60% has to be borrowed from a partner bank, at least 40% of eligible costs must go to green equipment, and business software is eligible only up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT (cost terms checked on 30 July 2026). National statuses were rechecked on 18 November 2026; no national call finances the digitalization of a practice. What remains are the regional programmes, each with its own CAEN rules (Centru uses a list of excluded codes), and the EDIH network of European Digital Innovation Hubs, which contracts advice and testing services rather than paying a grant. In the Centru Region, PR Centru Action 2.2 call 2 takes submissions in MySMIS2021 from 2 December 2026 to 2 February 2027, with grants of EUR 15,000–200,000 for accountancy and architecture firms organised as companies under Law 31/1990 (guide 4.3, 5.4, 5.1.2; checked on 18 November 2026). Northdan builds the platform and the technical documentation as a software vendor; the application file stays with you and your consultant.

What the funding landscape looks like for practices and consultancies

The useful truth, stated plainly: the two active national schemes exclude a practice for two different reasons. POCIDIF Action 2.1 decides eligibility on the authorised CAEN code and rejects an applicant without it at contracting stage. SME Eco-Tech no longer restricts itself to manufacturing — trade and services became eligible on 7 July 2026 — but it caps eligible business software at RON 15,000 excluding VAT and requires a partner-bank investment loan plus a green-equipment share of at least 40% of eligible costs, so a practice's platform cannot be its subject. That is why the realistic plan has two threads: the subsidised EDIH services — digital assessment and technology testing, contracted rather than paid out — and the regional calls, each with its own list of codes.

When a regional call opens, its guide establishes who is eligible and which costs qualify; a practice that has already analysed its processes will be able to move fast, but the budget itself has to be rewritten onto that call's cost categories.

In the Centru Region: Action 2.2, submissions 02.12.2026–02.02.2027

An accountancy (CAEN 6920), architecture (7111) or engineering (7112) firm organised as a company under Law 31/1990 or a cooperative under Law 1/2005, with its activity in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș or Sibiu, can receive EUR 15,000–200,000, at most 90% of eligible value, as de minimis aid, within the EUR 300,000 ceiling per single undertaking over 3 years, which counts de minimis aid already received (guide 5.1.2, 3.5, 5.4, 3.4, 3.13). Submissions run in MySMIS2021 from 2 December 2026, 12:00, to 2 February 2027, 12:00 (guide 4.3). Codes 6910, 6920, 7111 and 7112 are not in Annex 3. The implementation place is the main office or a work point at the trade registry with at least one fiscal year of activity (guide 5.7.4); net or operating profit above zero in the previous year and one employee are also required (guide 5.1.1.6, 5.1.1.7). For submissions in January 2027 the previous year is 2026; the guide gives no example for that window and does not say what counts when the 2026 accounts are not yet approved, so get written confirmation from the ADR Centru helpdesk before building the file (guide 5.1.1.6). Checked on 18 November 2026 against the guide and annexes.

For lawyers the obstacle in Centru is the legal form, not the CAEN code: the guide accepts only companies under Law 31/1990 and cooperatives, and sole traders are not listed (guide 5.1.2, 5.1). In our reading, a sole law office, associated offices and professional law partnerships — like a notary office, an accountant working as a natural person or a sole architecture office — do not fit; for written confirmation, ask the ADR Centru helpdesk. An accountancy or architecture SRL is the case the call covers.

The CAEN test covers the whole company: no excluded code authorised at submission, at any location, and no revenue from excluded activities in the current year or the last fiscal year (guide 5.1.1.1, Annex 5). An accounting firm with IT codes is out: 6209 (Annex 3, row 20), 6311 (row 21), 6202 (18), 4741 (9), 4651 (7). Check the ONRC certificate first.

Intangible assets are mandatory; hardware is optional and only if it serves the solution (guide 3.6, 5.2.1, 5.3.2.2). Turning paper archives into indexable digital files is its own line (guide 5.3.2.7); BIM or CAD on subscription is eligible only for implementation, then paid from own funds during the 3-year durability period (guide 5.3.2.5). Under criterion 1.4 clients count as users: over 10 unique annual users earn 10 points, so a client portal reaches it (Annex 4). The initial audit (Annex 13) comes from an independent entity with CAEN 6202/6220 or an EDIH (guide 5.3.2.11). On one project Northdan is the auditor, or the supplier, or the plan author, never two (guide 5.3.2.8); we do not write the funding application and do not promise approval.

What we actually implement in a professional practice

The core trio: the template engine that generates recurring documents, the client portal with document upload and status tracking, and the indexed electronic archive with full-text search. On top of it, depending on the practice: time tracking per case and time billing, deadlines and notifications, e-mail integration and electronic signature.

Each component goes into the budget with a price and a verifiable result, and delivery happens in stages, with an acceptance report and role-based training — the case administrator learns different flows from the partner who approves. The delivery documentation later becomes payment-claim material, in the form guides typically require.

Frequently asked questions

Are there funds dedicated to digitalizing accountancy or law practices?

No national call finances it (statuses rechecked on 18 November 2026; SME Eco-Tech cost terms on 30 July 2026): POCIDIF Action 2.1 is restricted to nine ICT codes, and SME Eco-Tech caps eligible business software at RON 15,000 excluding VAT on top of a mandatory bank loan. In the Centru Region, Action 2.2 call 2 takes submissions from 2 December 2026 to 2 February 2027, with grants of EUR 15,000–200,000, but only for companies under Law 31/1990 or cooperatives (guide 4.3, 5.4, 5.1.2). Code 6910 is not excluded; in our reading a law practice has no eligible legal form, so ask the ADR Centru helpdesk.

Which software solutions suit funding in a consulting firm?

Document automation from templates, the client portal with document submission and case status, indexed electronic archiving, time tracking per case and time billing — all typically classifiable as digitalization software, per the guide of the call that opens.

What exactly does an EDIH give a professional practice?

Contracted services, not money: an assessment of the practice's digital maturity, guidance on choosing solutions, testing before purchase. Legally it is non-financial state aid granted under a contract, and the applicant on the calls that fund the hub is the hub itself, never the firm.

How is a client portal justified in a project budget?

Through its operational functions: client accounts, document upload, approval flows, status notifications — each with its own price. At handover it is demonstrated on a real case, and the acceptance report records the verified functionality.

Can a sole law office apply to the Centru Region digitalization call 2.2?

No, in our reading of the guide. Call 2.2 accepts only companies registered under Law 31/1990 and cooperatives (section 5.1.2), and a sole law office, associated offices and professional law partnerships are not such companies. Code 6910 is not in Annex 3; the obstacle is the legal form. For written confirmation, ask the ADR Centru helpdesk.

Can an accounting firm in Brașov or Sibiu receive up to EUR 200,000 for digitalization?

Yes, if it meets, among other things, these conditions: an SME and a company under Law 31/1990, at least one full fiscal year of activity, net or operating profit above zero in the previous fiscal year, at least one employee and no excluded CAEN code authorised, such as 6209 or 6311. The grant is EUR 15,000–200,000, at most 90% of eligible value and within the EUR 300,000 de minimis ceiling over 3 years, submitted 2 December 2026 to 2 February 2027 For January 2027 submissions the previous year is 2026 and the guide does not say what counts if the 2026 accounts are not yet approved: ask the ADR Centru helpdesk for written confirmation. (guide 5.1.2, 5.1.1.6, 5.1.1.7, 5.1.1.1, 5.4, 4.3).
