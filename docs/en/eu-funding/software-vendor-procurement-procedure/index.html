How a private beneficiary awards the software contract under Order 1284/2016

Quotes collected at submission justify the budget; the software contract is awarded only after the financing contract is signed, under Order 1284/2016 — and mixing the two produces financial corrections.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

A private beneficiary with a 2021-2027 cohesion-funds project, such as call 2 of Action 2.2 in the Centru Regional Programme (a call for non-ICT SMEs implementing in the Centru Region, submission 2 December 2026 – 2 February 2027), awards the software contract under Order no. 1284/2016, amended by MIPE Order no. 1354/2023 and MIPE Order no. 1143/2026: direct purchase below 270,120 lei excluding VAT (art. 7(5) of Law 98/2016) and a competitive procedure, with a notice in the Achiziții Beneficiari Privați application, at or above that threshold. The application was unavailable from 15 July 2026 after a cyberattack and has been usable again since 18 August 2026; for the outage period the order's deadlines are extended (art. 6(2), introduced by Order 1143/2026).

The call 2 financing contract requires the file of each purchase to be uploaded in MySMIS2021 within 10 working days of signing the purchase contract (Annex 15, para. (19)), but the guide names neither the order nor a threshold; we read the order as applying because PR Centru is funded from ERDF 2021-2027 (art. 5(1)(d)), and you confirm it at the ADR Centru helpdesk. One more condition is rarely read, the incentive effect: expenses for starting the investment before the application is submitted are ineligible (guide 5.3.3), and starting means the first legally binding order commitment (Glossary, item 11). Exceptions: project consultancy, including the digitalisation plan, and the initial digital maturity audit, no earlier than 1 January 2021. Checked on 18 November 2026 on legislatie.just.ro (consolidated form of 17 July 2026) and in the call 2 guide. Northdan Soft takes part in such procedures as an ordinary bidder, including the risk of losing; we do not write the financing application, do not draft the request for quotation we bid on and do not promise approval.

How we help

An offer mapped onto your specification

We answer point by point to the requirements in the request for quotation, in their order, with a price per chapter — evaluation becomes a direct comparison, not an interpretation.

Documents ready for the procurement file

The offer comes with identification details, validity, deadlines and full terms — the pieces the procedure file has to contain at any verification.

A draft contract aligned with the offer

After award we supply the draft contract with the same content as the winning offer — no new clauses slipped in between selection and signature, the classic source of findings.

Two moments, two sets of rules

The first moment, at submission, serves to justify the budget. The call 2 guide asks for a cost-justification table whose supporting documents may be 3 price quotes and unit prices from verifiable, objective sources (guide 7.4, item 7). That is a budget justification, not an award.

The second moment, after contracting, is the award. Below the threshold of art. 7(5) of Law 98/2016 the purchase is direct, against supporting documents, with no publication; at or above it the competitive procedure applies, with a notice in the application, a technical specification, evaluation and a file. Supply contracts stay under this order and do not move to Law 98/2016, however large the value; below 270,120 lei it is still a direct purchase. The offer deadline is at least 6 calendar days for supply and 10 for services and works, counted from publication of the notice.

The threshold is 270,120 lei excluding VAT for products and services and 900,400 lei for works, read in Law 98/2016 as in force on the purchase date because it is revised. The estimated value may not be split into smaller contracts to avoid the procedure (item 1.4 of the procedure; the sanction is a 25% correction). Modules of the same software system add up — that is how we read the rule, so calculate the value on the whole system.

What changed in 2023 and 2026

MIPE Order no. 1354 of 30 March 2023 added the 2021-2027 cohesion funds (ERDF, ESF+, Cohesion Fund, EMFAF, JTF) to the order's scope. Before any direct purchase the beneficiary prepares a note determining the estimated value, from market research: name, quantity, unit price, total and price source. The estimated value starts from the funding application and is updated before purchase. An award result published later than 5 calendar days after signing carries a 3% correction.

MIPE Order no. 1143 of 16 July 2026 added the extension of deadlines when the application is down for reasons not attributable to beneficiaries, based on the ministry's notices. The application went offline on 15 July 2026 and reopened on 18 August 2026, moved to the Government Cloud. Funds with their own manual, such as PS PAC 2023-2027 at AFIR, are excluded by art. 5(2): the AFIR manual applies there, not this order.

The incentive effect, in plain words

In call 2, expenses for starting investments before the application is submitted are ineligible (guide 5.3.3). The Glossary defines the start as the first legally binding order commitment. The exceptions in 5.3.1 are project consultancy, including the digitalisation plan, and the initial digital maturity audit, provided they are not earlier than 1 January 2021.

Asking for price quotes for the budget is not starting a procurement — the guide itself allows quotes at submission. The difference is what you do with them: a price justification stays a justification, while a published notice, an invitation to bid or a firm order moves things into procedure territory. When in doubt, ask the managing authority first, not after.

What makes quotes comparable

A usable request for quotation lists the numbered requirements of the technical specification, the estimated volumes, the desired deadline and the expected price format, and makes the breakdown by component, staged deadlines and warranty terms mandatory elements. Offers that lack them can be declared non-compliant.

Without that discipline you receive three documents in three languages — one by modules, one by developer days, one with a single global price — and a choice nobody can justify on paper. Comparability is not obtained at evaluation but in the request for quotation.

What stays in the file, and for how long

For the competitive procedure the file keeps the request for quotation sent, proof of publication or the invitations, the offers received intact, the completed evaluation grid, the selection report and the communications to bidders, with the signed contract on top, identical to the winning offer. For a direct purchase the file is shorter but does not vanish: the estimated-value note with the market offers attached, order or contract, invoice, payment order, bank statement, receipt note.

Call 2 deadlines (Annex 14): the file for purchases made before the financing contract goes into MySMIS2021 within 1 month of its signing, and the file for asset purchases within 3 months of signing. Separately, each purchase contract or addendum signed during implementation goes into MySMIS2021 within 10 working days (Annex 15, para. (19)). Keep the file until the programme is officially closed or durability ends, whichever comes later, and for at least 5 years from 31 December of the year of the last payment (Annex 15, para. (9)). Durability is 3 years from the final payment (guide 5.1.1.4; Annex 15, art. 1), and the guide, also at 5.1.1.4, additionally requires keeping the investment in the region for at least 3 years from project completion — confirm the starting date with ADR Centru. Records of aid received are kept for at least 10 years (Annex 15, para. (10)).

What we do not do in your procedure

We do not draft the request for quotation for a procedure we bid on and do not propose requirements cut to our product: a specification visibly oriented to one supplier taints the selection, and the finding hits the beneficiary, not the bidder. We do not issue courtesy offers to fill a file and do not take over your consultant's role. Call 2 adds two rules: intangible assets are bought from third parties unrelated to the buyer (5.3.2.1), and the consultant who wrote the digitalisation plan cannot supply software in the same project (guide, digitalisation plan section).

And the point other suppliers leave unclear: a quote issued before submission, to justify the budget, does not reserve the contract. The procedure after contracting is real, and if someone else answers your specification better, the contract goes there. We prefer to say so before, not after.

Frequently asked questions

I am a private company — do I really need a competitive procedure for the project software?⌄

Yes, from 270,120 lei excluding VAT upward for products and services; below it the purchase is direct, with an estimated-value note. Order 1284/2016 covers 2021-2027 cohesion funds through MIPE Order no. 1354/2023 and was amended by Order 1143/2026. Concrete obligations sit in your financing contract.

How many quotes do I need: three, as at submission?⌄

The three quotes at submission justify the budget (guide 7.4, item 7); they do not award the contract. The award follows Order 1284/2016 and the financing contract: a notice in the application, at least 6 or 10 calendar days, evaluation. The number of offers is not taken from the budget table.

What do I do if the Achiziții Beneficiari Privați application is down?⌄

Deadlines are extended by the outage period when the cause is not attributable to you (art. 6(2), Order 1143/2026), based on notices on the ministry's site. The application was offline from 15 July to 18 August 2026, when it reopened.

Can I ask for price quotes before submission without losing the incentive effect?⌄

Yes: the call 2 guide itself asks for cost justification documents at submission. What must not start before is the investment, meaning the first legally binding order commitment (guide 5.3.3, Glossary item 11), except project consultancy and the initial audit (5.3.1).

Can the supplier help write the specification without compromising the procedure?⌄

It can contribute to the functional part — what the system has to do — but not to requirements that describe one bidder's product. If only one firm can meet the specification, the procedure becomes open to challenge, and the risk stays with the beneficiary.

How long must I keep the procurement file?⌄

Upload it into MySMIS2021 within 10 working days of the purchase contract. Keep it until the programme is officially closed or the durability period ends, whichever comes later, and for at least 5 years from 31 December of the year of the last payment (Annex 15, para. (9)). Records of aid are kept at least 10 years (Annex 15, para. (10)).
