Custom software paid by a grant: what call 2 of the Centru Region requires

The only budget line with no catalogue price, so the only one defended purely by specification — and in call 2 it must be bought from a third party, not built by your own staff.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

Custom-developed software is an eligible cost today in call 2 of Action 2.2 under the Centru Regional Programme 2021-2027, which funds as an intangible asset "software purchased or developed/adapted to order" (guide 5.3.2.1): a grant of 15,000–200,000 € for non-ICT SMEs with an implementation site in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș or Sibiu, with filing between 02.12.2026, 12:00, and 02.02.2027, 12:00 (guide 4.3.1, 4.3.2, 5.4). The software is bought "on market terms from third parties with no links to the buyer" and used exclusively by the beneficiary, and the work of your own employees is not reimbursed: personnel costs are ineligible (5.3.3). A consultant who writes the plan cannot supply software in the same project (5.3.2.8), and the auditor must be distinct from the suppliers (5.3.2.11). Checked on 18 November 2026 against the call 2 guide and its annexes.

Northdan Soft is the software supplier: we do not write the funding application or the digitalisation plan, we do not run the maturity audit in projects where we deliver, we do not assess a company's eligibility, we do not promise approval and we do not issue courtesy quotes. The 20% own-staff rule belonged to the POCIDIF 2.1 call, closed on 30 October 2026, and does not apply in call 2.

How we help

A specification that removes interpretation

Modules, screens, integrations and scope boundaries written down in black and white — the basis on which evaluation, acceptance and reimbursement rest without argument.

Effort estimated per component, not globally

Every module has its own person-days and cost; if clarifications come, we can defend each figure with the work structure behind it.

A quote issued in the form the application needs

A signed, dated document with an identifiable issuing company, attachable to the cost-justification summary as one of the 3 price sources (guide 7.4 item 7; Annex 4, criterion 2.3 a).

What call 2 funds in custom-built software

Guide section 5.3.2.1 a) lists e-invoicing, digitalised ordering circuits, RPA, CRM, PLM, ERP, financial, supplier, HR and logistics applications, databases and migration, IoT, VR/AR, AI, blockchain and chatbots. Item b) covers the website and online shop, item e) "other types of software … created to order", and item f) installation, configuration, commissioning and testing. The idea that regional calls fund only ready-made software does not hold here: the guide explicitly names software "developed/adapted to order".

Three conditions change how it is bought: the software comes from a third party with no links to the buyer, is used exclusively by the beneficiary, and stays in its assets, associated with the project, for at least 3 years after completion (5.3.2.1). SaaS or cloud subscriptions are reimbursed only for the implementation period, then paid from own funds (5.3.2.5). Maintenance is an operating and upkeep cost, so ineligible (5.3.3). And the order cannot be placed before filing: expenses for starting the investment before the application is submitted are ineligible (5.3.3).

How to justify a line with no catalogue price

Budget values must respect price reasonableness under OUG 66/2011, backed by offers and unit prices from verifiable, objective sources (guide 7.3). The cost-justification summary says the documents can be "3 price offers and unit prices" (7.4 item 7), and the evaluation grid gives up to 2 points when costs are substantiated by at least 3 sources (Annex 4, criterion 2.3 a). Three comparable quotes on custom work exist only if the specification is precise enough for three suppliers to price the same scope.

That is why the specification is written before the budget. Person-days follow from it, and the figure follows from person-days.

Who writes the plan, who delivers, who audits

Call 2 requires a digital maturity audit before filing and another at the end, submitted with the last payment claim, and the final report certifies the progress made (5.3.2.11). The auditor is external and independent: distinct from the beneficiary and from the suppliers of the audited products or services, a legal entity authorised under CAEN 6202 (Rev. 2) or 6220 (Rev. 3), or a European Digital Innovation Hub (EDIH).

The same logic applies to the digitalisation plan: if a consultant writes it, that consultant cannot deliver software or draft the audit report in the same project (5.3.2.8). So the supplier who wrote the code cannot audit it, and the audit remains a separate line with a third party.

Ownership of the code and requirements that appear along the way

The supply contract must state clearly who owns the source code and what usage rights the beneficiary receives. The software must be registrable in the company's assets and stay associated with the project for at least 3 years after completion (5.3.2.1), and the guide asks for durability of 3 years from the final payment (5.1.1.4), so confirm the exact term with ADR Centru.

The funded scope stays the one in the dossier. Major changes require a notification or an addendum to the financing contract. New requirements are separate extensions, paid outside the grant.

The 20% own-staff rule: a closed call

The guide for Action 2.1 of POCIDIF, approved by Order MIPE no. 965/23.06.2026, prohibited fully outsourcing the technical activity: at least 20% of the aid allocated to the core activity went to the beneficiary's own staff, with a technical coordinator and two technical experts. It closed on 30 October 2026, 17:00 (Order MIPE no. 1500/29.09.2026).

Call 2 reverses the situation: personnel costs are ineligible (5.3.3), and on simplified cost options, direct and indirect, the guide says "Not applicable" (5.3.4).

Custom development in programmes that do not fund innovation

SME Eco-Tech, open until 30 December 2026, 20:00, caps software needed for the activity at 15,000 lei excluding VAT and declares ineligible assembly, labour, commissioning and staff training — exactly the costs that make up implementation for a software component. Access to the scheme requires a project of at least 668,100 lei in eligible costs and an investment loan of at least 400,860 lei from a partner bank, so custom development cannot be the core of an application here.

The PNRR C9 I3.1 call, with the 20,000–100,000 euro band that still circulates online, has been closed to applications since 2023; beneficiaries under contract had until 30 October 2026 for payment claims, a deadline that has passed.

Indicative figures and what we do not do

We publish indicative bands; the firm figure comes from a written quote after analysis. A small automation falls in 3,000–10,000 €, a business application in 15,000–60,000 €, a complex platform starts from 60,000 €. For web applications: 5,000–15,000 € simple, 15,000–50,000 € with integrations and reports, over 50,000 € for large volumes. The price moves with each connected system, the number of roles and approval flows, and the migration of old data, typically 10–20% of the project.

We do not run the maturity audit in projects where we deliver software, because the guide requires an auditor distinct from the supplier.

Frequently asked questions

Is custom software development an eligible cost in PR Centru call 2?⌄

Yes, as an intangible asset under 5.3.2.1 a) and e) of the guide, if the software comes from a third party with no links to the buyer. The call is for non-ICT SMEs with an implementation site in the Centru Region, with filing between 02.12.2026 and 02.02.2027, 12:00.

How many price quotes does a one-off development need?⌄

Three, in PR Centru call 2: the cost-justification summary asks for "3 price offers and unit prices" (guide 7.4 item 7), and the grid awards 2 points for at least 3 sources (Annex 4, 2.3 a). For a one-off, the specification must be precise enough for three suppliers to price the same scope.

Can I build the application with my own employees and claim their salaries?⌄

Not in call 2: personnel costs are ineligible, except the unit cost for training (guide 5.3.3). The software is bought from a third party with no links to the buyer (5.3.2.1), so internal work stays at the company's own expense.

Can the software supplier also write the digitalisation plan or the maturity audit?⌄

No, not in the same project of PR Centru call 2. The consultant who writes the plan cannot deliver software or draft the maturity audit (5.3.2.8), and the auditor must be distinct from the suppliers of the audited products (5.3.2.11). These are three separate roles.

Who owns code developed with grant money?⌄

Whoever the supply contract says, which is why the clause is negotiated before filing. The beneficiary needs rights sufficient to register the software in its assets and keep it associated with the project for at least 3 years after completion (PR Centru call 2 guide, 5.3.2.1).

Can my IT company get funds to develop its own software?⌄

Not through PR Centru call 2: Annex 3 excludes CAEN codes 6201, 6202, 6209 and 5829, among others. POCIDIF 2.1, the route for ICT firms, closed on 30.10.2026. The full picture is on the page dedicated to IT firms.
