When does the Comerț programme 2026 open, and what is known on 18 November

On 18 November 2026 the Comerț programme had money in the draft MEDAT budget, but no published procedure, calendar or application form.

On 18 November 2026, the Comerț programme (the programme for developing the sale of market products and services) has no open session and no implementation procedure for 2026, although the draft 2026 MEDAT budget listed 50 million lei in commitment appropriations and 3 million lei in budget appropriations for it (annex 3/35, Programme 8, March 2026), and the State Budget Law no. 43/2026 was adopted on 27 March 2026. The two facts are not yet tied to a date: a procedure is approved by ministerial order, then the registration window is announced, and neither step has been made public.

The last real edition, in 2023, closed registration on 4 January 2024 with 2,615 firms and ran on a clear rule: a grant of at most 50% of eligible costs, up to 500,000 lei, alongside an investment bank credit of at least 250,000 lei (Order 603/2023, point 5.4). A new edition cannot be assumed to repeat the formula. Northdan Soft builds a retailer's online store and stock management and prepares the technical documents of the dossier; it does not write the funding application and does not promise approval. Verified on 18 November 2026 on the programme page at imm.gov.ro, on economie.gov.ro and in the draft MEDAT budget annex (March 2026).

How we help

A dated status, with its source

The budget was proposed; the procedure and the session do not exist. Every claim on the page carries a verification date and an official document behind it.

The 2023 rules, cited by section

Grant of at most 50% and 500,000 lei, credit of at least 250,000 lei (point 5.4), firm age and CAEN code. A benchmark for planning, not a promise for 2026.

The relevant precedent is 2023

The grant was a capital premium on a bank credit, so the bank reviews the file first. Older editions with a pure grant are no longer a benchmark.

A digital component sized for sales

An online store tied to real stock, stock management and till integration: a chain that produces sales whether or not a session opens.

Status on 18 November 2026: budget proposed, procedure no

State Budget Law no. 43/2026 was adopted on 27 March 2026 (Official Gazette no. 242/27.03.2026). In the MEDAT annex 3/35, Programme 8 (code 518) shows 50,000 thousand lei in commitment appropriations and 3,000 thousand lei in budget appropriations for 2026, estimates of 81,000 thousand lei a year for 2027–2029 and a target of 200 beneficiaries in 2026–2027, 100 a year. Commitment appropriations are the sum up to which the ministry may take on obligations; budget appropriations are the money that can actually be paid in that year. The annex we read is the version sent to Parliament (senat.ro, March 2026), not the one published in the Official Gazette. In October 2026 the Finance Ministry published a draft budget rectification that also changes the MEDAT budget; the rectified figure for Programme 8 was not verified, so the 50 million may have moved.

Our reading, not an official statement: with only 3 million lei payable in 2026, a new edition would pay mostly from 2027. On the programme page at imm.gov.ro the newest item dates from 13.06.2025, economie.gov.ro shows nothing about 2026, and the procedure is not in public consultation either (checked 18.11.2026).

The order of steps stays as in 2023: first the implementation procedure, approved by ministerial order, with ceilings, eligible costs and criteria, then the announcement of the registration window. Until the first step, any per-beneficiary amount for 2026 is speculation.

What happened after the 2023 session

Registration closed on 4 January 2024 at 20:00 with 2,615 applicant firms (MEAT release of 5 January 2024). The bank check followed on 15–30 January 2024 (release of 12 January 2024). On 13 June 2025 the ministry announced a round for the waiting list, 16–27 June 2025, following rejections and withdrawals.

Under MEDAT Order no. 2,224/03.11.2025 (Official Gazette no. 1034/07.11.2025), beneficiaries may repay the credit partially or early without losing the capital premium, after reimbursement (release of 10 November 2025). Details of past editions are on the programme's archive page, and what was eligible in 2023, plus what you can prepare now, is further down this page.

The 2023 rules (Order 603/2023), the benchmark for a new edition

The grant was at most 50% of eligible costs and at most 500,000 lei, together with an investment credit of at least 250,000 lei (point 5.4): a 500,000 lei grant implied a project of about 1,000,000 lei in eligible costs. The firm had to be founded by 31.12.2019 (3.2 c), a shareholder could take part with one firm only (3.2 d), the activity had to be authorised for at least 12 months under a single CAEN Rev. 2 code (3.2 g), and tax debts, rescheduled ones included, excluded the firm (3.2 h).

One grant per firm was awarded in 2023–2027 (6.6). The scheme is valid to 31.12.2027, with payments to 31.12.2028 (2.1), and the bank checks the project's bankability first (7 g). All of this may differ in a new edition; until its guide, it is only a benchmark.

The digital part: what category 6.1 e) covered

In 2023, category 6.1 e) included a presentation website, an online store, domain registration without hosting, promotion of the site or store, software needed for the activity, electronic signature, IT equipment, licences, smartphones and tablets. Each cost had to be tied to the funded CAEN code (6.2). VAT, transport and commissioning were not eligible (6.3), and purchases were new assets only, no leasing or second-hand (6.4–6.5).

The core of a retailer's project is the online store: product catalogue, cart, online payments, courier delivery. Around it sits the stock software — inventory, intake, stocktaking — and integration with the tills and POS terminals, so a sale in the shop and a sale on the site draw down the same stock. For retailers with several locations we add consolidated reports by shop and channel.

As an order of magnitude, indicative and excluding VAT: an online store on an open-source platform usually falls between 1,500 and 8,000 euro, one built on custom flows starts from 10,000 euro, and a bespoke stock-management core — stock, purchasing, sales, invoicing — runs 10,000 to 15,000 euro. The final figure comes from a written offer after an analysis discussion and moves with the number of products, import from the old system and integrations.

From our side the dossier receives documents, not promises: a functional specification, a signed and dated technical offer, a budget by cost category and, at implementation, a line-by-line invoice and an acceptance protocol.

What you can prepare until the procedure appears

The list of eligible expenses is published together with the procedure, so there is none for 2026 yet; any list presented now as official is an extrapolation from 2023. The technical part of the dossier does not depend on it, though, and can be written now: an inventory of the systems you already have (till models and their protocol, payment terminals, the current stock application and its export format, courier accounts, the website platform), the functional specification (catalogue and attributes, price and promotion rules, the order flow through to delivery, returns, several locations, reports) and the data migration plan (how many products, how many customers, which order history, from which system). Migration is the most often underestimated item because it does not show in the interface.

The offer should be broken down so it can be mapped onto any nomenclature: for each line the module name, quantity, unit price and deadline; licences separate from labour, training on its own line, maintenance with a declared monthly rate and period; plus the offer date, the issuer's signature and the bidding company's details. An offer with a single global price cannot be matched to any list and is cut at evaluation. Indicatively and excluding VAT: connecting two systems by API (store and stock, stock and till) usually costs 3,000 to 15,000 euro, and maintenance of an online store 150 to 500 euro a month; the figure moves with the systems inventory above, and the firm price comes from the offer.

At claim stage the invoice has lines named exactly as in the approved budget, together with the signed acceptance protocol, proof of operation (live store address, admin accounts, screenshots from the management panel) and the user documentation. Naming differences between offer, invoice and approved project are the most frequent cause of clarification requests.

The Comerț programme in 2027

The draft MEDAT budget annex estimated 81,000 thousand lei for Programme 8 in 2027, and the 2023 scheme is valid to 31.12.2027 (point 2.1). Estimates are not decisions: there is no order, procedure or date for 2027. After 2027 a new scheme or an extension would be needed, which is our reading, not official information.

What is open now for a retailer

SME Eco-Tech takes applications until 30 December 2026, 20:00, from SMEs in production, trade and services; software counts up to 15,000 lei excluding VAT, alongside a bank credit of at least 60%. Call 2 of PR Centru (Action 2.2) runs from 02.12.2026 to 02.02.2027, with 15,000–200,000 euro per project, for firms in the six counties of the Centru region. Full rules are on their own pages.

What we do not do

We do not sell fiscal cash registers: those are bought from authorised distributors, while we handle their integration with stock management and the online store. It is a part of the order we give up, but it is the correct split of responsibility.

We do not announce session dates or issue eligibility verdicts. Northdan Soft is a software supplier; the application, the scoring and the submission stay with the entrepreneur and their funding consultant.

Frequently asked questions

When does the Comerț programme 2026 launch?⌄

There is no date. On 18 November 2026 MEDAT has published no implementation procedure and no session, although the draft 2026 budget listed 50 million lei in commitment appropriations for the programme (a figure not rechecked after the October 2026 rectification). The procedure is approved by order before the registration window.

How much money does the Comerț programme give?⌄

In the 2023 edition, a grant of at most 50% of eligible costs, up to 500,000 lei, together with a bank credit of at least 250,000 lei (Order 603/2023, point 5.4). A new edition may change the ceiling, the percentage and the credit.

Is the online store and software funded?⌄

In 2023, yes: category 6.1 e) included a presentation site, online store, software needed for the activity, licences and IT equipment. Each cost had to be tied to the funded CAEN code (6.2). A new edition's list is set by its own procedure.

Can I start the store now and claim it later?⌄

No, under the 2023 rules: expenses, including invoices issued before the funding agreement took effect, were not eligible (point 9.12). Build the store now only as a business investment, not as a future claim.

Is there money for the Comerț programme in 2027?⌄

The draft MEDAT budget annex estimated 81 million lei for 2027, and the 2023 scheme is valid to 31.12.2027 (point 2.1). These are estimates, not decisions: without an order and a procedure there is no session.

Can I connect the online store to the till I already have?⌄

In most cases, yes: we integrate stock management with compatible tills, so physical and online sales draw down the same stock. We check the specific model before confirming the solution, and we do not sell fiscal devices.

What can I do now so I do not improvise when the procedure appears?⌄

An inventory of existing systems, the functional specification of the store and stock management, the data migration plan and a signed, dated offer broken down by module. All of it stays valid whatever form the procedure takes.
