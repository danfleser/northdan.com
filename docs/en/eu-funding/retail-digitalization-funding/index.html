Funding for a shop: one stock for the shelf and the web

One stock for the shelf and the web: stock control wired to the tills, and an online store selling from the same quantities, can be funded in 2026 on two routes with very different software rules.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

On 18 November 2026 there is no national call dedicated to shops, but a non-food shop (clothing, footwear, furniture or household goods) has two routes to digitalization: if the activity is in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș or Sibiu, call 2 of Action 2.2 in the Centru Regional Programme gives 15,000–200,000 euro non-refundable (up to 90%), submitted from 02.12.2026 at 12:00 to 02.02.2027 at 12:00, to SMEs with profit in the previous year, at least one employee and no excluded CAEN code (guide 4.3, 5.4, 3.4); nationwide, SME Eco-Tech takes applications until 30 December 2026 at 20:00, with a grant of at most 40% and a mandatory bank loan. A bricks-and-mortar shop leaks margin in very specific places: supplier orders placed from memory, prices changed on the shelf label but not in the till, sellable goods stranded in the back room because nobody can see how fast they rotate. Retail digitalization starts exactly there — inventory management wired to the point-of-sale tills, goods receipts scanned with a barcode reader, replenishment orders generated from real sales — and then extends naturally to the second channel: an online store selling from the same stock, with synchronised prices, instead of a parallel ledger that fights the shop floor. For SME Eco-Tech, the change this summer has a precise date: an order published in the Romanian Official Gazette no. 558 of 7 July 2026 removed the condition that activity financed through SME Eco-Tech be manufacturing, admitting trade and services; applications run to 30 December 2026 at 20:00 and the grant covers at most 40% of eligible costs, capped at 267,240 lei, the equivalent of CHF 50,000. Two limits matter as much as that ceiling: the line for software needed to run the business is eligible only up to 15,000 lei excluding VAT, and green equipment must account for at least 40% of total eligible costs.

The Comerț (Trade) programme, the historic route from a shop to a grant, has no published procedure for 2026. Checked on 18 November 2026: the SME Eco-Tech deadline, the missing Comerț procedure and the final guide of the Centru call, published on 30 October 2026; the software ceiling (15,000 lei excluding VAT) and the 40% green-equipment threshold, in the consolidated SME Eco-Tech procedure. Northdan Soft builds the stock system and the online store as a software vendor and provides its own quote and technical specification; we do not write the funding application and do not promise approval.

How we help

One stock for the shelf and the web

The online store sells from the stock ledger's real quantities, with prices and availability synchronised automatically — no orders for products that do not exist and no duplicate records.

Replenishment driven by sales figures

The system proposes supplier orders from how fast products actually rotate, so cash sits in goods that sell rather than in dead stock on the back shelves.

A project written on shop-floor reality

The budget lists goods receipts, stocktakes, labelling and the online channel — operations an evaluator immediately recognises as a retailer's, rather than vague phrasing.

Non-food shops in the Centru region: Action 2.2

Call 2 of Action 2.2 uses a list of excluded codes (Annex 3), not a list of eligible ones, and specialised non-food retail is not excluded: clothing shops (4771), footwear and leather goods shops (4772), hardware and DIY shops (4752), code 4759 and non-specialised shops selling mainly non-food (4719) have no row in Annex 3. On certificates already moved to Rev.3, read the code off the ONRC certificate. Basic conditions: an SME set up as a company (Law 31/1990) or a cooperative, at least one full fiscal year, net or operating profit above zero in the previous fiscal year, and at least one employee (guide 5.1.2, 5.1.1.6, 5.1.1.7). Support is 15,000–200,000 euro, at most 90%, with at least 10% own contribution (guide 3.4, 5.4). For an application filed in 2026, the guide requires a company set up by 3 January 2025 at the latest and profit at 31 December 2025 (guide 5.1.1.6); for January 2027 filings the guide gives no example. For written confirmation, ask the ADR Centru helpdesk.

The claim that the call excludes trade in general is wrong: in retail only food, tobacco and fuel are excluded, plus computers, phones and audio-video. Annex 3 also excludes wholesale codes a shop often has authorised: food and drink (4631–4638), flowers and plants (4622), furs and hides (4624), tobacco (4635) and non-specialised food (4639), computers and telecoms (4651, 4652) (Annex 3, rows 7–8, 28–29, 105–117). Specifically: 4711, non-specialised shops selling mainly food, drink and tobacco (Annex 3, row 30); 4721–4725 and 4729, specialised food shops (rows 118–123; on Rev.3, 4729 appears as 4727); 4726, tobacco (row 31); 4730, fuel (row 47); 4741, 4742 and 4743, computers, phones and audio-video (rows 9–11; on Rev.3, 4740). Food shops have a separate page. The test applies to the whole company: no excluded code authorised on the submission date, at any location, and no income from excluded activities in the current year and the last fiscal year (guide 5.1.1.1; Annex 5, at any business location; activated means authorised and with activity in the accounts, note 6). A clothing shop that also has 4725 or 4726 authorised cannot apply, even if the project covers clothing only.

Each shop named as a place of implementation must be a registered office or working point in the ORC certificate at contract signing, with the digitalized activity carried out there for at least one fiscal year, the code authorised at submission and income in the submission year and the year before, proven separately for each working point (guide 5.7.4, 5.2.1). Opening a new shop, adding capacity or diversifying is not funded (guide 3.6, 5.2.1), and a company may file only one application in the call (guide 5.1.1.8). The bridge between the two programmes: de minimis aid received through SME Eco-Tech, described above, counts against the same 300,000 euro ceiling over three consecutive years at single-undertaking level, whatever the source, and is declared in Annex 11 (guide 3.13).

Omnichannel and the 1% e-commerce criterion

One stock for the shelf and the web means, under call 2.2, ERP or stock management, a digitalized order flow and e-invoicing (guide 5.3.2.1 a), an online store and e-commerce solutions (5.3.2.1 b), security of online transactions and personal data (5.3.2.1 c) and integration with the tills as installation, configuration and testing (5.3.2.1 f). Hardware qualifies only if it serves the solution and is closely tied to the digitalization process (guide 3.6, 5.3.2.2); inventory items under 2,500 lei each are not eligible (5.3.2.3), so a scanner or label printer bought on its own usually falls here: check the unit price excluding VAT in the quote. A SaaS e-commerce platform is paid for only during implementation, then from own funds across the three years of durability (5.3.2.5). The base activity must be at least 50% of the eligible budget (5.2.3). A firm first order placed with the supplier before the application is filed makes the investment ineligible; the digitalization plan, consultancy and initial audit may be bought beforehand (guide 5.3.3, 5.3.1; Glossary item 11). Unlike SME Eco-Tech, call 2.2 has no separate software ceiling (5.3.2.1). Our reading: Annex 3, row 30, lists code 4791 as the Rev.3 counterpart of the excluded 4711, while Rev.2 4791 is not on the list; a company whose certificate is already on Rev.3 and shows 4791 should ask the ADR Centru helpdesk in writing before paying for the file.

DESI criterion 11 requires e-commerce sales of at least 1% of total turnover; the order comes through a website, app or EDI, payment may be online or offline, and orders written by e-mail do not count (guide 5.2.2). Our worked example: at a turnover of 2,000,000 lei the threshold is 20,000 lei of online sales a year. Criterion 12 requires B2C sales above 10% of online sales; for a shop selling to consumers it follows once criterion 11 exists (our reading; the auditor certifies, Annex 13). Criteria 5, 6 and 7 (price lists, order tracking, link to social networks) can be met by a working online shop, while 9 (electronic invoices) and 10 (paid online advertising) only if the company issues electronic invoices and pays for online advertising; in 1.1 only criteria new compared with submission count (our reading; the auditor certifies, Annex 13). In the scoring grid, 1.1 gives 10 points if after the project the company meets at least 5 more DESI criteria than at submission (3–4 more: 7 points; 1–2: 3 points; 0 points means rejection), 1.2 gives 15 points for at least 7 of 12 criteria plus ERP, CRM, IoT, cloud, AI, VR-AR or RPA, and 1.4 gives 10 points for over 10 unique annual users, employees and/or customers (Annex 4). The threshold of at least 7 of 12 (RCR 13) must be reached at most one year after project end (guide 3.8). On one Centru project Northdan is either the software supplier (stock, online store, CRM), or the independent auditor (CAEN 6220, guide 5.3.2.11), or the author of the digitalization plan, never two of them (5.3.2.8). For written confirmation, ask the ADR Centru helpdesk.

What a retailer can realistically count on for funding

SME Eco-Tech is the only nationally funded route open to a shop right now, and it is an investment programme rather than a digitalization grant. A project starts at 668,100 lei of eligible costs, of which an investment loan of at least 400,860 lei must be contracted with a partner bank, and the software line stops at 15,000 lei excluding VAT. Support takes the form of de minimis aid, capped at 300,000 euro over the last three years per single undertaking, as set out in the consolidated implementation procedure. Since 30 October 2026, SME Eco-Tech also funds activity on a secondary CAEN code, subject to that code's age condition (OG 906/30.10.2026), and rejected firms may re-apply in the same session (second chance).

As of 18 November 2026 the ministry (MEDAT) has not published a procedure for a 2026 edition of the Comerț (Trade) programme: its imm.gov.ro page holds only documents of earlier editions, and the ministry's latest announcement concerns simplifying the implementation of projects already contracted under Microindustrialization and Trade. With no procedure there is no per-beneficiary ceiling, no co-financing rate and no submission calendar. The last real session, closed in January 2024, ran on a 500,000 lei ceiling of which half was a bank loan rather than a grant.

It is not a grant, yet the EDIH channel already works: SMEs receive digital-maturity assessments and solution testing as non-financial state aid, granted on a contract basis. The applicant on the calls that finance those hubs is the hub itself, never the company receiving the service. Start-Up Nation session 2 funds only a new firm set up by a graduate of the free 40-hour course aged 18 to under 30, with up to 250,000 lei and at most 90%; course enrolment on minimis.imm.gov.ro stays open until 27.11.2026 at 20:00, and an existing shop cannot use it. PNRR C9 digitalization is closed, and POCIDIF 2.1 closed on 30.10.2026 and was for ICT codes only.

How we build a shop's digitalization, step by step

The order that works: stock management first — product catalogue, scanned goods receipts, the link to the point-of-sale tills, rolling stocktakes. Only then the online channel, because e-commerce launched on top of chaotic stock multiplies the chaos rather than the sales.

Each phase becomes a budget line with a price and a verifiable result, and at handover a written acceptance report showing operations demonstrated on the shop's real goods: a scanned receipt, a sale rung through the till, an online order fulfilled from the same stock. Exactly the evidence a payment claim usually asks for, per the official guides.

In SME Eco-Tech, three costs stay with the company, because the programme procedure excludes them: VAT, installation and commissioning, and staff training. On a software component those are precisely the implementation costs, so they belong in the budget from the start. In call 2.2 in Centru, installation, configuration and testing (guide 5.3.2.1 f) and training (5.3.2.6) are eligible, and VAT is ineligible only if recoverable (5.3.3, Annex 9).

Frequently asked questions

When will a new session of the Comerț programme open for shops?⌄

There is no announced date: on 18 November 2026 the ministry had not published the procedure of a 2026 edition of the Comerț programme. Without a procedure there is no per-beneficiary ceiling, co-financing rate or submission calendar; the last session, closed in January 2024, had a 500,000 lei ceiling, half of it a bank loan.

Can a shop apply to SME Eco-Tech after July 2026?⌄

Yes: trade became eligible through the order published in the Official Gazette no. 558 of 7 July 2026, except for the fields excluded by de minimis rules, and since 30 October 2026 funding can also be requested on a secondary CAEN code, subject to that code's age condition (OG 906/30.10.2026). The annex listing eligible activity codes is part of the consolidated procedure and must be read in its current version.

Can a clothing shop in the Centru region ask for digitalization funding?⌄

Yes, if the company is an SME with at least one full fiscal year, profit in the previous fiscal year and at least one employee, and no code it has authorised, at any location, is in Annex 3: clothing (4771), footwear (4772) and DIY (4752) retail are not excluded; 15,000–200,000 euro, submission 02.12.2026–02.02.2027 (guide 5.1.1.1, 5.1.1.6, 5.1.1.7, 5.4).

How much must a shop sell online to meet the DESI e-commerce criterion?⌄

At least 1% of total turnover, from orders received through a website, app or EDI; orders written by e-mail do not count, and payment may be on delivery (guide 5.2.2, criterion 11). At a turnover of 2,000,000 lei the threshold is 20,000 lei a year (our calculation).

Can I use both SME Eco-Tech and the Centru call?⌄

Yes, the Centru call guide does not forbid using both programmes, but the same expense cannot be funded twice (auditors check double funding, guide 5.3.2.10), and de minimis aid received through SME Eco-Tech counts against the 300,000 euro ceiling over three consecutive years at single-undertaking level, declared in Annex 11 (guide 3.13).

How much software fits inside the SME Eco-Tech ceiling?⌄

Software needed to run the business is eligible up to 15,000 lei excluding VAT. The digital package also covers a website or online store, an electronic signature, IT equipment and licences, while green equipment must stay at a minimum of 40% of eligible costs.

Is e-commerce worth adding if most sales still happen in the shop?⌄

Yes, if it is built as a second channel on the same stock ledger: quantities and prices synchronise automatically and online orders are picked from the existing warehouse. As a parallel ledger filled in by hand, it creates more trouble than turnover.

Do a till, a POS or a barcode scanner qualify for Centru funding?⌄

Only if they serve the digital solution and are closely tied to it (guide 3.6, 5.3.2.2), and an inventory item under 2,500 lei each, excluding VAT, is not eligible (5.3.2.3). Integration with the tills, as installation, configuration and testing, is eligible (5.3.2.1 f).

I have a new company and want to open a shop: what can I apply for?⌄

The Centru call requires at least one full fiscal year (a company set up by 3 January 2025 at the latest for a 2026 application, guide 5.1.1.6) and profit. Start-Up Nation session 2 funds new firms of course graduates aged 18 to under 30, up to 250,000 lei, at most 90%, with course enrolment until 27.11.2026 at 20:00.
