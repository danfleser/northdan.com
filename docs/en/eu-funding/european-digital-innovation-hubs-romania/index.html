The EDIH network in Romania: digitalization support for companies

The European Digital Innovation Hubs offer SMEs and mid-caps across every region of Romania free or subsidized services: test-before-invest, digital skills and orientation towards funding.

Ghidul oficial al programului
ⓘNorthdan Soft este furnizor de servicii software, nu consultant de finanțare, și nu este afiliat autorităților de management sau agențiilor de dezvoltare. Informațiile despre programe au caracter orientativ — verifică întotdeauna condițiile în ghidul oficial al programului.

The EDIH network in Romania is fully operational: the European Digital Innovation Hubs, financed through the Digital Europe Programme and coordinated by the European Commission together with ADR, Romania's digitalization authority, offer SMEs and mid-cap companies services that are free or heavily subsidized. Coverage spans every region of the country, with hubs such as WeH, eDIH-DIZ Iași, FIT Brașov and DIGIVEST among those active. The services fall into three broad categories: test-before-invest — you evaluate a technology on your own concrete case before paying for it —, digital skills development for your team, and orientation towards the funding sources suited to your investment. Northdan Soft is not an EDIH and does not offer funding consultancy: we are the software vendor that picks up the conclusions of an EDIH journey and turns them into real implementation, with a technical offer, an eligible-cost budget and on-time delivery.

What an EDIH is and who funds the network

European Digital Innovation Hubs are centres co-financed through the Digital Europe Programme, the European Commission's programme for the adoption of digital technologies; in Romania, national coordination involves ADR — the Authority for the Digitalization of Romania.

Each hub brings together universities, clusters and technology companies from a region and works as a one-stop digitalization desk for SMEs and mid-caps: you assess, you learn, then you decide with full information.

The three service categories for companies

Test-before-invest: the hub's specialists help you trial a technology — from automation to artificial intelligence — on your company's own data and processes, before any purchase.

Skills development covers training the team on digital tools, while funding orientation shows you which European or national programmes match the investment you are preparing.

The step after EDIH: implementation

An EDIH journey usually ends with recommendations: which processes to digitalize, which technologies deserve the investment. That is where we take over — we build the recommended ERP, online store or application and deliver the technical documents for the funding you choose.

The simplest start: tell us on WhatsApp what the hub recommended, and we come back with a technical and budget estimate; the e-mail office@northdan.com works just as well.

Frequently asked questions

How much do the services of an EDIH hub cost for an SME?⌄

The core services — test-before-invest, training, funding orientation — are free or heavily subsidized through the Digital Europe Programme, within the limits of each hub's support scheme; the exact conditions are checked with the hub you choose.

Which EDIH hubs exist in Romania and which one is mine?⌄

The network covers every region of the country, and active hubs include WeH, eDIH-DIZ Iași, FIT Brașov and DIGIVEST. The full list and each hub's coverage area are consulted on the ADR website and on the hubs' own pages.

Does an EDIH offer cash grants to companies?⌄

Not directly: the hubs deliver services, not cash financing. The funding-orientation component, however, helps you identify the programmes — the POCIDIF calls, for example — through which the actual investment can be supported.

Can a large company approach an EDIH?⌄

The main audience is SMEs and mid-cap companies, and some hubs also work with public-sector organizations. Check with the hub in your region which categories of beneficiaries its service scheme accepts.
