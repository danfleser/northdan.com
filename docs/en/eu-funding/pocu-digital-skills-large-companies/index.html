The call record, with sources in plain view

The official title is „Digital skills for employees of large enterprises”, a call launched by the Ministry of European Funds through the managing authority for the Human Capital Operational Programme, under Priority Axis 3, Specific Objective 3.12. The applicant guide and its annexes were published as a 5.11 MB archive attached to the announcement.

The call budget: EUR 10 million, a figure that includes both the European Union contribution and the national contribution. The funding area: exclusively the less developed regions, named in the announcement as Centru, Sud-Est, Sud Muntenia, Nord-Est, Nord-Vest, Vest and Sud-Vest Oltenia.

Submissions ran through MySMIS2014 between 17 August and 15 November 2020. That was the only window; POCU 2014-2020 has ended and the call has no relaunch.

Three differences from the twin SME call

The first is the budget: EUR 10 million here, against EUR 20 million on the call dedicated to SME employees. The second is the deadline: 15 November 2020 here, 30 October 2020 there — 16 days more, even though both opened on 17 August 2020.

The third, and the most substantive: the large-enterprise call has an associated state aid scheme for organising and running adult training programmes, stated explicitly in the announcement. The SME call does not carry that note in its own announcement.

What does not differ: the axis and specific objective, the implementation area across the seven less developed regions, and the absence of the Bucharest-Ilfov region from the list. Both calls financed people, not technology.

What it means that the call carries a state aid scheme

When training is delivered to a large enterprise, the economic advantage reaches a firm competing on the market, so the support qualifies as state aid rather than a plain public service. In practice the value of the training received is attributed to the company and reported as such, even where the project is run by someone else.

The concrete parameters of the scheme — intensities, covered costs, reporting obligations — are those in the scheme documents and in the applicant guide attached to the announcement. We do not reproduce them here from secondary sources; for a company reconstructing its own aid history today, the source is the official document.

The note stays practically relevant: a large company that was a target group in such a project most likely has a state aid value recorded against its name from that period, and that counts towards cumulation in later years.

Who applied and who received the training

The target group was the employees of large enterprises. The applicant, meaning the entity signing the funding application, is the one defined in the applicant guide published as an archive on the call page; on POCU training calls that role generally fell to training providers and social partners, not to the beneficiary companies.

We do not publish the exact list of eligible applicants because we could not reopen the guide archive at the date of this verification. We preferred to say so plainly rather than fill the gap with a list taken from elsewhere.

The model matters for planning today: a large company wanting publicly funded training generally allies with a training provider or with the social partners who build the project, instead of applying on its own.

Where the line continued and where we step in

In 2021-2027, digital skills training is funded through the Education and Employment Programme, the successor of POCU. Those calls have their own guides, calendars and conditions, tracked on our dedicated pages so an archive is never mixed up with a current status.

Northdan Soft is not an accredited training provider, does not run courses and cannot be a partner on the training component of such a project. If that is what you need, we are not the right address, and that is more useful in the first sentence than after three conversations.

What we do build are the tools the trained team then uses: internal applications, integrations between systems, automations and the accompanying technical documentation. For large companies we also work in a dedicated-team format, with cadence and deliverables set by contract. We do not draft funding applications and we do not promise approvals.

Frequently asked questions

What was the budget of the POCU call for large-enterprise employees?

EUR 10 million, a figure that includes the European Union contribution and the national contribution, per the official announcement of the POCU managing authority. The twin call dedicated to SME employees had double that, EUR 20 million.

When could projects be submitted, and is the call still active?

Submissions ran through MySMIS2014 between 17 August and 15 November 2020. The call is definitively closed, together with the whole Human Capital Operational Programme 2014-2020, and no relaunch has been announced.

Why did the large-enterprise call carry a state aid scheme?

Because training delivered to a large enterprise is an economic advantage for a firm active on the market. The announcement states explicitly that the call has an associated state aid scheme for organising and running adult training programmes; its parameters are read from the scheme documents.

Could employees based in Bucharest or Ilfov take part?

Not through this call. The announcement limits funding to projects implemented in the seven less developed regions: Centru, Sud-Est, Sud Muntenia, Nord-Est, Nord-Vest, Vest and Sud-Vest Oltenia. The Bucharest-Ilfov region is not included.

Where do I find the applicant guide for this call?

On the call announcement page on mfe.gov.ro, linked in the record above: the guide and annexes were published there as a 5.11 MB archive. That is also where the list of eligible applicants and the evaluation criteria sit.

What does Northdan Soft bring a large company investing in digital skills?

The tools side: we develop the internal software and integrations the trained team uses, we offer IT consultancy and, where needed, a dedicated development team. We are not a training provider and we do not handle the funding file.
