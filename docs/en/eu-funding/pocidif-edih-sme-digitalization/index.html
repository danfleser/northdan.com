POCIDIF EDIH, Actions 6.1 and 2.4: who applies, by when and what your company gets

On the POCIDIF calls for EDIH hubs only the hubs apply, never the companies. On Action 6.1 only DIGIVEST can still apply, until 30 December 2026; Action 2.4 closed on 25 October 2023.

POCIDIF funds SME digitalization through EDIH hubs in two calls: Action 6.1, launched on 30 April 2026 with EUR 20,918,624.37, where six hubs had a deadline of 30 June 2026 and DIGIVEST can apply until 30 December 2026 at 17:00 (guide 6.1, 3.3 and 4.3.2), and Action 2.4, closed on 25 October 2023; in both the applicant is the hub, and the company receives services, not money. Action 2.4 (call POCIDIF/1147/2/1) had EUR 12,873,886.83, of which EUR 9,998,518.23 for less developed regions and EUR 2,875,368.60 for more developed ones, and the state aid scheme was approved by Order MIPE no. 3151 of 18 August 2023. The support reaching a company is non-financial state aid, contracted between the hub and the final beneficiary. Verified on 18 November 2026 against the ministry call sheets, guide 6.1 (Order 611/2026, amended by Order 1098/2026) and the OIPSI list of registered projects.

Action 6.1: the deadline differs per hub, the budget is split across seven projects

The only eligible applicants on 6.1 are the EDIHs approved by the European Commission as "Seal of STEP" in the call DIGITAL-2025-EDIH-EU-EEA-08-CONSOLIDATION-STEP (Action 6.1 "… with STEP sovereignty seal"). Action 6.1 is a non-competitive call with submission by a deadline (guide 6.1, 3.1). Deadlines differ per hub: FIT EDIH, DIH4Society, CiTyInnoHub, Digital Innovation Zone, Wallachia eHub and Transilvania Digital Innovation Hub had a deadline of 30 June 2026 at 17:00, while DIGIVEST can apply until 30 December 2026 at 17:00 (4.3.1 and 4.3.2). The 30 April – 30 December 2026 window repeated on many sites does not apply to everyone: 30 April is only the launch.

The budget is EUR 20,918,624.37 from the ERDF (RON 106,634,779.59): EUR 18,421,430 for less developed regions and EUR 2,497,194.37 for Bucharest-Ilfov (3.3). Five projects are funded 50% from this programme, the other 50% coming from the Digital Europe Programme, while TDIH and DIGIVEST get 100% because the European budget of that part ran out (2.1 and 3.3). Allocations per hub (5.4): DIH4Society EUR 2,263,000.25; FIT EDIH 2,283,565.87; Digital Innovation Zone 2,188,416.45; CiTyInnoHub 2,233,750.19; Wallachia eHub 1,550,162.50; TDIH 5,731,096.55; DIGIVEST 4,668,632.56.

The guide was approved by Order 611 of 28 April 2026; Order 1098 of 9 July 2026 changed only section 5.3.1 (expense eligibility), not the deadlines. The list of registered projects published by OIPSI for 6.1 (version of 3 June 2026, still attached on 18 November 2026) holds only two projects: TEDIHT 2.0 (SMIS 365738, RON 29,214,837.77) and Wallachia eHUB 2.0 (SMIS 365701, RON 7,915,489.54), RON 37,130,327.31 in total. The list says nothing about the other five hubs.

How a company reaches a hub's services: request, declarations, selection

The company procedure under guide 6.1 (3.13): the hub publishes its services, prices and available budget; the company files a request with the hub with four to six declarations; the hub selects on criteria that include "first come, first served"; a contract is signed; the aid value is the one in the hub's price list.

Ceilings (3.13): an SME can receive support up to 100%, with a maximum of EUR 220,000 per enterprise over three fiscal years, including the current one, checked in RegAS. A mid-cap enterprise (up to 3,000 employees, not an SME) receives de minimis aid, a maximum of EUR 300,000 per single undertaking over three years (EU Reg. 2023/2831). The EUR 220,000 ceiling adds up all consultancy and innovation-support aid the company received; it is not the value of an EDIH package. Order MIPE no. 440 of 25 March 2026 extended the scheme of Order 3151/2023 to mid-cap enterprises.

Each hub has a minimum target of firms served (guide 6.1, 3.8.1, indicator RCO 04): FIT EDIH 86, DIH4Society 80, CiTyInnoHub 84, Digital Innovation Zone 98, Wallachia eHub 64, TDIH 160 and DIGIVEST 165, 737 in total.

Two roles that get confused: the applicant and the final beneficiary

Under Action 2.4 the applicant was the hub, and the company is a final beneficiary. The distinction is not semantic: the hub files the application, signs the contract with the managing authority and answers for the indicators, while the company signs with the hub a contract granting non-financial state aid for the services received.

The managing authority published a dedicated instruction precisely on verifying these non-financial aid contracts granted to SMEs within Action 2.4 projects. The practical consequence for a company: the value of the services received is booked as aid and counts towards its own aid ceilings, even though not a single leu passes through the company's account.

Action 2.4: the seven contracted projects and the counties they cover

The list of contracted POCIDIF projects, in its July 2024 version, contains exactly seven projects under Action 2.4, all marked at the time as under implementation, totalling RON 63.39 million of granted funding from the ERDF and the national budget. The document is two years old at our verification date, so it is a 2024 snapshot, not a live status: we cannot confirm what stage each project is at today, and network consolidation has meanwhile moved to Action 6.1.

The declared implementation areas, as they appear in that list: Transilvania Digital Innovation Hub, through Asociatia Transilvania IT, in the North-West region, county of Cluj; DIGIVEST, through ADR Vest, in the West region, county of Timis; Digital Innovation Zone, through the Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iasi, in the North-East, covering Iasi and Neamt; DIH4Society, through Asociatia Cluj IT, in the North-West, covering Bihor, Bistrita-Nasaud, Cluj, Maramures, Satu Mare and Salaj.

The rest of the coverage: CiTyInnoHub, through Ovidius University of Constanta, in Bucharest-Ilfov and the South-East, covering Bucharest, Braila, Buzau, Constanta, Galati, Tulcea and Vrancea; FIT EDIH, through Iceberg Plus, in Bucharest-Ilfov and the Centre, covering Bucharest, Alba, Brasov, Covasna, Mures, Sibiu and Cluj; Wallachia eHub, through Spiru Haret University, in Bucharest-Ilfov, the North-East and South-Muntenia, covering Bucharest, Vaslui, Prahova and Teleorman.

On 6.1, Wallachia eHUB 2.0 declares South-Muntenia and Bucharest-Ilfov, covering Prahova, Ilfov, Bucharest and Calarasi, not Vaslui and Teleorman as on 2.4, and TEDIHT 2.0 adds "regions under-represented by other EDIHs" (OIPSI list for 6.1).

What an EDIH hub does not give you

It does not give you money. It does not reimburse licences, subscriptions or software development invoices, it does not write your funding application for another programme and it does not issue price offers you can attach to a dossier. The support is in kind, assessed and reported as non-financial state aid.

Nor does it build your production system. Test-before-invest means you try a technology on a real scenario, with the hub specialists, before a purchase; the outcome is a better-informed decision and usually a requirements list — not a working ERP.

And one point that costs us: if all you need is the test-before-invest journey or team training, the hub is the right address and we have nothing to sell you at that stage. We are not an EDIH hub, we do not deliver the services funded by the call, and we do not present ourselves as any hub's accredited partner.

What we deliver after the EDIH journey

We take the hub technical conclusions and turn them into something that works: specifications, architecture, development, integration with existing systems, data migration, testing and technical documentation. The same documentation serves later if the investment enters a funding dossier.

As orders of magnitude, indicative and excluding VAT: a simple web application starts at EUR 5,000 and climbs to EUR 15,000-50,000 once it has integrations and approval flows; implementing an off-the-shelf ERP falls within EUR 5,000-20,000, and a custom management core within EUR 10,000-15,000. The firm figure comes only after an analysis discussion, and our price guides explain what moves it.

What we do not do, stated once: we do not write funding applications, we do not assess your company's eligibility for any programme and we do not promise approvals. We are the software vendor; the dossier stays with you or with the consultant you work with.

Frequently asked questions

Who can still submit a project under POCIDIF 6.1 EDIH?⌄

Only DIGIVEST, through ADR Vest, can still submit a project under Action 6.1, until 30 December 2026 at 17:00. The other six hubs had a deadline of 30 June 2026 (guide 6.1, 4.3.2). Companies do not submit projects: they request services from the hub, once the hub publishes its offer.

Can I still submit a project under POCIDIF Action 2.4?⌄

No, and you could not have before either. Submissions ended on 25 October 2023 at 23:59, and the only eligible applicants were the EDIH hubs selected by the European Commission through the Digital Europe Programme. Companies are final beneficiaries of the services, not applicants.

How much is EDIH support worth to a company?⌄

An SME can receive services up to 100% of their value. The EUR 220,000 ceiling over three fiscal years adds up all consultancy and innovation-support aid the company received; it is not the value of an EDIH package. Mid-cap: de minimis, at most EUR 300,000. The real value is in the hub's price list (guide 6.1, 3.13).

What documents does a hub ask a company for?⌄

The hub asks for statements on own responsibility: SME or mid-cap status, eligibility for innovation state aid or de minimis, no double funding, aid received in the current and the two previous fiscal years, and de minimis cumulation (guide 6.1, 3.13, step 2).

How do I pick the right EDIH hub for my county?⌄

Start from the declared area (2.4): North-West, Transilvania Digital Innovation Hub and DIH4Society; West, DIGIVEST; North-East, Digital Innovation Zone; South-East, CiTyInnoHub; Centre, FIT EDIH; Bucharest-Ilfov, CiTyInnoHub, FIT EDIH and Wallachia eHub; South-Muntenia, Wallachia eHub (on 6.1: Prahova, Ilfov, Bucharest, Calarasi).

Where do I find the guide, orders and instructions for the POCIDIF EDIH calls?⌄

In the POCIDIF guide register of the ministry, linked in the call fact card. For 6.1: the guide approved by Order 611/28.04.2026 and Order 1098/09.07.2026. For 2.4: Orders 3152/18.08.2023 (the guide), 5870/20.11.2023 and 665/04.03.2024, plus Instructions no. 2 and no. 4.
