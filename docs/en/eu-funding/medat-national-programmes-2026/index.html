EU Funding

What the Ministry of Economy runs for entrepreneurs in 2026, and the stage each programme is in

Only one ministry programme is receiving funding applications in November 2026, SME Eco-Tech, and thresholds decide it, not the ceiling; Femeia Antreprenor has no new session; the 2024 edition opened payment claims on 10 November 2026, and Start-Up Nation is in its course stage only.

At 18 November 2026 the Ministry of Economy, Digitalisation, Entrepreneurship and Tourism (MEDAT) has one programme receiving funding applications, SME Eco-Tech, until 30 December 2026, 20:00; enrolment in the free Start-Up Nation course closes on 27 November 2026, 20:00; Femeia Antreprenor, 2024 edition, has been in the payment-claim stage since 10 November 2026; and Microindustrializare and Comerț have no session announced for 2026. Entrepreneurship programmes should be read by threshold, not by funding ceiling: the maximum tells you how much you may receive, the minimum tells you whether you are allowed to apply at all. SME Eco-Tech requires an investment project of at least RON 668,100 in eligible costs, of which the loan contracted with a partner bank must be at least RON 400,860 — conditions that rule out from the start a small firm that only wanted an online shop. The grant is at most 40% of eligible costs, capped at RON 267,240 (CHF 50,000), and the deadline was extended from 26 July to 24 September, then to 30 December 2026. Verified on 18 November 2026 against MEDAT announcements: the deadlines above, the thresholds, and the specific software limit. For a software vendor the last one is the awkward figure, which is exactly why we state it first: the software needed to carry out the activity is eligible only up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT. Northdan Soft builds the software and its acceptance documents; it does not write the funding application and does not promise approval. Below: the four SME Eco-Tech figures, Start-Up Nation, the Femeia Antreprenor timeline, the programmes with no session in 2026, and IT costs.

SME Eco-Tech: the figures that tell you whether you qualify

The four figures that decide everything, in the order they are checked. Minimum project size: RON 668,100 in eligible costs. Mandatory loan from a partner bank: at least RON 400,860, that is at least 60% of eligible costs. Grant: at most 40% of eligible costs, capped at RON 267,240 (CHF 50,000). Submission deadline: 30 December 2026, 20:00. In addition, at least 40% of a project's eligible costs must be green or digital equipment (MEDAT release of 22 September 2026). The call budget is RON 288,843,072 (CHF 54 million); the partner banks are Banca Transilvania, BRD, CEC Bank, ProCredit Bank and UniCredit Bank.

The position at 29 September 2026, from the MEDAT release on the first agreements: 432 applications filed, 140 accepted, 103 rejected, 189 in clarification; 41 beneficiaries had obtained the loan and signed the funding agreement. The eligibility scope has widened: through the order published in Monitorul Oficial nr. 558 of 7 July 2026 the programme is no longer restricted to manufacturing, and firms in trade and services, as well as micro-enterprises, became eligible; the RON 2 million turnover threshold for micro-enterprises was removed by Order 1241 of 7 August 2026. The order published in Monitorul Oficial nr. 906 of 30 October 2026 introduced a "second chance": rejected firms may file a new application in the same session, assessed first come, first served within the remaining budget, and financing may also be requested for activities carried out under a secondary CAEN code, subject to the seniority criterion. Any description presenting the programme as reserved for manufacturing, or still announcing the July or September deadline, describes the superseded session.

The programme's digital side carries an explicit limit: the software needed to carry out the activity is eligible up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT, per the implementation procedure. That means SME Eco-Tech does not fund a custom platform; it funds equipment, and the software component accompanies it — typically a consumption-monitoring application or a small production-management module, sized within that limit. A supplier proposing a RON 100,000 software project on this line has either not read the procedure or is counting on you not having read it.

Start-Up Nation: the course until 27 November, then funding

The fourth edition has, at 18 November 2026, one closed session and one under way. Session 1 closed on 29 May 2026. In session 2, MEDAT reopened 17,600 places in the free 40-hour course on 6 October 2026, 10:00, on minimis.imm.gov.ro; on 4 November 2026, 12:00, more than 11,400 were still free. Enrolment in the course closes on 27 November 2026, 20:00.

The order of steps is fixed: the entrepreneurship course first, then the funding application, for up to RON 250,000, at most 90% of eligible costs, by companies newly founded by graduates. The MEDAT announcements do not give the filing date for those applications, and the general deadline for completing spending in this edition is 8 December 2027. An entrepreneur who discovers this after 27 November 2026 may have missed enrolment, because funding cannot be requested without the completed course.

For the technical part of the plan, a digital chapter written in advance, on real offers rather than rounded estimates, is the only part that does not depend on an unknown calendar. Our dedicated page on session 2 tracks precisely what has been announced and what has not.

Femeia Antreprenor 2024: from unblocking to payment claims

Femeia Antreprenor has no new enrolment in 2026. What happens in 2026 is the continuation of the 2024 edition, whose call drew 6,818 women-owned firms applying for funding of up to RON 200,000. On 22 April 2026 MEDAT announced the unblocking of the edition after the 2026 state budget law was adopted: about RON 200 million in commitment appropriations, RON 197 million in budget appropriations and a target of about 1,000 beneficiaries. Rejection decisions went out between 27 and 30 April, appeals could be filed until 6 May and were resolved between 29 April and 22 May, and admission decisions began on 25 May 2026.

On 28 May 2026 MEDAT began the signing of funding agreements. The agreement is generated in minimis.imm.gov.ro and signed electronically within at most 20 days of receiving the admission decision, and the beneficiary uploads proof of co-financing: a bank statement, a loan approval letter or a loan contract. Firms with rescheduled tax debts are not eligible to sign, and the beneficiary also opens a dedicated project account at the partner bank.

On 10 November 2026 the claims module opened, also in minimis.imm.gov.ro. A claim is filed only after all purchases are delivered, received and paid; reimbursement is made in a single instalment, after the documents are checked and the partner bank carries out a certification visit. The file includes the reimbursement request (Annex 7), the claim form (Annex 8), the invoices with proof of payment and the documents proving the two jobs (contracts, REGES extract, REVISAL). For licences and software, Annex 7 asks for additional documents; clarifications must be sent within at most 5 days. This is where a software supplier matters: the invoice, the acceptance report and the licence must be in order before filing.

Programmes with no session in 2026, and how we treat them

Microindustrializare, Comerț și servicii and Accelerarea dezvoltării IMM had their last call in the 2023 edition (enrolment from December 2023 to January 2024). On economie.gov.ro, at the 18 November 2026 check, no session is announced for any of them in 2026. Meșteșuguri și artizanat and TIMM are also on the ministry's list of SME programmes; we state nothing about their 2026 status. The consequence for an entrepreneur is straightforward: there is no ceiling, deadline or budget on which to build a plan, however often figures appear in the press.

We do not publish allocations circulating without a procedure behind them, we do not present a line as about to open, and we do not call closed a line for which we hold no act. When a procedure appears it sets the ceiling, the eligible costs and the calendar at the same time — until then, any of the three would be a guess.

IT costs and justifying the price

The categories that recur from one edition to the next in national entrepreneurship programmes are the same: a presentation website and an online shop, management applications of the ERP or CRM type, IT equipment, software licences and the services of building the solution. Their admissible weight in the budget varies sharply between lines — SME Eco-Tech is the clear example, with its RON 15,000 excluding VAT limit — and confirmation always comes from that session's procedure, not from a general list.

Justifying the price is the part most dossiers underestimate. Guides normally require at least two dated offers or a market analysis for each cost, and evaluators independently check reasonableness against market prices. A technical offer that describes the solution, not just a sum, survives that check; a rounded one does not.

This is where Northdan Soft operates, and it stops exactly where the consultant's work begins: we write the solution specification, the technical offer, the budget by eligible cost category and the draft development contract, and after admission we build the solution and hand over the acceptance documents. We do not write the business plan, do not file the application, do not assess your company's eligibility, and do not issue accommodation offers to inflate a budget — a formal offer discovered at evaluation compromises the dossier, not merely that line.

Frequently asked questions

Which MEDAT programmes receive applications in November 2026?

SME Eco-Tech, with a funding application, until 30 December 2026, 20:00. Enrolment in the free Start-Up Nation course closes on 27 November 2026, 20:00. No other line has open enrolment at the 18 November 2026 check. Before building a plan on SME Eco-Tech, check the two disqualifying thresholds: a project of at least RON 668,100 in eligible costs, and a bank loan of at least RON 400,860.

How much software can SME Eco-Tech pay for?

Software needed to carry out the activity is eligible up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT, per the implementation procedure. That suits a monitoring module or a small-scale management application, not a custom-built platform. If what you actually need is a complex application, this programme is not its route, and we say so before drawing up any budget for you.

Is SME Eco-Tech still reserved for manufacturing companies?

No. Through the order published in Monitorul Oficial nr. 558 of 7 July 2026, firms in trade and services and micro-enterprises became eligible. The order in Monitorul Oficial nr. 906 of 30 October 2026 allows a second application for rejected firms and financing of an activity under a secondary CAEN code, subject to the seniority criterion. Older descriptions still present it as manufacturing-only — a services company that stops at them loses an open route for no reason.

What are the Start-Up Nation session 2 deadlines?

Enrolment in the free course closes on 27 November 2026, 20:00, on minimis.imm.gov.ro. Financing of up to RON 250,000, at most 90% of eligible costs, is requested after completing the course, and the MEDAT announcements do not give the filing date for those applications. The general spending deadline is 8 December 2027; session 1 closed on 29 May 2026.

Can I still apply to Femeia Antreprenor in 2026?

No. There is no new enrolment in 2026. The 2024 edition was unblocked on 22 April 2026, funding agreements have been signed since 28 May 2026, and from 10 November 2026 beneficiaries can file their payment claims in minimis.imm.gov.ro.

Why do you not publish the sums announced in the press for the other programmes?

Because an allocation without a published procedure creates no right and can change entirely before launch. The ceiling, the eligible-cost list and the calendar are all set at once, by the session's implementation procedure. Until it appears, any figure is an intention, and an investment plan built on an intention is rewritten completely the day the real document is published.

What can I prepare now, whichever programme opens first?

Three things, all of which stay valid: a description of the investment in terms of a measurable result, two dated offers for each significant cost category, and a technical specification of the digital component. With those, reacting to a launch takes days. Without them, the first three weeks of any session are spent chasing offers, exactly when every supplier is buried in the same requests.
