Start-Up Digital Package: your business plan's digital chapter, turnkey

For the new firm in Start-Up Nation: website, online store or bookings, e-Factura invoicing and training, delivered as one package.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

The Start-Up Digital Package delivers the digital part of a Start-Up Nation plan — website, online store or bookings, invoicing with e-Factura (Romania's e-invoicing system) and training — and fits two situations today: session 1 firms, which began signing financing contracts on 28 September 2026 and must spend within 12 months, no later than 8 December 2027, and people aged 18 to under 30 enrolled in the session 2 course, open until 27 November 2026, 20:00 (Romania time).

Checked on 18 November 2026 against the Ministry of Economy (MEDAT) communiqués of 28 September and 4 November 2026 and the consolidated procedure (Order 328/2025): in session 2, firms set up by course graduates can receive at most 250,000 RON, up to 90% of eligible costs, and the opening date of the firms' window has not been announced. We deliver the digital part and its documents: the specification, the dated technical offer, the itemised budget, the handover protocol and the supplier documents for the reimbursement claim. We do not write the business plan, we do not file the application and we do not promise approval; the consultant who writes the plan cannot also supply the website, which is why we stay supplier only.

In practice founders buy in pieces: a freelancer for the website, an agency for the logo, a subscription for invoicing, nobody responsible for the whole. The package ties them into one delivery with one point of contact.

What you get

The digital chapter, fully covered

Website, identity, online sales, management software and training in a single purchase — the business plan cites one vendor and a budget broken down by component, with the letter g) expenses kept separate from the others.

Configured around the business model

A café needs bookings and local presence, a manufacturer needs an online store with stock control — the package moulds itself to what you sell, not the reverse.

Documents on the programme's categories

A technical offer dated on the day it is signed, with specifications and unit prices, and a budget breakdown by component, with the letter g) expenses of the Start-Up Nation procedure kept separate from the others.

You start with systems, not promises

At handover your team knows how to operate everything we delivered: training is included and documented, and the credentials and manuals stay with you.

How the package is composed, by business type

Service businesses — clinics, salons, workshops, consultancies: a presentation website with an online booking system, so clients reserve on their own while you watch the calendar from your phone. Invoicing and client records run in the management software.

Product businesses — manufacturing, trade, import: an online store with stock, payments and courier integration, linked to the management system, so every order lowers the stock and generates the documents correctly.

On top of either variant sits the basic visual identity — logo and essential materials — so the company's presence looks consistent from day one.

Where the package fits in the Start-Up Nation procedure

Digital spend falls under point 5.2 para. (2) letter g) of the procedure: presentation website, online store, domain registration, promotion of the site or store, software needed for the activity, electronic signature, IT equipment and licences. Letter g) sets no separate cap for each item; neither a "25,000 RON minimum for the digital package" nor an "8,000 RON cap for the website" appears in the procedure text. Eligibility is decided at evaluation and at reimbursement, not by the supplier. The logo, the visual identity and the training are not named at letter g); we put them on separate budget lines, so the plan can cover them from the firm's own contribution if the evaluation does not accept them.

The electronic signature can be bought before the contract. Consultancy has its own rule at letter h): at most 10,000 RON, and a firm that supplies consultancy in a project cannot supply any other eligible cost in the same project, or all of its costs go unreimbursed.

Programme status on 18 November 2026

Start-Up Nation, session 1: contracts have been in implementation since 28 September 2026; spending and the reimbursement claim must be completed within at most 12 months of the contract entering into force, but not after 8 December 2027 (point 6.3.2 para. 22 of the procedure).

Start-Up Nation, session 2: the 40-hour course is open for sign-up until 27 November 2026, 20:00, and the certificate is required for stage two; the firms' window has no announced date.

Comerț (Trade): planned, no procedure and no session in 2026. Microindustrializare (Micro-industrialization): planned, no session announced; the 2023 edition's rules (a grant plus a mandatory bank loan, a firm founded by 31.12.2019) do not fit a firm founded now.

From contract to reimbursement

We deliver in stages and hand over with an acceptance protocol, plus the invoices and supplier documents the reimbursement claim requires (annex 9 of the procedure). For a session 1 firm we deliver what the approved plan describes, in the form described there.

In session 2, a contract signed in 2027 has less than 12 months before the 8 December 2027 deadline, so the order of work is set from day one: first what blocks sales (website, invoicing), then the rest.

What the components cost, roughly

Indicative bands, in euro excluding VAT: website on an adapted template 300–1,500 €, custom website 1,500–5,000 €; WooCommerce or PrestaShop store 1,500–8,000 €; booking system built into the site 2,000–8,000 €; invoicing and stock software on subscription from 5.84 €/month + VAT; e-Factura module in an existing invoicing tool 0–500 €; logo and identity 150–800 € from a freelancer, 1,000–5,000 € from a studio.

The final figure comes from the written offer, once we know the business model.

What the package does not include

The business plan, consultancy and filing the application are not part of the package. IT equipment is eligible under letter g) but we do not supply it, and paid-advertising budgets stay outside the offer.

Frequently asked questions

I have not submitted the business plan yet — can you help me size the digital budget?⌄

Yes, this is the right moment: we discuss the business model, choose the components and you receive the offer with unit prices before you write the budget. The offer carries its issue date, so the plan's digital chapter rests on real figures.

Does the package also fit Comerț or Microindustrializare?⌄

No, as of 18 November 2026: both programmes are planned, with no procedure and no announced session. The 2023 edition required a bank loan next to the grant, and Microindustrializare required a firm founded by 31.12.2019, so a new firm could not apply.

What happens if my plan is not selected?⌄

No development contract is signed before financing is confirmed. The offer is dated at issue; for a future session we reissue it at that date and at that day's prices rather than recycling the old one.

This is my first company and I am not technical — how hard is it to operate?⌄

The package is designed for founders without a technical background: interfaces are simple, training runs on your concrete operations — an order, an invoice, a booking — and you keep short manuals plus a WhatsApp support channel.

I have a session 1 contract — can you still deliver on time?⌄

Yes, if we start now. Spending must happen within 12 months of the contract, no later than 8 December 2027. We start with what blocks sales, website and invoicing, then the store or bookings, training and handover.

Is there a separate cap for the website in Start-Up Nation?⌄

No: letter g) of the procedure sets no per-item cap, and "25,000 RON for the digital package" or "8,000 RON for the website" do not appear in the text. Costs are justified through the approved plan, the offer and the reimbursement claim, within the project budget.
