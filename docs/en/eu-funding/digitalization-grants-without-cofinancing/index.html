Are there digitalization grants with no own contribution? The answer on 18 November 2026

No, not for money: on 18 November 2026 every open call asks for a share from the company, and 100% remains only in EDIH services, in closed calls and in guides not yet launched.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

On 18 November 2026 no open call pays 100% of a digitalization project for a company that already operates: Action 2.2 call 2 of the Centru Regional Programme, open for submission from 02.12.2026 to 02.02.2027, gives EUR 15,000–200,000 but at most 90% of the eligible value, so at least 10% own contribution (guide 3.4, 5.4, 5.5); SME Eco-Tech gives a grant of at most 40%, capped at RON 267,240, only alongside a bank loan of at least 60%; and the only support with no leu from the company is the services of the EDIH hubs — assessment, testing, training — which are services, not money. Start-Up Nation session 2 gives at most 90%, but only to new firms founded by graduates of the entrepreneurship course, whose enrolment closes on 27.11.2026 at 20:00. The 100% figure that circulated in 2026 came from POCIDIF Action 2.1 (guide 3.4: de minimis 100%, newly established micro and small firms 100%, regional aid 40–75%), a call closed on 30.10.2026 at 17:00, open only to firms with an ICT CAEN code and to projects from EUR 200,000.

Verified on 18 November 2026 against the Centru call 2 guide, the consolidated SME Eco-Tech procedure, the MEDAT communiqués on Start-Up Nation, the POCIDIF 2.1 call page, the Action 6.1 EDIH guide and the fngcimm.ro page. Northdan Soft builds the software and its acceptance documents; we do not write the funding application and we do not promise approval.

How we help

The full amount, not just the headline percentage

Before submission you see everything that leaves the company account: the share not covered by intensity, the ineligible items, VAT, and the cash tied up until reimbursement.

Costs grounded on the Centru grid

In Centru call 2, criterion 2.3 (eliminatory, maximum 6 points) gives up to 2 points for costs backed by at least three verifiable sources and up to 4 for a plan that classifies expenses correctly as eligible or ineligible; we separate eligible from ineligible line by line so the budget passes verification.

A supplier offer, not a file we also wrote

We issue the technical offer only as a supplier, never for a funding application we also drafted: the offer stays independent and the company can compare it with other suppliers.

What each open call asks of the company on 18 November 2026

PR Centru, Action 2.2, call 2: de minimis, EUR 15,000–200,000, at most 90%, so at least 10% from the company. Non-ICT SMEs from Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș and Sibiu may apply, with at least one full fiscal year, net or operating profit above zero and at least one employee (guide 3.4, 5.4, 5.5).

SME Eco-Tech, until 30.12.2026 at 20:00: grant of at most 40%, maximum RON 267,240, only with a bank loan of at least 60% and a project of at least RON 668,100. Software qualifies up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT (implementation procedure, definitions and 3.2 lit. l).

Start-Up Nation session 2: at most 90%, up to RON 250,000, only for new firms of young people who finish the course; course enrolment closes on 27.11.2026. Centru intervention 1.4.3 ITI, open until 16.12.2026, also requires at least 10% and is a productive call with software only as a capped add-on, so not a digitalization route.

Minimum own contribution loses points in Centru call 2

Zero co-financing is not only unavailable, the minimum also costs points. At grid criterion 3.1 (Annex 4), 10–15% inclusive earns 1 point out of 12, above 15% up to 20% earns 4, above 20% up to 25% earns 8, and above 25% earns 12.

Cash flow adds to the money: pre-financing comes in tranches of at most 10% (Annex 15), and a reimbursement request has a minimum of RON 100,000 (guide, ch. 12). The contribution mechanics, its proof and VAT are explained on the page about co-financing a digitalization project.

Where 100% appears in 2026 and why it does not suit a digitalization

POCIDIF 2.1 had 100% only on de minimis aid and on newly established micro and small firms (guide 3.4, MIPE Order 965/23.06.2026), for ICT CAEN codes and projects from EUR 200,000; it closed on 30.10.2026. In the Bucharest-Ilfov Regional Programme, the draft guide for Action 1.1 Proof of Concept proposes, at 3.4, “maximum 100% of the eligible value” within the de minimis ceiling, with a budget of EUR 5,000,000. On 18.11.2026 it was still listed under draft guides in consultation, not launched, and it funds an innovative conceptual model at TRL 4–5, not buying or implementing a system for the business.

In Sud-Muntenia, 100% is only the authority's current proposal for the innovation pilot call PRSM/1083: research and innovation, CAEN 72, a product at TRL 4 or above; the pre-selection questionnaire had a deadline of 20.11.2026.

EDIH services are financed 100% for the company, but are recorded as state aid, up to EUR 220,000 per undertaking over three fiscal years (guide 6.1, 3.13). The company applies to the hub's own call, for example eDIH-DIZ IMM, open since 05.10.2026. The hub, not the company, applies to POCIDIF 6.1: six hubs submitted by 30.06.2026, and DIGIVEST by 30.12.2026 at 17:00 (guide 6.1, 4.3.2).

What the company pays even at maximum intensity

Ineligible costs first. In Centru call 2 they include second-hand goods, maintenance and running costs, and inventory items under RON 2,500 per unit are not reimbursed (5.3.3). Nothing involving a firm order is made before submission; the exceptions are application consultancy, the digitalization plan and the initial audit.

Then VAT, eligible only when it cannot be recovered, and cash flow: the company pays its suppliers, then claims reimbursement. The rule of 20% of aid for own staff belongs to POCIDIF 2.1 only and does not apply in Centru.

SME Eco-Tech: grant tied to a loan, software capped

The mechanism is unambiguous: a grant of up to 40% of eligible costs, maximum RON 267,240 (CHF 50,000), the rest through a loan at a partner bank. The thresholds in lei: a minimum project of RON 668,100 in eligible costs, an investment loan of at least RON 400,860. Above the threshold the grant ceiling does not rise, so the covered share falls.

For a digital project three limits matter: software qualifies up to RON 15,000 excluding VAT, applications are filed until 30.12.2026 at 20:00, and at least 40% of eligible costs must go to green equipment (renewable energy, waste collection or recycling, resource saving), with the digital package not counting toward that threshold, so the programme does not suit a digital-only project. The bank may refuse the loan after acceptance into the programme. Separately from SME Eco-Tech, the FNGCIMM POCIDIF grant-plus-guarantee instrument listed no partner bank on 18.11.2026.

The EUR 20,000–100,000 band with 10% is PNRR C9, closed

The EUR 20,000–100,000 band with a 10% own contribution belongs to the PNRR C9 SME digitalization call, whose applications closed in 2023. Pages published in 2026 still present it as a current offer. Only the execution deadlines remain: implementation by 31.07.2026 (MIPE Order 964/2026) and payment of the aid by MIPE until 30.10.2026 (Order 963/2026), both now past.

A quick test for any page with a figure: look for the number of the order that approves the guide and the date of verification.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a 100% funded digitalization call in 2026?⌄

No, on 18.11.2026. Centru call 2 gives at most 90%, SME Eco-Tech at most 40% plus a bank loan, Start-Up Nation session 2 at most 90%. POCIDIF 2.1, which had 100% on de minimis, closed on 30.10.2026, and guides proposing 100% are not launched.

What can I get for digitalization without paying anything?⌄

Services, not money: the EDIH hubs offer assessment, testing and training, financed 100% but recorded as state aid up to EUR 220,000 per undertaking over three fiscal years. The company applies to the hub's call. Custom software development stays at the company's own cost.

How much do I put in at Centru call 2?⌄

At least 10% of the eligible value, plus everything ineligible and VAT if it is recoverable. The 10–15% step earns only 1 point out of 12 at criterion 3.1; above 25% earns 12. Pre-financing comes in tranches of at most 10%, so cash flow matters too.

If the intensity is 100%, do I really pay nothing?⌄

No. Ineligible costs needed for implementation are borne by the beneficiary, and a cost becomes eligible only after actual payment during the implementation period. Intensity covers the eligible cost, not cash flow: the company pays suppliers and recovers later.

Why does the EUR 20,000–100,000 offer with 10% contribution keep circulating?⌄

Because it belongs to the PNRR C9 SME digitalization call, whose applications closed in 2023, republished on pages never updated. Only execution deadlines remain: implementation by 31.07.2026 and payment of the aid by 30.10.2026, both past.
