Do digitalization grants without co-financing exist? The honest answer

Searching for 100% non-repayable funding is legitimate, but the reality of Romanian schemes is different: the own contribution is the rule, not the exception.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

Here is the frontal answer to the question in the title: digitalization funding that demands no financial effort of your own is practically absent from Romania's current landscape, and anyone promising otherwise is selling you something. Even the most generous recent call — PNRR C9, with grants of EUR 20,000–100,000 — imposed an own contribution of at least 10%, on top of which VAT and ineligible costs inevitably landed on the company. Under the Swiss SME Eco-Tech scheme, open until September 2026, the ratio is flipped outright: support stops at 40% and the company carries the rest. The only form of support where you pay nothing for the "funding" itself is the subsidised service offer of the EDIH hubs — but there you receive advisory, testing and training at reduced cost, not money. Fully free training did exist through PNRR C7 I19, yet that call is closed and in implementation. The moral for your company's budget: always plan an own contribution. Northdan Soft helps you size it correctly, with budgets that separate, in black and white, the eligible, the ineligible and the taxes.

Why the schemes require an own contribution

Co-financing is not bureaucratic malice but a natural-selection mechanism: a company putting in its own money picks projects it genuinely needs and sees them through. Schemes without a contribution have historically attracted speculative applications and abandoned implementations.

The current benchmarks confirm the trend: the minimum 10% under the former PNRR C9 was the friendly exception, while at SME Eco-Tech the company covers at least 60% of the investment. Future guides — Start-Up Nation session 2, the announced PTJ calls — will publish their own thresholds at launch.

The partial exceptions and how to use them

EDIH services remain the closest thing to "free": subsidised technology advisory, testing and training, without a cash grant. They are excellent as a preparation stage — you clarify which technology you need before investing in it.

The free training under PNRR C7 I19 proved such windows appear occasionally but close fast — that call has long been in its implementation phase. The lesson: windows with zero own effort are caught only through constant monitoring of the official sources.

Frequently asked questions

Is any 100% non-repayable digitalization grant available at this moment?⌄

At the verification of 28 July 2026, no — both schemes with active submissions assume consistent own effort: SME Eco-Tech finances at most 40%, and POCIDIF 2.1 applies the intensities from its own guide. Even at high intensities, VAT and the ineligible items remain the company's.

What can I get for digitalization without paying anything?⌄

Services, not money: the EDIH hubs offer subsidised advisory, technology testing and training. For actual software development there will always be an own-cost component — either co-financing on a grant or direct investment.

How do I reduce the own effort if I cannot eliminate it?⌄

Three levers work: choose the scheme with the highest intensity you are genuinely eligible for, split the implementation into modules to spread payments over time, and finance the contribution through a loan — possibly guaranteed under the scheme run by FNGCIMM, Romania's national SME credit guarantee fund, if the call's guide permits it.
