Digital skills training under the PEO programme: what the call reimbursed

The PEO digital skills call reimbursed courses through unit costs, not software invoices, and submissions closed on 15 November 2024 after two extensions.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

In the PEO call "Digital skills for the labour market", initiation, improvement and specialisation courses were reimbursed at RON 24.81 per course hour for each certified trainee, qualifications at levels 2, 3 and 4 at RON 3,002.18, 4,971.91 and 9,635.63 per participant, ERDF-type costs including IT equipment were capped at 15% of eligible direct costs, and indirect costs were paid as a fixed 15% of direct staff costs (specific-conditions guide, 5.3.2 and 5.3.5). MySMIS2021 opened on 28 May 2024, and the original 23 August 2024 deadline was moved by two corrigenda: to 27 September, then to 15 November 2024 at 16:00; the final lists of selected projects appeared on 16 June 2025. Only authorised continuing vocational training providers could apply (5.1.2), and training the beneficiary's own employees was not eligible (3.7). Checked on 18 November 2026 against the guide, both corrigenda and the call page on mfe.gov.ro.

The boundary between training and software does not depend on any single call. The angle we look from is that of a software vendor: the most effective digital training happens on the tools employees actually use every day, and when we implement a system, user instruction is part of delivery. Northdan Soft is not an authorised training provider and does not issue recognised certificates.

Cost lines in the guide (5.3.2 and 5.3.5)

Unit costs: the RON 24.81 per programmed course hour per certified trainee covers the course fee, assessors, trainers, trainees' transport and accommodation, rooms, equipment and teaching materials, plus a RON 5 per hour trainee allowance. Transport and accommodation therefore cannot be claimed separately for these courses, and management and indirect costs are excluded. Qualification unit costs (RON 3,002.18 at level 2, 4,971.91 at level 3, 9,635.63 at level 4, per participant) cover everything except participation costs (transport, accommodation, meals, allowances) and project management.

Real costs: management and implementation staff, travel, specialised services, organising events and courses, certification fees, materials, meals, rent, leasing and trainee allowances of at most RON 5 per hour, repayable on drop-out except for reasons not attributable to the trainee.

Caps: ERDF-type costs, IT equipment included, could not exceed 15% of eligible direct costs, and indirect costs were a fixed 15% of direct staff costs (Article 54(b) of Regulation 2021/1060). Initial assessment (A.1) and training (A.2) together had to make at least 50% of the eligible budget (5.2.3).

The parallel route under the recovery plan shows the same boundary: under investment C7 I19, de minimis aid of at most EUR 17,000 per SME was granted exclusively as training programmes, with implementation ending on 31 December 2025.

Where a software vendor legitimately fits

The guide (5.3.2) lists "development of applications and IT systems intended for activities with the target group" under consultancy and expertise services, and "licences and software" among consumables. Software could therefore enter the budget, but bought by the training provider for its own course or assessment, for example a course platform. It was not a company's own system. This is our reading; confirmation comes from the funding contract of the project concerned.

At contracting, the beneficiary submitted the list of equipment and platforms with a justification note and a declaration that it would not buy the same type of platform for the same purpose from two projects (7.6).

What followed after 15 November 2024

The final lists of selected projects appeared on 16 June 2025, and the projects are in implementation. A successor-type call, PEO STEP-LLL (Priority 11, MIPE Order 767/2026, EUR 92 million, national, for training providers, employment-service providers, union and employer confederations, sectoral associations and research institutes), ran from 29 May to 30 September 2026 and is closed. It covers training in critical technologies, so it is not a digital skills call by name; we do not describe its cost rules because we have not read its guide.

The indirect route depends on the call you file under. PR Centru 2.2 call 2 pays EUR 658.63 per employee who completes a certified programme of at least 12 hours on the IT solution in the project, with submission from 2 December 2026 to 2 February 2027, for non-ICT SMEs in the Centru region only (details on that call's page). Under SME Eco-Tech, open until 30 December 2026, staff training is ineligible. The conclusion: it depends on the call, neither guaranteed nor excluded.

Training and software: how they complement each other

The scenario that works: the company implements a new system — stock, production, sales — and employees go through structured training on that system and on general digital skills. The software reaches its potential, and the people gain certified, transferable abilities.

We naturally cover the first part and the applied instruction on the systems we deliver; for certified courses under PEO-type schemes we work with authorised training providers, each with their own deliverables and documents.

Double funding is not only the same invoice claimed twice — it is also the same hour of the same person claimed from two projects. The separation is made on five verifiable elements: the contract, the invoice, the timetable, the attendance list and the content delivered. The call's target group had its own rules (3.7): at least 500 trainees per project, aged 18 to 64, excluding employees of public institutions and public health and education bodies, and ISCED levels 0–2. The corrigendum of 24 September 2024 also removed from the target group people in the "Ține pasul" calls who receive digital skills training, "to avoid double funding".

Frequently asked questions

When did the PEO Digital skills call close?⌄

On 15 November 2024 at 16:00. MySMIS2021 opened on 28 May 2024, and the original deadline of 23 August 2024 was extended by the corrigendum of 13 August 2024 to 27 September, then by the one of 24 September 2024 to 15 November. The final lists of selected projects appeared on 16 June 2025.

How much did the PEO call reimburse per course hour?⌄

RON 24.81 per programmed course hour for each certified trainee, on authorised initiation, improvement or specialisation courses (GO 129/2000). The sum includes RON 5 per hour for the trainee and covers the course fee, trainers, room, materials, transport and accommodation; it excludes project management and indirect costs (guide 5.3.5).

Could a company train its own employees through this call?⌄

No. Only authorised continuing vocational training providers could apply (guide 5.1.2), and target-group members could not be employed by the beneficiary or the partner (3.7). A company took part by sending its employees, aged 18 to 64, to the courses of a provider that had won a project.

Which digital training funding is open right now?⌄

No PEO call for training employees was open on 18 November 2026: STEP-LLL closed on 30 September 2026. As a related line in an investment project, PR Centru 2.2 call 2 pays EUR 658.63 per certified employee, with submission from 2 December 2026 to 2 February 2027; under SME Eco-Tech training is ineligible.

Can I combine a digitalization project with a training project?⌄

Yes, under one strict condition: the same cost cannot be reimbursed from two sources. The software system comes from one project, the certified training from another, with distinct deliverables and documents — a boundary we build explicitly into our contracts.

Does Northdan run certified courses funded through PEO?⌄

We are not an authorised vocational training provider — we deliver applied instruction on the software systems we implement, plus the accompanying materials. For certified courses under training schemes we work with authorised providers, keeping the deliverables separate.
