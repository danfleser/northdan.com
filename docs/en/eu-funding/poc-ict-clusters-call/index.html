POC Action 2.2.1, ICT clusters: three calls between 2015 and 2020, each one's amounts and what followed

POC 2.2.1 ran three calls for innovative ICT products by SMEs, all closed, with different bands (RON 3.24M; EUR 0.5-3.5M; EUR 1-5M); the line continues in POCIDIF 2.1, which also closed on 30 October 2026.

The programme's official applicant guide
ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

POC Action 2.2.1 financed ICT SMEs and consortia of SMEs from ICT clusters through three calls, all closed: 2015-2016, with a cap of RON 3,240,000 in state aid plus de minimis; 2019, with EUR 500,000-3,500,000; 2020, with EUR 1,000,000-5,000,000. Checked on 18 November 2026 against the guides of the three calls published by OIPSI and the action's page on mfe.gov.ro.

The action's official page gives an intensity of 20-80% of eligible costs for investment, industrial research, experimental development and innovation, differentiated by activity, company size and region; de minimis aid was financed at 100%, but capped at EUR 200,000 and at 20% of the project's eligible costs. The call no longer accepts projects: POC is the 2014-2020 programme. We document the page because its logic carried on in POCIDIF Action 2.1, the call that closed on 30 October 2026, where the stake is the same — products of a firm's own, not services delivered for others — but the ceilings, the CAEN codes and the scoring grid are different. Northdan Soft looks at both calls from the position of software supplier and technical partner, not funding consultant.

How we help

✓The bands of the three calls, from the guides

RON 3,240,000 plus de minimis (2015-2016), EUR 500,000-3,500,000 (2019), EUR 1,000,000-5,000,000 (2020); de minimis at 100%, no more than EUR 200,000 and no more than 20% of eligible costs.

✓Status stated in the first sentence

The call is closed and will not relaunch under POC. Anyone seeking funding for an ICT product needs the 2021-2027 line, not this action's archive.

✓The real differences from the successor

POCIDIF 2.1 changes the ceiling, requires authorised CAEN codes from the rev.3 nomenclature and adds a scoring threshold. We set them side by side, with each one's figures.

What the action financed and who could apply

A project qualified if it led to developing ranges of ICT products and services applicable across the rest of the Romanian economy, for the vertical integration of ICT solutions, or to strategic innovative projects with an impact on the whole ICT industry nationally or internationally. The official page is explicit about the ambition: the aim was the move from outsourcing to innovation-driven development, plus collaboration between cluster-type structures in the ICT industry.

Eligible applicants were SMEs operating in Romania, centred on the ICT field or active within ICT-centred clusters, as well as consortia — partnerships — of SMEs from such clusters. Support covered investments in tangible and intangible assets within an initial investment, industrial research and experimental development, SME innovation projects, and process and organisational innovation. CAEN codes required in calls 2 and 3 (rev.2, main or secondary, only the lead in a consortium): 2611, 2612, 2630, 6201, 6202, 6203, 6209, 7211, 7219. Cluster membership had to be proven "where applicable", so an ICT-centred firm could apply without it.

De minimis aid was a separate category with its own regime: market research, information and publicity, consultancy, permits, authorisations, training, audit, management, subscriptions. It was financed in full, but with a double cap — EUR 200,000 and 20% of the project's eligible costs. That structure, a small de minimis attached to a large aid amount, reappears in the call that followed. In call 2 the guide capped personnel costs at 50%, buildings and land at 20%, overheads at 15% of personnel and consultancy at 10%.

The three calls: windows, allocations, amounts

Call 1: online registration from 2 November 2015 to 29 February 2016, 16:30 (after an extension); the OIPSI list holds 235 proposals. Cap of RON 3,240,000 for state aid plus de minimis (at most EUR 200,000 over three fiscal years, at most 20%), up to 36 months; an SME could be funded in one project only. The action had EUR 59.7 million in public funds, of which EUR 50 million ERDF.

Call 2: competitive, time-limited, 60 days in MySMIS2014, registrations from 30 January to 29 April 2019; 223 projects were submitted. Allocation of EUR 29,062,500 (EUR 25 million ERDF: 18.75 million less developed regions, 6.25 million more developed ones), EUR 500,000-3,500,000 plus de minimis, up to 36 months. Scheme: MFE Order 952/20.09.2018 (MCSI 781/24.09.2018). Figures as at launch.

Call 3: from 2 October 2020, 09:00, to 2 November 2020, 18:00, in MySMIS2014 (30 days). Allocation of EUR 13,691,758.06 (ERDF and state budget), with a possible overrun up to EUR 50,000,000 on the schemes' budget; EUR 1,000,000-5,000,000 (the minimum includes de minimis), up to 24 months. Schemes: MFE Orders 1093/11.09.2020 (state aid) and 1092/11.09.2020 (de minimis). Figures as at launch.

Listings that give "EUR 500,000-3,500,000" as the action's band describe call 2 only.

Why this call's figures cannot move onto a 2026 dossier

On programme lists recompiled year after year, 2014-2020 calls still appear next to open ones. POC ended with its programming period, and a figure taken from it into today's budget produces a project mis-sized from day one.

The concrete difference: under POCIDIF Action 2.1 the non-reimbursable ceiling for a project whose result is an innovative software solution is EUR 1,500,000, and the EUR 3,000,000 figure applies only to projects whose result is an innovative hardware product manufactured by the beneficiary. The minimum is EUR 200,000, against EUR 500,000 under call 2 and EUR 1,000,000 under call 3. A firm budgeting from the old bands (up to EUR 3.5 or 5 million) gets it wrong in both directions.

The legal reference changes too: for the POCIDIF 2.1 call the reference document is the applicant guide approved by MIPE Order no. 965 of 23 June 2026, and the state aid and de minimis scheme by OMIPE no. 875 of 15 June 2026. Any figure not found there has no place in a funding application.

The successor: POCIDIF Action 2.1, closed on 30 October 2026

Submission opened on 30 June 2026 at 15:00 and closed on 30 October 2026 at 17:00, after an extension by MIPE Order no. 1500/29.09.2026, through MySMIS2021, with a call allocation of EUR 48,813,278 from the ERDF and the state budget. Approval requires a minimum of 70 points, and the excellence threshold is 92: projects reaching it enter contracting in descending score order, after all projects are evaluated, up to 50% of the allocation (Order 1500/29.09.2026) — the submission date no longer decides the queue.

The entry restriction is the authorised CAEN code, in the rev.3 nomenclature: 2611, 2612, 2630, 5821, 5829, 6210, 6220, 6290 or 6310. That is the exact opposite of the rule on internal-digitalization calls, where some ICT codes sit on an exclusion list; lists do not transfer between programmes. Implementation runs to at most 24 months and does not pass 31 December 2029.

One limit we state before any offer: the POCIDIF 2.1 guide forbids fully outsourcing the technical activities, and at least 20% of the non-reimbursable assistance allocated to the core activity must go to the beneficiary's own staff. We cannot be your entire technical team on this call, however convenient the contract would be — and it is better to hear that from the supplier than from the evaluator.

POC 2.2.1 and Action 2.2.1 of POCIDIF

POCIDIF 2021-2027 also has an Action 2.2.1, "E-government in public administration and institutions" (Measures 1-3), but for public institutions, not firms. Same number, different programme: it has no connection to the POC 2.2.1 described here.

Frequently asked questions

What amounts did POC Action 2.2.1 grant?

It depended on the call: the first capped state aid plus de minimis at RON 3,240,000; the second granted EUR 500,000-3,500,000; the third EUR 1,000,000-5,000,000. Intensity was 20-80%, and de minimis 100%, but capped at EUR 200,000 and at 20% of eligible costs.

Can I still submit a project under POC 2.2.1?

No. The action belongs to POC 2014-2020 and that programming period has ended; the official page announces no window. The successor, POCIDIF Action 2.1, no longer accepts projects either: it closed on 30 October 2026. No line of this kind is open at the moment.

Who could apply under this action?

SMEs operating in Romania, centred on the ICT field or active within ICT-centred clusters, as well as consortia — partnerships — of micro, small and medium enterprises from such clusters. Individual projects and partnership projects shared the same funding framework.

How does POCIDIF 2.1 differ from the POC 2.2.1 calls on the money side?

The minimum drops to EUR 200,000 (call 2 required EUR 500,000, call 3 EUR 1,000,000), and the maximum for a project whose result is a software solution is EUR 1,500,000; the EUR 3,000,000 figure applies only to projects whose result is an innovative hardware product the beneficiary manufactures, with the part above 1.5 million justified by production-line financing.

Where do I find the POC 2.2.1 guides and the successor's?

The guides of the three POC 2.2.1 calls are on each call's OIPSI page, and the action's presentation is on mfe.gov.ro. For POCIDIF 2.1, the guide was approved by MIPE Order no. 965/23.06.2026 and amended by Order no. 1500/29.09.2026; submission in MySMIS2021 is closed.

How many calls did POC 2.2.1 have and when?

Three: November 2015 to February 2016 (235 proposals), 30 January to 29 April 2019 (223 projects submitted) and 2 October to 2 November 2020. Each had a different funding band, allocation and maximum project duration, which is why the action has no single amount.

Is POC 2.2.1 the same as Action 2.2.1 in POCIDIF?

No. POC 2.2.1 financed innovative ICT products by SMEs in 2014-2020. Action 2.2.1 in POCIDIF 2021-2027 is "E-government in public administration and institutions" (Measures 1-3), for public institutions, not firms. They share a number but are different programmes with different beneficiaries.

Which CAEN codes did POC 2.2.1 require?

In calls 2 and 3 the guides required, in rev.2, one of the codes 2611, 2612, 2630, 6201, 6202, 6203, 6209, 7211 or 7219, as main or secondary activity; in a consortium, only the lead. The successor POCIDIF 2.1 uses different codes, from the rev.3 nomenclature.
