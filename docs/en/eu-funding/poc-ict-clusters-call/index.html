POC Action 2.2.1, ICT clusters: funding between EUR 500,000 and 3,500,000, closed with the 2014-2020 period

The funding band of the historic call for innovative ICT products was EUR 500,000 to 3,500,000, with de minimis capped at EUR 200,000; the line moved to POCIDIF Action 2.1, where a software result is capped at EUR 1.5 million.

The programme's official applicant guide
ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

Non-reimbursable funding under POC Action 2.2.1 ranged from EUR 500,000 to EUR 3,500,000, according to the action's official page on the site of the Ministry of Investments and European Projects, checked on 30 July 2026. Intensity ran from 20% to 80% of total eligible costs for investment, industrial research, experimental development and innovation activities, differentiated by activity, company size and region of implementation, while the de minimis component was financed at 100% but capped at EUR 200,000 and at 20% of the project's total eligible costs. The call no longer accepts projects: POC belongs to the 2014-2020 programming period and the action lists no open window. We document the page because its logic survived intact in POCIDIF Action 2.1, the call open until 30 September 2026, where the stake is the same — products of a firm's own, not services delivered for others — but the ceilings, the CAEN codes and the scoring grid are different. Northdan Soft looks at both calls from the position of software supplier and technical partner, not funding consultant.

How we help

The funding band, read off the action's own page

EUR 500,000 to 3,500,000, intensity of 20-80% on investment and research activities, de minimis at 100% but no more than EUR 200,000 and no more than 20% of eligible costs.

Status stated in the headline, not in a footnote

The call is closed and will not relaunch under POC. Anyone seeking funding for an ICT product needs the 2021-2027 line, not this action's archive.

The real differences from the successor

POCIDIF 2.1 changes the ceiling, requires authorised CAEN codes from the rev.3 nomenclature and adds a scoring threshold. We set them side by side, with each one's figures.

Technical documents for today's dossier

As a software supplier we prepare the specifications, the offer by functionality and the budget by eligible cost category for the project submitted on the line open now.

What the action financed and who could apply

A project qualified if it led to developing ranges of ICT products and services applicable across the rest of the Romanian economy, for the vertical integration of ICT solutions, or to strategic innovative projects with an impact on the whole ICT industry nationally or internationally. The official page is explicit about the ambition: the aim was the move from outsourcing to innovation-driven development, plus collaboration between cluster-type structures in the ICT industry.

Eligible applicants were SMEs operating in Romania, centred on the ICT field or active within ICT-centred clusters, as well as consortia — partnerships — of SMEs from such clusters. Support covered investments in tangible and intangible assets within an initial investment, industrial research and experimental development, SME innovation projects, and process and organisational innovation.

De minimis aid was a separate category with its own regime: market research, information and publicity, consultancy, permits, authorisations, training, audit, management, subscriptions. It was financed in full, but with a double cap — EUR 200,000 and 20% of the project's eligible costs. That structure, a small de minimis attached to a large aid amount, reappears in the call that followed.

Why this call's figures cannot move onto a 2026 dossier

On programme lists recompiled year after year, 2014-2020 calls still appear next to open ones, as if they were alternative routes for the same buyer. They are not: POC ended with its programming period, and a figure taken from it into today's budget produces a project mis-sized from day one.

The concrete difference: under POCIDIF Action 2.1 the non-reimbursable ceiling for a project whose result is an innovative software solution is EUR 1,500,000, and the EUR 3,000,000 figure applies only to projects whose result is an innovative hardware product manufactured by the beneficiary. The minimum is EUR 200,000, not 500,000. A firm budgeting from the old 3.5 million band gets it wrong in both directions.

The legal reference changes too: for the call open now the reference document is the applicant guide approved by MIPE Order no. 965 of 23 June 2026, and the state aid and de minimis scheme by OMIPE no. 875 of 15 June 2026. Any figure not found there has no place in a funding application.

The successor open now: POCIDIF Action 2.1

Submission opened on 30 June 2026 at 15:00 and closes on 30 September 2026 at 17:00, through MySMIS2021, with a call allocation of EUR 48,813,278 from the ERDF and the state budget. Approval requires a minimum of 70 points, and the excellence threshold is 92: projects reaching it enter contracting in submission order, up to 50% of the allocation — so for a very strong project the submission date decides the queue.

The entry restriction is the authorised CAEN code, in the rev.3 nomenclature: 2611, 2612, 2630, 5821, 5829, 6210, 6220, 6290 or 6310. That is the exact opposite of the rule on internal-digitalization calls, where some ICT codes sit on an exclusion list; lists do not transfer between programmes. Implementation runs to at most 24 months and does not pass 31 December 2029.

One limit we state before any offer: the POCIDIF 2.1 guide forbids fully outsourcing the technical activities, and at least 20% of the non-reimbursable assistance allocated to the core activity must go to the beneficiary's own staff. We cannot be your entire technical team on this call, however convenient the contract would be — and it is better to hear that from the supplier than from the evaluator.

What we deliver on a software product project

Product specifications, a technical offer broken down by functionality, a budget by eligible cost category, the supply contract or pre-contract, then the development itself and the documents required at each reimbursement claim. We do not draft the funding application and we do not submit in MySMIS; if you work with a consultant on the administrative side, we coordinate with them directly.

For sizing we use the bands published in our own cost guides rather than percentages of the grant: a commercially launchable SaaS platform sits indicatively in the EUR 15,000-60,000 band, and a mature product with full billing, roles and integrations in EUR 60,000-150,000 and above. The firm figure follows an analysis stage, and the offer is written at a value we can actually honour at contracting.

Frequently asked questions

What amounts did POC Action 2.2.1 grant?⌄

Non-reimbursable funding ran between EUR 500,000 and EUR 3,500,000, at an intensity of 20-80% on investment, industrial research, experimental development and innovation activities. The de minimis component was granted at 100%, but capped at EUR 200,000 and at 20% of the project's eligible costs.

Can I still submit a project under POC 2.2.1?⌄

No. The action belongs to the Competitiveness Operational Programme 2014-2020 and that programming period has ended; the action's official page announces no submission window. The current route for an SME with an ICT CAEN code and a product idea is POCIDIF Action 2.1.

Who could apply under this action?⌄

SMEs operating in Romania, centred on the ICT field or active within ICT-centred clusters, as well as consortia — partnerships — of micro, small and medium enterprises from such clusters. Individual projects and partnership projects shared the same funding framework.

How does POCIDIF 2.1 differ on the money side?⌄

The minimum drops from EUR 500,000 to EUR 200,000, and the maximum for a project whose result is a software solution is EUR 1,500,000; the EUR 3,000,000 figure applies only to projects whose result is an innovative hardware product manufactured by the beneficiary, with the part above 1.5 million justified by financing the production line.

Where do I find the official document for the call open now?⌄

The applicant guide for POCIDIF Action 2.1 was approved by MIPE Order no. 965 of 23 June 2026, and the aid scheme by OMIPE no. 875 of 15 June 2026; both download from the ministry's site. Submission is exclusively through MySMIS2021, until 30 September 2026 at 17:00.
