POC Action 2.2.1: the historic call for innovative ICT products

The call that pushed Romanian ICT firms "from outsourcing to innovation-driven development" is history — but its direct successor, POCIDIF 2.1, accepts projects until 30 September 2026.

"From outsourcing to innovation-driven development" was the declared ambition behind POC Action 2.2.1 — full name: "Supporting the growth of value added generated by the ICT sector and of innovation in the field through cluster development". Between 2014 and 2020 it was the main instrument through which SMEs in Romania's ICT sector received grants to build products of their own. The call ran in several rounds: in 2017, then in May–June 2018, and the final window was a continuous call open from 17 December 2018 to 14 February 2019. Today it is definitively closed, like the whole POC programme, and the financed projects are complete. We document it here because its logic lives on: POCIDIF Action 2.1, the call currently open until 30 September 2026, is its direct descendant in the 2021-2027 period, with the same stake — innovative software products developed by ICT firms, not services delivered for others. Northdan Soft looks at both calls from the same position: that of a supplier and technical development partner, not a funding consultant.

How we help

The truth about the call's status

POC 2.2.1 no longer accepts projects and will not be relaunched — we tell you so from the headline, so you shift your attention straight to the line that took its place.

Lessons from a call that worked

The 2.2.1 rounds showed what set winning files apart: a product idea with transferable know-how, not a mere shopping list of IT purchases — a conclusion equally valid on POCIDIF 2.1.

The bridge to the call open now

We show exactly where this funding line moved: POCIDIF Action 2.1, with submissions open until 30 September 2026 for SMEs holding an ICT CAEN code.

Technical documents for the new file

As a software supplier we prepare the technical offer and the budget by eligible cost category for your project on the successor line, then actually build the product.

The call's history: three rounds for the ICT sector

Action 2.2.1 was reserved for SMEs in the ICT sector and for ICT clusters, with calls managed by the POC managing authority and the intermediate body OIPSI. The first round ran in 2017, the second in May–June 2018, and the third took the form of a continuous call open from 17 December 2018 to 14 February 2019.

Grants reached, per the official applicant guides, the order of millions of euros per project, with an implementation period of at most 36 months. The exact conditions varied from one round to the next and remain available in the official POC managing authority archive, at the link in this call's fact sheet.

What it financed and the lesson it left

The call's declared aim was to move the Romanian ICT sector "from outsourcing to innovation-driven development": the money went to developing innovative ICT products, services and applications with transferable know-how, not to delivering bespoke services. Firms had to demonstrate that the resulting product had market value of its own.

The lesson that matters for any future file: the evaluation rewarded the product, not the company. A credible architecture, a realistic 36-month development plan and a demonstrable innovation component weighed more than turnover. It is exactly the frame of mind with which POCIDIF 2.1 is approached today.

The successor open now: POCIDIF Action 2.1

The funding line for innovative software products did not disappear — it moved into POCIDIF 2021-2027. Action 2.1, again dedicated to SMEs with an ICT CAEN code, accepts projects until 30 September 2026 and finances the development of new services, applications and products through innovation and advanced technologies: AI, IoT, AR/VR, SaaS platforms.

If you have a product idea that would have fitted POC 2.2.1, today it is submitted on POCIDIF 2.1. Northdan Soft joins such projects as the technical partner: we build the product, prepare the technical offer and budget for the file and deliver the documents needed at each reimbursement — the funding application itself stays with you or your consultant.

Frequently asked questions

Can I still submit a project under POC 2.2.1?⌄

No — the last submission window closed on 14 February 2019, and the POC 2014-2020 programme has ended entirely. The current alternative for ICT SMEs with product ideas is POCIDIF Action 2.1, open until 30 September 2026.

What kind of projects did POC 2.2.1 actually finance?⌄

The development of innovative ICT products, services and applications with transferable know-how, by SMEs in the ICT sector and ICT clusters — with grants that reached, per the official applicant guides, the order of millions of euros and implementation of at most 36 months.

How does POCIDIF 2.1 resemble the old 2.2.1 call?⌄

In its core logic: both are reserved for ICT SMEs and finance the development of a software product of the company's own, not the purchase of software for internal use. POCIDIF 2.1 adds an emphasis on advanced technologies — AI, IoT, AR/VR — and its own calendar, with a 30 September 2026 submission deadline.

If I want to apply on the POCIDIF 2.1 successor, how does Northdan Soft help?⌄

With everything belonging to the technical side of the file and the project: the detailed technical offer by functionality, the budget by eligible cost category, then the actual development of the product. We do not write funding applications — for the administrative part you work with a consultant, with whom we coordinate directly.
