The 2026 EU funding calendar for companies: fixed deadlines and estimated dates

Every known deadline at 18 November 2026, in chronological order, with the source of each date — what is open now, what opens with a published window and what has closed, plus the SME calls that the ministry's calendar estimates up to May 2027.

ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

Romania's 2026 EU funding calendar is published by the Ministry of Investments and European Projects (MIPE) as an interactive register rebuilt almost weekly from MySMIS2021, and for an existing company the first firm deadlines at 18 November 2026 are 16 December (Centru 1.4.3, ITI areas) and 30 December 2026 (SME Eco-Tech). The nearest closing hour is 27 November at 20:00, for enrolment in the Start-Up Nation courses, which are aimed at people aged 18 to 30, not at existing companies. MEDAT, Romania's ministry for entrepreneurship, extended SME Eco-Tech from 24 September. The newest launched window is call 2 of Action 2.2 in the Centru programme, opening on 2 December 2026 at 12:00 and closing on 2 February 2027 at 12:00. POCIDIF Action 2.1 closed on 30 October 2026 at 17:00, after MIPE Order no. 1500/29.09.2026 moved the deadline. At European level the call DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-AI4SME stays open until 14 January 2027. Layered over these fixed dates sit the calls with only an estimated date, from the ministry's calendar: the January 2026 version lists 292 calls worth 6,617,288,234 EUR, and the update the ministry announced in May 2026 estimates about 226 calls, with over 4.8 billion EUR in total allocations and about 3.6 billion EUR of EU funds. Those are estimated months, not binding deadlines, so have the technical side of your project ready before a window opens. Calendar checked on 18 November 2026.

The deadlines of 2026–2027, in order, as at 18 November 2026

Open now: 27.11.2026, 20:00 — enrolment in the free Start-Up Nation courses closes (platform minimis.imm.gov.ro), for people aged 18 to 30; completing the course is the condition for stage two, in which companies newly set up by graduates can obtain up to 250,000 RON, at most 90% of eligible costs. 16.12.2026, 12:00 — Intervention 1.4.3 of the Centru programme closes, for SMEs with real activity in the Țara Făgărașului and Moții–Țara de Piatră ITI territories (25,000–200,000 EUR per project, budget 7,647,059 EUR). 30.12.2026, 20:00 — SME Eco-Tech closes, extended by MEDAT from 24.09.2026: a grant of at most 40% of the investment, capped at 267,240 RON, plus a bank loan of at least 60%; since August 2026 (MEDAT Order no. 1241/07.08.2026, Monitorul Oficial no. 671 of 13.08.2026) micro-enterprises no longer need a turnover of 2 million RON.

Launched, window still ahead: 02.12.2026, 12:00 – 02.02.2027, 12:00 — Centru programme, Action 2.2, call 2, “Digital enterprises for an advanced economy”: SMEs outside the ICT sector in the Centru region, 15,000–200,000 EUR per project, budget 14,113,032 EUR, final guide of 30.10.2026. 02.12.2026 – 15.01.2027, 12:00 — Centru programme, Intervention 2.1.2, call 2, a regional pilot open-innovation platform for smart cities: a partnership led by a research organisation, a single project funded, 1,764,706 EUR. 21.12.2026, 12:00 — Centru 1.5.1, call 2, business incubators and accelerators, closes; it is for incubator founders, not for an ordinary company. 14.01.2027 — the European call DIGITAL-ECCC-2027-DEPLOY-CYBER-11-AI4SME, “Strengthening cybersecurity capacities of European SMEs with cybersecure AI-powered solutions”, open since 01.09.2026 on the Funding & Tenders portal, closes; eligibility is read in the topic sheet.

Closed: POCIDIF Action 2.1, the call offering 200,000–1,500,000 EUR in non-repayable aid for innovative software solutions built by SMEs with an ICT CAEN code, allocation 48,813,278 EUR — it received applications between 30.06.2026 and 30.10.2026 at 17:00. The 2026 round of the Digital Europe Programme closed on 01.10.2026. The payment deadline for PNRR C9 projects, 30.10.2026 (Order 963/2026), has passed; implementation had ended earlier, on 30.06.2026 (MIPE Order 607/2026), or 31.07.2026 for projects that notified the extension in time (MIPE Order 964/2026). Further out: 08.12.2027 — the general cut-off for Start-Up Nation beneficiaries to finalize expenditure.

The ministry's 2026 calendar and the SME calls it estimates

The 2026 calendar has two versions to read separately. The January 2026 one has 292 calls and 6,617,288,234 EUR (5,146,020,638 EUR EU funds), published under Article 49(2) of Regulation (EU) 2021/1060. The update the ministry announced in May 2026 estimates about 226 calls, over 4.8 billion EUR in total allocations and about 3.6 billion EUR of EU funding. Since then the calendar is the interactive register at mfe.gov.ro/calendar-apeluri-de-finantare, rebuilt from MySMIS2021 exports: at 18 November 2026 the latest set, of 16.11.2026, held 1,097 calls for the whole 2021–2027 period (940 validated, 155 in editing, 2 finalized in editing). It filters by programme, call type, objective, fund and status, and exports CSV or XLSX. The start and end dates in a row are the authority's estimates, not submission windows.

The calendar's firmness shows on POCIDIF Action 2.1: it listed an estimated opening in June 2026 and closing in August 2026, while MIPE Order no. 965 of 23.06.2026 set submissions from 30.06.2026 to 30.09.2026, and Order no. 1500 of 29.09.2026 moved the close to 30.10.2026 at 17:00. The same gap appears at Centru: the ministry's 16.11.2026 data set still listed call 2 of Action 2.2 for November–December 2026, while the regional agency's call page fixes 02.12.2026 – 02.02.2027.

SME calls in the calendar that have not launched yet, with the estimate and their real status at 18 November 2026. POCIDIF Measure 1.1.1, support for innovative enterprises: 45,410,384 EUR in the more developed regions and 157,859,017 EUR in the less developed ones, still in editing, estimated window December 2026 – February 2027, no guide published. Bucharest-Ilfov Action 1.7, advanced digital transformation of SMEs, 25,000,000 EUR: the ministry's set estimates September 2026 and the ADR calendar of 13.07.2026 puts the final guide in October 2026; the guide is not published and the call has not launched. South-East Action 1.3, SME digitalization, 9,090,204 EUR for the region and 1,572,114 EUR for the Danube Delta ITI: estimated 30.09–31.12.2026, not launched. South Muntenia RSO1.1, research and innovation in micro-enterprises and SMEs, 29,882,352.94 EUR: the agency page shows “Pending” after public consultation on 29.04–22.05.2026, and the ministry's set estimates 02.12.2026–31.05.2027. Centru Action 10.1 STEP, 10,949,820 EUR for companies in the region: guide consultation ended on 30.10.2026, submission dates are blank, and the ADR Centru calendar indicates November 2026 – March 2027 only as a guide. Other POCIDIF calls have opened and closed meanwhile: Measure 1.1.2 (53,174,731 EUR) closed on 16.11.2026, and Measures 1.3.2 (13,351,703 EUR) and Action 1.4 measure 1 (13,761,318 EUR) closed on 30.10.2026.

Estimated dates are months, not deadlines. What they allow is internal planning: a company that defines its solution and budget now enters the window with the documentation ready.

Calls without a fixed date: AFIR and MEDAT — plus a correction on PEO

AFIR sessions for the PNS interventions open according to the calendar published by AFIR. On 18 November 2026 the agency's submission portal shows session DR-12 (investments in the consolidation of holdings of young farmers and farmers aged up to 45) open from 06.10.2026 to 02.12.2026 at 15:59; its “other sectors” component ran out of funds on 11.11.2026, while the “livestock sector” component remains open. Sessions DR-14 (small farms) and DR-18 (floriculture, medicinal and aromatic plants) ran from 01.09.2026 to 16.11.2026 and have ended. Digitalization has no intervention of its own and enters as part of an investment project.

On the MEDAT side, Start-Up Nation session 2 has launched: the training stage is open from 06.10.2026 until 27.11.2026 at 20:00 on minimis.imm.gov.ro, for people aged 18 to 30; the sources consulted show no date yet for the financing stage. Microindustrializare and Comerț remain conditional on the adoption of MEDAT's 2026 budget. The rest of the funding wave is described, with attribution, in the MIPE estimative calendar dated January 2026.

PEO no longer belongs in this category, and the correction is ours. The call „Competențe digitale pentru piața muncii” appeared in this calendar as having a published guide and no scheduled launch. The managing authority's announcement says the opposite: MySMIS2021 opened on 28 May 2024 at 16:00 and projects could be submitted until 23 August 2024 at 16:00, with no extension and no later round on the announcement page. A call that closed in the summer of 2024 has no place in a calendar of future deadlines, so we remove it from here and keep it in the archive below. On digital training we identified no open session at the date of this check; when the authority announces one, it enters the calendar with its source.

Archive: closed calls people still search for, with source and date

A closed call does not mean the same thing everywhere, and the difference decides what is left to do: it can be closed for good, closed with projects in implementation (submission is over, but implementation and payment deadlines keep running for beneficiaries) or still have its applications under evaluation. Below are the closed digitalization calls people keep searching for, each with its window and source.

POCIDIF Action 2.2.1, Measure 1, call 2 (often searched as "pocidif cadastru"): launched on 20.05.2024, closed on 19.08.2024, allocation 152,248,563.70 EUR, the only eligible applicant being the National Agency for Cadastre and Land Registration as partnership leader; the call page on mfe.gov.ro shows it as closed, checked on 18.11.2026. DigiLocal, for town halls and county councils: registration was active from 19.09.2024 at 11:00, documents could be submitted until 25.09.2024 at 12:00, and funding was awarded in the order of the registration number, according to the programme page on economie.gov.ro, checked on 18.11.2026. PEO "Competențe digitale pentru piața muncii": submission between 28.05.2024 and 23.08.2024, with no later round, according to the PEO managing authority announcement. POCIDIF Action 2.4, SME digitalization through EDIH hubs (code POCIDIF/1147/2/1): submission between 25.08.2023 at 10:00 and 25.10.2023 at 23:59, allocation 12,873,886.83 EUR; the applicants were the EDIH hubs, not companies, and a company received support through a hub.

The other closed calls are described above, at their dates: POCIDIF Action 2.1 (30.06.2026–30.10.2026 at 17:00), PNRR C9 for SMEs (implementation ended on 30.06.2026, or 31.07.2026 with an extension, payments until 30.10.2026) and the 2026 round of Digital Europe (closed on 01.10.2026). The full pages of open or upcoming calls stay in the calendar; for an archived call, the first step is to look for its successor in the estimated calendar above, remembering that the date there is an estimate and the binding deadline appears only in the order approving the guide.

How we prepare clients before a call opens

Our rule is simple: the technical offer must be ready before submission, not drafted against the clock inside the call window. When a client tells us which programme they are pursuing, we define the software solution together, write the specifications and build the budget on the eligible-cost structure from the guide — so that at launch the consultant receives the documents within the same week.

This approach matters most for calls with short windows or continuous submission until the budget runs out, where filing order can count. We stop at the technical file: we do not draft the funding application, we do not estimate scores and we do not promise approvals — we are the software vendor, and the dossier stays with the company or its consultant.

Frequently asked questions

What is the nearest deadline in the 2026 calendar?⌄

For an existing company, at 18.11.2026, the first deadline is 16.12.2026 at 12:00 — Centru 1.4.3, only for SMEs in the ITI areas — followed by 30.12.2026 at 20:00 for SME Eco-Tech. Earlier, on 27.11.2026 at 20:00, enrolment in the Start-Up Nation courses closes, but that is for people aged 18 to 30, not for existing companies. On 02.12.2026 call 2 of Action 2.2 in the Centru programme opens, until 02.02.2027.

How many calls does the 2026 EU funding calendar have?⌄

About 226 calls, with over 4.8 billion EUR in total allocations and about 3.6 billion EUR of EU funding, according to the update MIPE announced in May 2026. The January 2026 version listed 292 calls and 6,617,288,234 EUR. The calendar is refreshed from MySMIS2021; the 16.11.2026 set holds 1,097 calls across the whole 2021–2027 period.

What dates does Start-Up Nation session 2 have?⌄

The training stage runs from 06.10.2026 until 27.11.2026 at 20:00 on minimis.imm.gov.ro, for people aged 18 to 30. Completing the course is mandatory for stage two, in which companies newly set up by graduates can obtain up to 250,000 RON, at most 90% of eligible costs. We found no date for the financing stage in the ministry's sources; we add it when it appears.

Where do the dates in this funding calendar come from?⌄

Firm deadlines come from ministerial orders for PNRR, from the applicant guides and call sheets of the POCIDIF, SME Eco-Tech and Centru calls, from MEDAT announcements for Start-Up Nation, from the European Funding & Tenders portal and from the paying agency's submission portal for agricultural sessions. Estimated dates come from the ministry's interactive calendar, mfe.gov.ro/calendar-apeluri-de-finantare, in the 16.11.2026 set fed from MySMIS2021.

What should I prepare if my call has not opened yet?⌄

For an unlaunched call in the calendar, everything that depends on the software vendor can be finished in advance: solution specifications, the technical offer and the budget by cost category. That way, at opening, your dossier only waits on the administrative components, not the technical documentation.

What do I do if I missed a digitalization call?⌄

First check the call's state: closed for good, closed with projects in implementation, or with applications still under evaluation. Then look for its successor in the estimated calendar, where the date is an estimate, not a deadline, and prepare the technical part early (specifications, offer, budget). The closed calls people still search for are listed in the archive on this page, with window and source.
