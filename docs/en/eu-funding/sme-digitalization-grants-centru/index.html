Digitalization grants in the Centru region: what a company in each county can do with call two

Companies in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș and Sibiu can apply from 2 December 2026 to 2 February 2027: the final guide was published on 30 October 2026.

The programme's official applicant guide
ⓘNorthdan Soft is a software supplier, not a funding consultancy, and is not affiliated with any managing authority or development agency. Programme information is indicative — always check the conditions in the programme's official applicant guide.

An SME company (SRL, SA or cooperative) with activity outside the ICT sector, implementing its project at a registered office or work point in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș or Sibiu, can receive €15,000–200,000 non-reimbursable from PR Centru, Action 2.2, call 2, if it puts in at least 10% of the eligible value; applications are filed in MySMIS2021 between 2 December 2026, 12:00, and 2 February 2027, 12:00 (guide 3.5, 5.4, 3.4, 4.3). The budget is €14,113,032, the aid is de minimis (guide 3.3 and 3.13), and the final guide has been published since 30 October 2026 (guide 4.1). Verified on 18 November 2026 against the final guide and its annexes.

Here you see where the project can sit, what a company in each county usually builds and which other call overlaps; the call rules are on its own page. Northdan Soft is based in Aiud, Alba County. On one project we take a single role, software supplier or auditor, never both. We do not write the funding application and do not promise approval.

Where we are: final guide, submission opens 2 December

The applicant's guide has been published since 30 October 2026 (guide 4.1). The call is competitive: submission opens on 2 December 2026 at 12:00 and closes on 2 February 2027 at 12:00, in MySMIS2021 (guide 4.3 and 4.4). ADR Centru holds an online information session on 23 November 2026 at 12:00 on Zoom, with registration through the form on regiocentru.ro.

Projects filed until 2 January 2027 go to contracting with at least 85 points, if they fit within the allocation; those above 50 and below 85 stay in competition with the second month. If the allocation is not covered at closing, every project with at least 50 points goes through (guide 8.4).

The guide's age and profit example (a company founded by 3 January 2025, with profit at 31 December 2025) covers only 2026 filings; for January 2027 the guide gives no example, so ask the ADR Centru helpdesk in writing, which answers within 10 working days (guide 5.1.1.6 and 1.1). A company with profit in 2025 but no certain profit in 2026 has the criterion covered if it files by 31 December 2026 (guide 5.1.1.6, the 2026 example).

What a company in any county can fund, and what not

The project must include intangible assets: ERP, CRM, PLM, RPA, e-invoicing and digitalized ordering, AI solutions, websites and online shops, cybersecurity, IoT, VR/AR (guide 3.6 and 5.2.4). Hardware is optional and only if it serves the digitalization plan (guide 5.2.1, 5.3.2.2): a project of only laptops or servers is not eligible (guide 5.2.4). Productive equipment is not eligible even if used digitally (guide 3.6 and 5.2.1).

Annex 3 removes not only IT but also agriculture, fishing, part of the food industry (meat, fish, dairy, juices, fruit and vegetables, milling, sugar), alcoholic drinks, agri-food wholesale, food shops (4711, 4721-4729), tobacco, fuels and electronics. Not the whole food industry is excluded, only the Annex 3 rows: bread (1071, 1072), chocolate (1082), coffee (1083), ice cream (1052) and mineral water and soft drinks (1107) are not on the list. The test covers the whole company and its revenue in the last fiscal year and the current year (guide 5.1.1.1).

Implementation place: one work point in the region, or several

The applicant is a company (SRL, SA) or a cooperative, not a sole trader (PFA), individual enterprise (II) or branch (guide 5.1, 5.1.2); it has at least one full fiscal year and net or operating profit above 0 in the previous fiscal year (guide 5.1.1.6) and at least one employee (guide 5.1.1.7). The project is implemented in the Centru region, urban or rural (guide 3.5). At contract signing, the implementation place must be a main or secondary registered office (work point) on the ORC certificate. The digitalized activity must already run there, with at least one fiscal year of history, CAEN codes authorised at submission, and revenue from them in the submission year and the year before (guide 5.7.4).

A company with work points in several counties files one application (guide 5.1.1.8). The project can cover several work points, but age, CAEN codes and revenue are proven separately for each (guide 5.7.4).

In our reading, the guide does not require the registered seat in the region: a company from Cluj or Bucharest with a real work point in Mureș or Brașov appears to be eligible for that point, and a point outside the six counties does not (guide 3.5 and 5.7.4). However, the guide describes the target group as "enterprises from the Centru Region" (guide 3.7), so get the ADR Centru helpdesk's written answer before paying for the audit or the plan.

Brașov: manufacturing and distribution

In Brașov, a manufacturer or distributor usually starts with an ERP with stock, e-invoicing and a digitalized order flow. Făgăraș municipality, Victoria town and 19 communes are in ITI Țara Făgărașului, where call 1.4.3, SME competitiveness within ITI, is open from 16 November to 16 December 2026, with its own guide and rules (guide 1.4.3, section 3.5); Brașov city is not. Both calls are de minimis, at most €300,000 per single undertaking over three years (guide 3.13). The page on digitalization funding for companies in Brașov County has the detail.

Sibiu: industry, services and rural tourism

the towns of Avrig and Tălmaciu, plus 11 communes, among them Șelimbăr are in the same ITI Țara Făgărașului, under the call 1.4.3 guide (guide 1.4.3, 3.5); Sibiu municipality and Mediaș are not. Industry goes for ERP and e-invoicing, services for CRM and RPA, guesthouses in Mărginimea Sibiului for bookings and the website (5520 is not in Annex 3), if the guesthouse is a company, not a PFA or II (guide 5.1.2). The page on digitalization funding for companies in Sibiu County has the detail.

Mureș: online sales and growing companies

In Mureș the sales channel comes first: an online shop tied to stock and invoicing, then ERP and CRM. There is no ITI here. Dairy (1051) and meat (1011-1013) take the whole company out of the call (guide 5.1.1.1). The page on digitalization funding for companies in Mureș County also covers the Just Transition programme.

Alba: from Alba Iulia to Aiud

The Moții – Țara de Piatră area (Abrud, Baia de Arieș, Câmpeni, Zlatna and 24 mountain communes) has call 1.4.3 open until 16 December 2026, 12:00; Alba Iulia, Aiud, Sebeș and Blaj are outside it (guide 1.4.3, 3.5). Northdan Soft works from Aiud. The page on digitalization funding for companies in Alba County covers the vineyard trap.

Harghita: workshops and services with two-language customers

A workshop or service firm with Romanian- and Hungarian-speaking customers gains from a CRM and a bilingual quote-and-order flow; the software can be bilingual, the file cannot: the application is in Romanian and foreign-language documents need a certified translation (guide 7.2). No ITI. Dairy (1051) and food shops (4721-4729) exclude the company; a bakery (1071) and a roaster (1083) do not, unless the company also has the shop code 4724 or wholesale 4637 authorised, or revenue from them (guide 5.1.1.1). The page on digitalization funding for companies in Harghita County has the detail.

Covasna: same project, same check

A small manufacturer or service firm in Sfântu Gheorghe or Târgu Secuiesc builds a project on a simple ERP, CRM and digital ordering. No ITI. Mineral water and soft drinks (1107) are not in Annex 3, juices (1032) are excluded (guide 5.1.1.1). The page on digitalization funding for companies in Covasna County also covers the single-undertaking rule.

Who you work with, in what order

Digital maturity is audited before submission by an independent external auditor with CAEN 6202 or 6220, or by an EDIH, and again at the end of the project (guide 5.3.2.11, Annex 13). On one project, the same firm can be only one of three: the auditor, the author of the digitalization plan or the software supplier. Northdan can take one of the three roles, never two (guide 5.3.2.8). We do not write the funding application, do project management or promise approval. Do not order software or equipment before submission: costs of starting the investment before the application are ineligible (guide 5.3.3). Only the initial audit, the digitalization plan and the application consultancy can be contracted before submission (guide 5.3.1).

Frequently asked questions

When are applications submitted for call two in the Centru region?

Between 2 December 2026, 12:00, and 2 February 2027, 12:00, exclusively in MySMIS2021 (guide 4.3 and 4.4). The final guide was published on 30 October 2026; verified on 18 November 2026.

How much can a company get and how much does it pay?

The non-reimbursable grant is a minimum of €15,000 and a maximum of €200,000 per project, under de minimis (guide 5.4). The company provides at least 10% of the eligible value, so aid is at most 90% (guide 3.4), and anything above the ceilings stays with it.

Can a software company in the Centru region apply?

No. The call is for SMEs with activity outside the ICT sector, and an Annex 3 CAEN code authorised at submission, for example 6201, 6202 or 6209, takes the company out of the call (guide 5.1.1.1).

We run an agricultural or food business in Mureș, Harghita or Covasna. Can we apply?

It depends on the CAEN code. No, if you have an Annex 3 code authorised, for example 1051 (dairy), 1011-1013 (meat) or 4721-4729 (food shops), or had revenue from it in the last fiscal year and the current year. Bread (1071) and coffee (1083) are not on the list, but an own shop with 4724 excludes the company. The test covers the whole company (guide 5.1.1.1).

Who performs the digital maturity audit?

An independent external auditor, a legal entity with CAEN 6202 or 6220, or an EDIH, distinct from the company and from the audited suppliers (guide 5.3.2.11). Northdan Soft, in Aiud, can be the auditor on a project where it is neither the software supplier nor the author of the digitalization plan.

The company has work points in two counties of the region. Does it file two applications?

No. Each applicant files a single application in this call (guide 5.1.1.8). The project can cover several work points in the six counties, but the age of the activity, the authorised CAEN codes and the revenue are proven separately for each work point (guide 5.7.4).
