A custom web application built with grant funding

Which route funds a web application depends on who uses it: your own company, or the clients who buy it — and for the second, POCIDIF 2.1 closed on 30 October 2026.

A web application can still be funded with non-repayable money in winter 2026–2027 on two routes: as custom-developed software in call 2 of Action 2.2 of the Centru Regional Programme (grant EUR 15,000–200,000, maximum 90%, for non-ICT SMEs in Alba, Brașov, Covasna, Harghita, Mureș and Sibiu, applications 2 December 2026 12:00 to 2 February 2027 12:00) and inside a Start-Up Nation session 2 business plan (up to RON 250,000, maximum 90%, for companies of people under 30 who complete the free course, enrolment until 27 November 2026, 20:00). POCIDIF Action 2.1, the route for applications sold as a product (grant EUR 200,000–1,500,000), closed on 30 October 2026 at 17:00 (MIPE Order no. 1500/29.09.2026). SME Eco-Tech, open until 30 December 2026 at 20:00, caps software at RON 15,000 excluding VAT, so it does not cover a custom build. Off-the-shelf software has a blind spot: the processes that make your company different, and a custom web application covers exactly that gap. The stake of the file is the specification: a custom application with no enumerated functionality is an alarm signal for any evaluator. Northdan Soft is the software supplier in the file; we describe the application module by module in the technical offer, build the budget on eligible costs and deliver in stages with settlement documents at every acceptance. We do not write the grant application and do not promise approval. Checked on 18 November 2026 against the final PR Centru 2.2 call 2 guide, the POCIDIF 2.1 call sheet on mfe.gov.ro and the MEDAT announcements for Start-Up Nation and SME Eco-Tech.

Which funding lines support a custom web application

If the application serves your own activity, the open route is PR Centru 2.2 call 2: the guide (5.3.2.1) accepts software purchased or developed/adapted to order, with no separate software ceiling. Conditions: the base activity (digitalizing internal processes) is at least 50% of the eligible budget, the company has at least one employee, and the digital maturity audit is done before applying. The assets must be used exclusively by the beneficiary, bought from unrelated third parties and kept at least 3 years after completion, so an application sold to clients (SaaS) does not fit, and ICT firms cannot apply (3.6, 5.2.1). That is our reading; confirm in writing with helpdesk@adrcentru.ro.

In Start-Up Nation session 2, stage 1 is the free course for people under 30 (enrolment on minimis.imm.gov.ro until 27 November 2026, 20:00) and stage 2 funds the graduates' new companies with up to RON 250,000, maximum 90% of eligible costs. The session's procedure includes software in the digital component of the business plan (item 5.2 para. 2 let. g), next to the website and online shop), and the firm that writes the plan cannot also supply the software.

If the application is the company's product, the route was POCIDIF 2.1, exclusively for SMEs with ICT CAEN codes, EUR 200,000 to EUR 1,500,000; it closed on 30 October 2026. The next call for innovative companies (POCIDIF 1.1.1) is a MIPE estimate for 15 December 2026 to 15 February 2027, not a launch.

From specification to production: our delivery process

We begin with short workshops mapping the processes, then fix the specification that goes into the file. After approval we develop iteratively, with demonstrations at regular intervals, so surprises appear early, when they cost little.

At closure we hand over the application in production, the documentation and the acceptance reports for the payment claim, plus warranty and optional maintenance for the durability period. What we do not do: write the grant application, issue eligibility verdicts, or act as your entire technical team — the closed POCIDIF 2.1 guide required at least 20% of the aid for the core activity to go to the beneficiary's own staff, and in PR Centru the consultant who writes the digitalization plan cannot also supply the software (5.3.2.8).

Frequently asked questions

Can a web application for internal use be funded in 2026?

Yes, in the Centru Region: call 2 of Action 2.2 accepts custom-developed software (guide 5.3.2.1), a grant of EUR 15,000–200,000, maximum 90%, applications 2 December 2026 to 2 February 2027, for non-ICT SMEs with at least one employee. Elsewhere: Start-Up Nation 2 for new companies of people under 30, while SME Eco-Tech covers software up to RON 15,000 only.

Can an IT company fund its own SaaS after 30 October 2026?

Not from the open digitalization calls: PR Centru 2.2 excludes ICT firms and requires the software to be used exclusively by the beneficiary. POCIDIF 2.1 closed on 30 October 2026; the next call for innovative companies (POCIDIF 1.1.1) is a MIPE estimate for 15 December 2026 to 15 February 2027.

How is a web application's cost estimated for the budget?

From the specification: we count the modules, screens and integrations, estimate the effort for each and obtain a cost broken down by component. That transparency is exactly what evaluators look for — a global sum without decomposition raises questions.

Can the web application be hosted anywhere after the funding?

Hosting is chosen freely, but the durability period requires the investment to remain functional and demonstrable. We recommend documented infrastructure with administration access held by the beneficiary, so at monitoring visits the application can be shown without depending on third parties.

What separates a funded web application from a subscription to an existing SaaS?

The developed application becomes the company's asset: you adapt it, extend it, and pay no per-user fees. SaaS is a recurring cost that settles poorly in projects. When the company's processes are specific, own development pays off strategically, not only through the grant.
