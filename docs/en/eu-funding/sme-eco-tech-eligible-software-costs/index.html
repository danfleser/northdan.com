What SME Eco-Tech funds, and where the digital package stops

The SME Eco-Tech procedure funds five categories of costs and two mandatory plaques, and for software the deciding figure is the 15,000 lei excluding VAT ceiling on the software line.

The eligible costs in SME Eco-Tech are, per the implementation procedure (4.1), equipment producing renewable energy, equipment for waste collection and recycling, equipment and systems that save resources, the digital package and other conventional technology equipment, plus two information plaques of at most 200 lei; the first three categories must together make up at least 40% of the eligible costs (4.1 para. 3). For software, the software needed to run the activity is eligible up to 15,000 lei excluding VAT (4.1 let. d): the ceiling is attached to that one line, not to the whole package, and the presentation website, the online store and the IT equipment enter separately; licences are listed separately with no figure, but we budget them as if the ceiling applied to them too. Implementing an ERP or a production management system does not fit on this line, whatever it is called in the budget; a licence for a few modules may fit. The third condition is the one most often missed: staff training, commissioning, assembly and labour are not eligible costs (4.3 para. 2), so precisely the implementation costs of a software component stay with the company.

Verified on 18 November 2026 against the consolidated procedure of 8 July 2026, MEDAT Order no. 1,773 of 27.10.2026 (Official Gazette no. 906 of 30.10.2026) and the MEDAT announcements: deadline 30 December 2026, 20:00, funding in order of submission (2.4). The software line ceiling, the contents of the digital package and the exclusion list are those of the procedure.

All eligible categories in the procedure (4.1)

The procedure lists five categories (4.1 para. 1): (a) equipment producing renewable energy, for example photovoltaic panels, wind turbines, solar thermal, geothermal heat pumps; (b) equipment for collecting, recycling and reusing waste, for example separators, baling presses, shredders, composting stations; (c) equipment, systems and digital applications that save resources, for example high-efficiency boilers, ventilation, UPS, cogeneration, utility metering and monitoring systems; (d) the digital package; (e) other conventional technology equipment, i.e. fixed assets in groups 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3.6 of the Catalogue approved by Government Decision 2139/2004, except gambling equipment, billiard tables and jukeboxes; Two mandatory information plaques are added, one indoors and one outdoors, eligible up to 200 lei, under penalty of the aid not being granted (4.1 para. 1–2).

The green floor is calculated on categories a, b and c only: together they must make up at least 40% of the eligible costs (4.1 para. 3). The digital package and conventional equipment do not count towards it. Standalone storage batteries are not eligible (MEDAT warning of 10.07.2026). A utility monitoring system is an example under category c, so it counts towards 40%; whether its software sits under category c or on the 15,000 lei line is our reading, so get written confirmation from MEDAT before budgeting it under category c.

Every expense is tied to the activities of the CAEN code for which funding is requested (4.3 para. 1), assets must be new (4.4), and cryptocurrency mining is excluded (4.2 para. 1).

What the digital package contains, item by item

Per the implementation procedure (4.1 para. 1 let. d), the digital package covers a presentation website or an online store; domain registration, without hosting; promotion of the site or store; the software needed to run the activity, up to 15,000 lei excluding VAT; the electronic signature; PC-type IT equipment — desktop, server, monitor, printer or multifunction device, portable systems; licences; smartphones and tablets.

Two details change how the budget is written. First, the 15,000 lei excluding VAT ceiling applies to the software line, not to the whole package — the website and the equipment have their own treatment. Second, hosting does not appear in the package: domain registration and promotion of the site or store are reimbursed, the annual hosting subscription is not, and it stays an operating cost of the company.

The ceiling is written in the procedure only on “software needed to run the activity”, while “licences needed to run the activity” appear separately, later in the same letter, with no figure. Some summaries extend the ceiling to licences. We budget as if it applied to both, and we do not use the licence line to push software above 15,000 lei unless MEDAT confirms otherwise in writing.

What is not reimbursed, and why it counts twice for software

Per the implementation procedure, VAT, permits and fees, packaging, transport, staff training, commissioning, assembly and labour are not eligible (4.3 para. 2). Neither are leased or second-hand assets, nor costs already financed from other sources (4.6), nor the financial costs of the loan (4.2 para. 3). Invoices issued before the financing agreement enters into force are not eligible (4.3 para. 4).

On a machine purchase those exclusions are a small share of the value. On a software component they are the work itself: installation and configuration, commissioning and user training. A budget that ignores this discovers at reimbursement that the licence was eligible and the implementation was not.

The rules that concern the software supplier

At registration the applicant uploads at least one price offer for each eligible expense (4.9 para. 3), so the supplier's offer exists before the application. Purchases follow public procurement principles, including economy, efficiency and effectiveness; at prices out of line with the market the bank may ask for clarifications or refuse payment (4.9 para. 1–2).

No invoice is issued before the agreement enters into force (4.3 para. 4). Payments are made from the dedicated account, only with the bank's consent, in instalments, within an implementation period of at most 12 months (point 5; definitions). Payments in foreign currency are converted at the BNR rate of 23.06.2025, the date the approval order was published in the Official Gazette (4.3 para. 5).

Kinship up to the second degree, including spouses and in-laws, between the beneficiary's shareholders and the supplier excludes the aid (3.4 para. 3), and a beneficiary cannot also be a supplier to other beneficiaries of the programme (4.10). Supporting documents are uploaded within at most 7 working days of the last payment, per Annex 8, under penalty of recovery of the aid (point 5). At monitoring, asset serial numbers are compared with the document uploaded with the invoice, and the assets must be at the implementation site and in operation (point 6).

The figures that frame a project budget

A project starts at 668,100 lei of eligible costs (definitions). The grant covers at most 40%, capped at CHF 50,000 — 267,240 lei — and the loan contracted with a partner bank must be at least 400,860 lei (definitions). Above the threshold the grant cap stays fixed, so the share it covers falls.

On top of that sits the green condition: at least 40% of total eligible costs must be equipment for renewable energy, waste collection or recycling and resource saving (4.1 para. 3). Together with the software line ceiling, it explains why digital accompanies rather than leads.

Registrations opened on 26 May 2026 and the deadline was extended three times: to 24 July, to 24 September and then to 30 December 2026, 20:00; funding is awarded in order of submission (2.4). Any presentation still announcing July or September describes a window that has passed.

The CAEN code the costs are tied to

Since 7 July 2026 (Official Gazette no. 558) the programme admits manufacturing, trade and services, not only manufacturing. Each application is tied to a single CAEN code, principal or secondary, held since at least 1 January 2022 (3.2 let. h, as amended by MEDAT Order no. 1,773 of 27.10.2026), and each expense must match the activities of that code (4.3 para. 1). The remaining eligibility conditions are on the programme page.

What we build inside the programme and what we refuse

What fits: a presentation website on an adapted theme costs an indicative 300–1,500 €, a custom one 1,500–5,000 €, and a WooCommerce store with Romanian courier and invoicing integrations 1,500–8,000 €. Our prices are indicative, excluding VAT and expressed in euro, while the programme ceilings are in lei; we do not convert our indicative bands, and foreign-currency payments follow the procedure's rule: the BNR rate of the date the approval order was published (4.3 para. 5).

What does not fit: implementing an ERP costs an indicative 5,000–20,000 €, above the programme's software line, and the configuration, migration and training part is not eligible anyway (4.3 para. 2). A licence for a few modules may fit on the software line.

The limit that costs us a contract: we do not split an ERP into several business-software lines to squeeze it under the ceiling, we do not invoice training or commissioning under a licence heading, and we do not issue the invoice before the financing agreement enters into force. Northdan Soft is the supplier of the digital component, not a funding consultant: we do not write the funding application, assess the company's eligibility, negotiate with the bank or promise approval.

Frequently asked questions

Which costs are eligible in SME Eco-Tech?

Five categories in the procedure (4.1): equipment for renewable energy, for waste collection and recycling, equipment and systems that save resources, the digital package and other conventional technology equipment, plus two information plaques of at most 200 lei. The first three categories must together make up at least 40% of the eligible costs.

Does an ERP fit inside the 15,000 lei excluding VAT ceiling?

Implementing an ERP does not fit: it costs an indicative 5,000–20,000 €, while the software line stops at 15,000 lei excluding VAT. A licence for a few modules may fit, but configuration, migration and training are not eligible (4.3 para. 2). We do not split an ERP across lines to get under the ceiling.

Are a presentation website and an online store eligible under SME Eco-Tech?

Yes, both are in the digital package (4.1 para. 1 let. d), together with domain registration and promotion of the site or store. The domain is reimbursed without hosting: the annual hosting subscription stays an operating cost of the company, but promotion of the site or store is eligible.

Does the digital package count towards the 40% floor?

No. The 40% floor is reached only with equipment for renewable energy, for waste collection and recycling, and equipment that saves resources (4.1 para. 3). The website, software, licences and IT equipment of the digital package do not count; a utility monitoring system does, being an example under category c.

Do software licences fall under the 15,000 lei ceiling?

The procedure writes the 15,000 lei excluding VAT ceiling only on the software needed to run the activity; licences needed to run the activity appear separately in let. d, with no figure. Some summaries extend the ceiling to licences. We budget as if it applied to both, and we ask for written MEDAT confirmation before budgeting above it.

Is user training on the new system reimbursed?

No. Staff training appears on the ineligible list in the procedure (4.3 para. 2), alongside commissioning, assembly and labour. For a software component these are the implementation costs themselves, so they are budgeted separately, from the company's own funds, not from the grant.

Can the supplier issue the invoice before the financing agreement?

No. Invoices issued before the financing agreement enters into force are not eligible (4.3 para. 4). The price offer is uploaded at registration (4.9 para. 3); the invoice comes after the agreement enters into force and is paid from the dedicated account, only with the bank's consent, in instalments, within 12 months.

Which documents does reimbursement require for the software line?

The procedure requires the supporting documents of the payments to be uploaded, per Annex 8, within at most 7 working days of the last payment from the dedicated account, under penalty of recovery of the aid. We hand over the invoice, the acceptance protocol and documentation with equipment serial numbers and licence keys, for the monitoring check.
