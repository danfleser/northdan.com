The Vest SME digitalization call closed in 2025. The winning companies are delivering now.

From Timișoara to Reșița, the companies selected under Vest's 15.38 million euro call have entered the phase of contracts — and of deadlines.

Ghidul oficial al programului
ⓘNorthdan Soft este furnizor de servicii software, nu consultant de finanțare, și nu este afiliat autorităților de management sau agențiilor de dezvoltare. Informațiile despre programe au caracter orientativ — verifică întotdeauna condițiile în ghidul oficial al programului.

Between 10,000 and 100,000 euros per grant, a total budget of 15.38 million, four counties — Arad, Caraș-Severin, Hunedoara and Timiș: that was call PRV/1.2/1, through which the Regional Programme (Programul Regional) Vest funded SME digitalization. Submissions closed in September 2025, and the selected projects are now moving through contracting into implementation. For the winners, the real test is execution: turning a funding application — often written with a consultant, under deadline pressure — into actual systems that pass acceptance and the reimbursement request. This is where the typical surprises surface: vague specifications that must be made concrete, suppliers chosen on paper who fail to deliver, expense categories that must be respected to the letter. Northdan enters at exactly this point: we develop the software component according to the approved project, flag mismatches between application and reality early (before they become eligibility problems) and hand over each phase with the documents the reimbursement process needs. Companies in Vest that missed the call have their most relevant national alternative in POCIDIF 2.1 — for SMEs with a technology CAEN code, accepting applications through 30 September 2026 — plus the free services of the DIGIVEST hub for preparing the next step.

Frequently asked questions

Our PRV/1.2 contract specifies a turnkey ERP — what does that mean at acceptance?⌄

Exactly what you define now, before development: the list of modules, the flows covered, the acceptance criteria. Our recommendation is a specifications document agreed at kick-off — which we draft together — so acceptance becomes a checklist exercise, not a negotiation under reimbursement-deadline pressure.

The supplier named in our funding application no longer responds — can we switch?⌄

Yes, under the terms of the funding contract: as a rule the procurement is redone according to the applicable procedures, and the new supplier delivers the same approved expense categories. We have taken over projects in exactly this situation; the essential part is documenting the history and informing your project officer before, not after.

What are the chances Vest launches a second SME digitalization call?⌄

None announced officially at the time of our check — line 1.2's budget was committed in the first call. The region's forward plans centre on the smart-city call for local authorities, itself not yet launched. For companies, the active road remains the national programmes; we keep their statuses verified on the dedicated pages.
