IT start-up with no revenue: which EU funds you can get in 2026

With no revenue history, two European routes accept you now: Start-Up Nation, if you are under 30 and enrol on the course by 27 November 2026, and EIC Accelerator, if you already have a prototype.

On 18 November 2026 an IT start-up with no turnover has two European funding routes open to it: Start-Up Nation, financed from the Education and Employment Programme 2021-2027, with up to RON 250,000 and at most 90% of eligible costs, for a company set up after the free course that people aged 18 to under 30 can enrol on until 27 November 2026, 20:00; and EIC Accelerator, which also accepts individuals who intend to found a company, with a grant of up to EUR 2.5 million for innovation at TRL 6–8. Start-Up Nation session 1 has ended, and the date for session 2 funding applications has not been announced. POCIDIF 2.1 closed on 30 October 2026 and requires a company with ICT activity and at least EUR 200,000 per project, so it comes only after the first years of the company.

Checked on 18 November 2026 against the MEDAT announcements of 30 September and 4 November 2026, the Start-Up Nation procedure (MEDAT Order no. 328/2025, as amended) and the official EIC Accelerator page. Our role is technical: we do not write the application and we do not promise approval. For each route we put on paper what you build, in which stages and at what cost, so the founder knows what is defensible before submitting.

Who counts as a start-up in each programme

The three programmes define a start-up differently. In Start-Up Nation the company does not exist yet: it is founded after the course, by its graduate. EIC Accelerator accepts single start-ups and SMEs, and also individuals who intend to launch a company. POCIDIF looks at age (a newly established company, with the limit described in our guide to funding for new companies).

So you choose the route by stage, not by name: an idea with no company, a prototype with no company, or a company with a technical team and a project of at least EUR 200,000.

Start-Up Nation session 2, read by an IT founder

Session 2 funds Pillar I only, for people aged 18 to under 30 with at least ISCED 2 education. The order of steps: the 40-hour course (online or in person), with enrolment on minimis.imm.gov.ro until 27.11.2026, 20:00, out of 17,600 places, of which more than 11,400 were still free on 4 November; then founding the SRL; then the funding form. Up to RON 250,000, at most 90% of costs, own contribution of at least 10%, a minimum score of 50 out of 100.

For an IT founder the structural conditions matter. The graduate must be legal representative, sole or majority shareholder and administrator, and cannot be a shareholder, administrator or employee in another company funded under the call, and cannot be the majority shareholder of any other company (SRL or SA), including your own contracting SRL; with two co-founders, the cap table must therefore be decided before incorporation. The company creates at least 2 full-time, indefinite-term jobs, kept for 24 months after payment, and keeps operating for 3 years. Head office and all work points must be in the development region of the graduate's domicile (the permanent address on the ID card), for the whole implementation and sustainability period, and the CAEN Rev. 3 code must be authorised by payment. Spending happens within 12 months of the contract, but not after 8 December 2027.

What Start-Up Nation pays for in a software product

The eligible lines in the procedure include a presentation website, an online shop, software needed for the activity, licences, IT equipment and an electronic signature. For those who hire the 2 people, salaries, utilities, accounting and rent are paid as a lump sum of up to RON 80,000, and consultancy is capped at RON 10,000.

Developing the product you will sell is not named as a separate line. That is our reading of the procedure, not a ruling: ask the AIMMT or the MEDAT helpdesk for written confirmation before budgeting your own application. Cost details are in our guide to eligible Start-Up Nation expenses.

EIC Accelerator: the route for a prototype, not an idea

The official EIC Accelerator page lists individuals intending to launch a company among eligible applicants, so you can apply before incorporating. The short proposal (a 12-page form, a pitch deck of up to 10 slides, a video of up to 3 minutes) can be submitted at any time. Full proposals have 2027 cut-offs: 20 January, 17 March, 1 September and 4 November, 17:00 Brussels time. The 2026 ones have passed.

The grant is up to EUR 2.5 million for TRL 6–8; equity investment is EUR 1 to 10 million. The page sets no revenue condition. It does expect a product demonstrated in a relevant environment: a product idea with no prototype does not reach TRL 6.

Other pre-seed routes: cascade calls and incubators

On oportunitati-ue.gov.ro, the beneficiary filter “Start-up” lists European calls for start-ups, for example Seedbed Venture Catalyst and InnoNext FSTP. Each has its own page, amounts and deadlines, so read them there, not in summaries.

Under PR Centru 1.5.1 Stage II, incubator residents can receive EUR 50,000–100,000; the guide is in consultation until 8 December 2026 and the route is regional. The EDIH network does not give money to the company: the applicant is the hub, and the company receives non-financial support under a contract, as described on our EDIH Romania page.

Later, as an ICT company: POCIDIF

POCIDIF 2.1 closed on 30 October 2026 (MIPE Order no. 1500/29.09.2026). It requires at least EUR 200,000 per project, authorised ICT CAEN codes and a technical team with one coordinator and two experts. For newly established enterprises the ceilings are EUR 750,000, 1,000,000 or 1,500,000. The full conditions, codes and scoring rules are in our guide to EU funding for IT companies.

Frequently asked questions

Can I get EU funds for an IT start-up with no revenue?

Yes, through two routes. Start-Up Nation funds companies founded after a free course, for people under 30, with up to RON 250,000. EIC Accelerator also accepts individuals with no company, but expects a prototype near TRL 6. POCIDIF needs an ICT company and at least EUR 200,000 per project.

Does Start-Up Nation pay for developing the app I will sell?

The procedure does not name developing your own product as an eligible line. It names a website, online shop, software needed for the activity, licences, IT equipment and a lump sum of up to RON 80,000 for salaries and rent. That is our reading: get written confirmation from AIMMT or the MEDAT helpdesk before budgeting.

Can I apply to Start-Up Nation if the company has two partners?

Yes, but the course graduate must be the sole or majority shareholder, administrator and legal representative. The graduate cannot be the majority shareholder of any other company (SRL or SA), including your own contracting SRL, nor shareholder, administrator or employee in another company funded under the call. A minority co-founder is allowed.

What age do I need for Start-Up Nation session 2?

Between 18 and under 30, with at least ISCED 2 education. The 40-hour course, online or in person, has enrolment on minimis.imm.gov.ro until 27 November 2026, 20:00. After the course you found the company and fill in the funding form, whose date has not been announced.

Can someone with no company yet apply to EIC Accelerator?

Yes. The official page lists individuals intending to launch a company among applicants. The short proposal can be submitted at any time, and full proposals have 2027 cut-offs at 17:00 Brussels time. The grant reaches EUR 2.5 million, but it expects a product at TRL 6–8, not just an idea.

When does POCIDIF become an option for an IT start-up?

Once the company has an authorised ICT CAEN code, a technical team with one coordinator and two experts, and a project of at least EUR 200,000. Call 2.1 closed on 30 October 2026, so watch for the next calls. The conditions are described in our guide to funding for IT companies.
