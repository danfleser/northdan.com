What Femeia Antreprenor 2024 reimburses: IT equipment, website and software

The 2024 procedure has ten cost categories; the IT part sits in two of them, with different rules.

In the 2024 edition of Femeia Antreprenor, ten categories of cost are eligible (procedure approved by MEAT Order 1,974/2024, point 4.3.1 items a–j), and IT splits in two: computers, laptops, servers, monitors, printers, tablets and smartphones fall under technological equipment only if each item costs at least 2,500 lei (item a and point 4.3.3, amended by Order 700/2026), while a website, online store, domain name without hosting, software, licences and electronic signature fall under item f, with no minimum value. New applications are closed: the ministry carried the 2024 call forward, resumed in April 2026, and since 10 November 2026 beneficiaries holding a financing agreement file reimbursement claims at minimis.imm.gov.ro, with every cost incurred and paid within 12 months of the agreement entering into force and no later than 15 November 2027 (point 6.6 para. 10). The grant is at most 200,000 lei and at most 90% of eligible costs (point 4.2), so at least 10% is your own co-financing, and VAT is not reimbursed (point 6.6 para. 15).

The reference list is not a business plan but the cost elements in the notification annexed to the financing agreement (Annex 6): costs that do not match them are not paid (point 6.6 para. 13). The 25,000 lei digital-component threshold belonged to the 2022 round and does not apply to the 2024 edition. Checked on 18 November 2026 against the consolidated procedure (legislatie.just.ro) and the ministry release of 10 November 2026. Northdan Soft builds the website, store or software and hands over the reimbursement documents; it does not write the application, assess eligibility or promise payment.

The ten eligible categories in the 2024 procedure

Items a–e (point 4.3.1): a) technological equipment from groups 2.1–2.2 of GD 2,139/2004 and assimilated assets, including IT equipment, each at least 2,500 lei, excluding gambling machines, billiard tables and jukeboxes; b) work, production, service and trade premises, with an ANEVAR valuation (except mobile premises) and no residential use; c) means of transport, with a car capped at 50,000 lei eligible value and one per beneficiary, except rent-a-car, driving schools and taxi; d) furniture, office equipment and protection systems (group 3 of GD 2,139/2004); e) a lump sum for salaries, utilities, accounting and rent of at most 60,000 lei, paid on proof that you created at least 2 jobs.

Items f–j: f) presentation website, online store, domain registration without hosting, software, electronic signature and licences needed for the activity; g) entrepreneurship courses, at most 5,000 lei per person; h) consultancy for the documentation and implementation, at most 10,000 lei; i) two mandatory information plaques, at most 500 lei; j) financial costs of the credit. Crypto mining or storage systems and astrology, spiritism, escort, dating and video-chat activities, including their websites, are not financed (point 4.3.2).

The IT part: item a and item f are not read the same way

Hardware is judged under item a and point 4.3.3: a tangible asset below 2,500 lei per item is not eligible, so a 1,800 lei monitor or printer is not reimbursed, however the company treats it for tax. Hardware must be new (point 4.5), be present and working at the implementation site, and be insured against all risks in favour of AIMMT (point 4.8).

A website, online store, software and licences sit under item f, where the 2,500 lei floor does not apply (point 4.3.3). The insurance duty covers tangible assets, so on our reading not software; confirm with AIMMT if you have a borderline case.

Order 700/2026 changed one more thing: scoring criterion E now refers only to purchases of at least 2,500 lei under item a. Laptops and PCs count towards that share; a website and software do not. If you scored on criterion E, you must keep the committed share at reimbursement, otherwise AIMMT reduces the amount or rejects the claim (point 6.6 para. 12; 6.12 para. 1).

What is not reimbursed on the digital side

Hosting: item f says domain „without hosting”, so hosting and later maintenance stay with the company. VAT is not reimbursed, the reimbursed value being the value excluding VAT (point 6.6 para. 15). Assembly, commissioning, labour and staff training for equipment are not eligible (point 4.4 para. 3). No aid is given for leased, second-hand or already grant-funded assets (point 4.6).

Invoices issued before the agreement enters into force are not reimbursed, except consultancy and the electronic signature (point 6.9). Every cost must tie to the activity of the CAEN code authorised for the funding (point 4.4 para. 1). A separate training or maintenance line on a software invoice is not named in item f: ask AIMMT before putting it on the invoice rather than assuming it is eligible.

Reimbursing a website and software: deadlines and evidence

The reimbursement module has been open since 10 November 2026, and payment is made in a single tranche once every purchase is complete and paid (point 6.12). All payments go out of the dedicated account at the partner bank (point 6.6 para. 4). For licences and software, Annex 7 requires the tax invoice and a contract showing the object of the cost, and the Annex 8 form asks for the cost name, number of items, serial or inventory number and invoice value with and without VAT.

Costs are certified by an on-site or online visit from the partner bank or AIMMT, with a report and mandatory photos (point 6.6 para. 16; 6.12 para. 2); you answer clarification requests within 5 days (point 6.6 para. 11). At reimbursement you may swap equipment for a better-specified one if it keeps the name and at least the operations of the original (point 6.6 para. 15); for software, get AIMMT's agreement first, since the rule is written for equipment. We hand over the live address, admin account, screenshots and user guide.

Supplier and consultant: two separate roles

A consultant paid under item h cannot also supply other costs in the same project: if found, both the consultancy and the other costs are refused. A company cannot be, in the same session, a beneficiary and a supplier to other beneficiaries (point 4.10 para. 1). Prices disproportionately above market lead to clarification requests or refused payment (point 4.9 para. 2).

We do not sign acceptance protocols before the deliverable exists and we do not date invoices before delivery. Northdan Soft is the supplier of the digital component, not a funding consultant: we work alongside your consultant so the quote, names and acceptance documents match Annex 6.

Frequently asked questions

Is a 2,000 lei laptop an eligible cost in Femeia Antreprenor 2024?

No. Since Order 700/2026, a tangible asset is eligible only if it individually costs at least 2,500 lei (point 4.3.3). The floor does not apply to a website, software and licences under item f, nor to the information plaques under item i.

Is website hosting reimbursed under Femeia Antreprenor?

No. Item f covers „domain registration without hosting”, so the domain, website and software are eligible but hosting is not. Hosting and maintenance after delivery stay with the company, on its own budget.

Does the 25,000 lei digital-component threshold apply in the 2024 edition?

No. The 2024 procedure has no mandatory digital component and no 25,000 lei floor; that rule belonged to the 2022 round. For 2024, what counts is the cost elements in Annex 6 and the categories in point 4.3.1.

Can the consultant who wrote the application also deliver the website?

No, if the consultant's fee is claimed under item h: a company providing consultancy in the programme cannot also supply other eligible costs in the project. If found, both the consultancy and the other costs from the same supplier are refused, so the website is ordered from a separate company.

What documents does reimbursement require for a website and software?

The tax invoice, a contract showing the object of the cost, proof of full payment from the dedicated account, the reimbursement form (Annex 8) and, at certification, a report with mandatory photos. For a website or software, prepare the live address and a demo of it working for the visit; the procedure requires a report and photos.

Can I still file a new application under Femeia Antreprenor?

No. There is no new submission session: the 2024 edition is in reimbursement, open since 10 November 2026 for beneficiaries with a financing agreement. The programme page covers the general picture and is updated if a new session is announced.
