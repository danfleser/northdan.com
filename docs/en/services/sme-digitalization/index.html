Digitalization for SMEs without the buzzwords

Not a “digital transformation programme” — a sequence of small, paid-back steps from paper and spreadsheets to connected systems.

For a small or mid-sized company, digitalization rarely means anything exotic. It means orders that stop living in a paper notebook, stock counted by software instead of memory, invoices that issue themselves from the same data the warehouse sees, and a manager who can answer “how did we do last month?” without a weekend in Excel. We guide SMEs through exactly that, step by step: an audit of how information currently flows, a prioritised plan where every step pays back before the next begins, and implementation that mixes affordable standard tools with targeted custom software where your process is genuinely specific. For Romanian companies we also map each step against active EU digitalization grants; international clients get the same pragmatic sequencing without the funding layer.

What you get

Steps sized to an SME budget

Each phase is scoped in weeks, has its own measurable payback, and works standalone — you can stop after any step and still be better off.

Standard tools where they fit

We are not attached to building everything: accounting, e-commerce and HR often run best on proven platforms, with custom software only in between.

People brought along

Training, transition periods and champions inside your team are planned parts of the project — because software nobody adopts digitalises nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Where does digitalization usually start in a company like ours?⌄

Wherever data is retyped between two places — that seam is where errors and hours concentrate. For most SMEs the first win is connecting sales orders to stock and invoicing.

Our team is not technical. Will they cope?⌄

Yes — tools are chosen partly for learnability, we train in your context with your data, and we keep a hotline period after each go-live. Adoption is a design criterion, not an afterthought.

Can EU funding cover part of this?⌄

For Romanian SMEs, frequently yes — digitalization grants recur under several programmes and we prepare the technical documentation for applications. Ask us what is currently open before you self-fund.
