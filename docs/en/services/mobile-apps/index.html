Services

Mobile apps your customers keep installed

Native or cross-platform, built by an EU team that also tells you when an app is the wrong answer.

A mobile app is the most expensive real estate on your customer’s phone, and the bar for staying installed keeps rising. We build iOS and Android applications for European companies — usually with Flutter or React Native from one shared codebase, natively in Swift or Kotlin when hardware access or performance demands it. The uncomfortable truth we share in the first call: many businesses do not need an app at all, and a well-built responsive web application serves them better without app-store friction. When an app is justified — offline field work, push-driven engagement, device sensors — we take it from product definition through store review to post-launch monitoring. You get an English-speaking team in the EU timezone and source code that belongs to you.

What you get

One codebase, both stores

Flutter and React Native let a single team ship to iPhone and Android together, cutting build and maintenance costs by roughly a third versus two native apps.

Store review handled

We deal with Apple and Google review requirements, signing, privacy declarations and staged rollouts — release mechanics you feel only when they go wrong.

Advice before code

If a PWA or responsive web app serves your case better, we say so before you spend a mobile budget — an app has to earn its slot on the home screen.

Frequently asked questions

Native or cross-platform — how do you choose?⌄

By feature list, not fashion. Heavy sensor use, Bluetooth peripherals or demanding graphics push us native; typical business apps — forms, lists, notifications, payments — are served excellently by one cross-platform codebase.

Do you publish under our developer account?⌄

Always. The Apple and Google accounts, certificates and store listings are created in your company’s name, so you keep full control even if we ever part ways.

What does maintenance look like after launch?⌄

OS updates, new device sizes and store policy changes arrive on Apple’s and Google’s schedule, not yours. We offer a monthly maintenance retainer that keeps the app compliant and crash-free.
