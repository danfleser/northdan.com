MVP development: launch, learn, then invest

The point of an MVP is a real answer from real users — before the serious money is committed.

Founders come to us with eighteen months of features in their head; the market usually only needs six weeks of them to give its verdict. MVP development is the discipline of finding that six weeks: the smallest product that puts your core value proposition in front of paying users. We have built MVPs for European founders and for established companies testing new lines of business, and the process is always the same — a scoping workshop that cuts scope until it hurts, a fixed-price build of six to ten weeks, and an architecture that is deliberately simple but not disposable, so the version that finds traction can grow instead of being thrown away. Working from Romania, we stretch pre-seed budgets considerably further than Western European agencies, without the communication overhead of going offshore.

What you get

Ruthless scoping workshop

We help you cut the feature list to the one flow that tests your riskiest assumption — the discipline most first-time founders need imposed from outside.

Fixed price, fixed date

A validated MVP scope gets a fixed quote and a launch date — because “as long as it takes” is how validation budgets die.

Simple, not disposable

We use proven frameworks and clean structure so the MVP that wins users becomes version one of the product — not a prototype you pay to rebuild.

Our four steps

A scoping workshop reduces the idea to the single loop that proves or disproves the business hypothesis, and we argue for cutting until it is uncomfortable. A fixed-price, fixed-date build of six to ten weeks follows, with weekly demos so the scope stays honest. Launch goes to real users, paying ones wherever possible, because free users tell you almost nothing about willingness to buy.

Then the measurement phase, which is the step most teams skip: instrumentation agreed in advance, a decision date in the calendar, and success criteria written down before the numbers arrive so they cannot be reinterpreted afterwards. What we hand over is simple but not disposable — if the hypothesis holds, the codebase grows into the product instead of being thrown away.

Frequently asked questions

What does an MVP cost with you?⌄

Most land between €6,000 and €18,000 for a mobile MVP, and between €8,000 and €30,000 for a web one, depending on integrations and complexity — deliberately quoted fixed after the scoping workshop, so runway math stays predictable.

We are not developers. Can you handle everything technical?⌄

Yes — infrastructure, domains, app accounts, analytics, deployment. You focus on customers and content; we run the entire technical side and explain decisions in plain language.

What happens after launch?⌄

You own the code either way. If traction comes, most clients keep us for iteration sprints; if you build an internal team or raise and hire, we hand over with documentation and onboarding calls.
