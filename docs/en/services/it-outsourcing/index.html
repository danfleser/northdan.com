Services

Outsource development, keep control

The economics of outsourcing with one-hour timezone difference and EU law on the contract.

Outsourcing software work fails in predictable ways: a ten-hour timezone gap that turns every question into a two-day round trip, contracts under distant jurisdictions, and code that arrives without the knowledge to maintain it. Nearshoring to Romania removes those failure modes while keeping the economics. Our engineers work when you work — at most one hour from any EU capital — communicate directly in English, and operate under EU contracts, EU invoicing and GDPR. Companies use us in two modes: full delivery of defined projects, where we carry scope and deadlines, or ongoing capacity that extends an in-house team. In both, transparency is structural — you see the repositories, the board and the people, from the first sprint to handover, and documentation is produced as we go rather than reverse-engineered at the end.

Frequently asked questions

Why Romania rather than Asia for outsourcing?⌄

Total cost, not hourly rate. Shared working hours, cultural proximity and EU jurisdiction eliminate the rework, delay and legal friction that consume offshore savings — for most EU companies the math favours nearshore.

How do we keep control of quality remotely?⌄

You see everything continuously: live repository access, demo every sprint, agreed quality gates in CI, and direct contact with engineers rather than a shielded account layer.

Can you start with something small before we commit?⌄

We prefer it — a two-to-four-week paid pilot with defined output lets both sides test the collaboration on real work before any framework agreement.
