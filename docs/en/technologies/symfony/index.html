Symfony: the PHP foundation for long-lived systems

The framework the whole PHP ecosystem feeds on — including Laravel.

Symfony is a foundation other PHP projects build on: its components underpin Laravel, Drupal and many commercial platforms. As a standalone framework it is the rigor-first choice: long-term-support versions announced in advance, carefully managed compatibility between releases and an architecture that survives years of successive changes. We recommend it to companies building systems with a ten-year horizon — B2B platforms, complex internal systems — and to projects that require predictability around support. The trade-off, stated plainly: initial development is slower and more expensive than Laravel, because Symfony demands more explicit structure. For fast, mid-sized projects, Laravel remains our standard recommendation.

Frequently asked questions

Symfony or Laravel — what is the practical difference?⌄

Laravel optimizes delivery speed; Symfony optimizes rigor and lifespan. For a complex internal system with a 10-year horizon we choose Symfony; for a portal shipping in three months, Laravel.

Do you take over existing Symfony applications?⌄

Yes — including old versions: we audit, upgrade step by step to the current LTS and stabilize the test suite before adding new features. No forced rewrites.

Does Symfony make sense for a small project?⌄

Rarely — its explicit structure becomes bureaucracy on simple projects. Below a certain complexity threshold we honestly recommend Laravel or even a no-code option; Symfony shines on large, long-lived systems.
